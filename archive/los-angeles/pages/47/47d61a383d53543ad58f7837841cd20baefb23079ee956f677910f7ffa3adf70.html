Case Number: 25NWLC09915    Hearing Date: July 1, 2026    Dept: Z

 25NWLC09915: TD BANK USA, N.A. vs IMELDA GOMEZ
The Court has read and reviewed the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel, the Declaration in Support of the Motion, and the Proof of Service, filed June 5, 2026.  Cal. Rules of Court 3.1362(d)(1) requires that the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel be accompanied with a declaration stating facts showing that the client has been noticed with the motion either at their current address or last known address.  The Declaration contained on MC 052 form is insufficient because the "Service" section, page 1, paragraph 3, is not filled out.   The Court DENIES the application.

Case Number: 25NWLC50212    Hearing Date: July 1, 2026    Dept: Z

25NWLC50212: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs CATHY T LE, AN INDIVIDUAL
The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff in support of the Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including a meet and confer letter dated April 30, 2026.
The Court notes that Plaintiff has filed proof of service of this motion on Defendant at the same address Defendant listed on their Answer.
Pursuant to Cal. Evid. Code § 452 and § 453, the Court GRANTS the request for the Court to take judicial notice of:
  1. The Complaint filed by Plaintiff on September 3, 2025;
  2. The Order granting Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted as to RFA # 1-8, entered on April 29, 2026; and
  3. Request for admissions filed February 13, 2026, in support of Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted.
The Court also takes judicial notice of:
  1. Defendant’s Answer filed on October 14, 2025.
The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot be reasonably controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions admitted are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff. 
The Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.
The Court orders Judgement in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $17,205.79, and $430.00 for costs, for a total judgment of $17,635.79.
