Case Number: 24NWLC20678    Hearing Date: September 14, 2026    Dept: Y

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on December 29, 2024 and filed with the Court on January 24, 2025.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order on Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Judgment entered by the Court on January 28, 2025.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $3,570.00, leaving an outstanding amount of $1,900.87 due and owing.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and against Defendant Ernesto Ramos, Jr. for the remaining sum of $1,900.87 plus court costs of zero, for a total judgment of $1,900.87.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC51823    Hearing Date: September 14, 2026    Dept: Y

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed Defendant Bethany L. Ware Purkett’s (“Defendant”) Motion for Leave to File Cross-Complaint (“Motion”).  Defendant filed her Motion on August 19, 2026.  According to the Proof of Service that she filed with the Court, Defendant served the Motion on Plaintiff Navy Federal Credit Union (“Plaintiff”) by mail on August 24, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Plaintiff.   

Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 requires that “all moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. . . . if the notice is served by mail, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by five calendar days if the place of mailing and place of address are within the State of California.”   

For proper notice to have been given by Defendant, the Motion should have been personally served no later than August 20, 2026 or served by mail no later than August 14, 2026 for a hearing date on September 14, 2026.  The Court continues the hearing on the Motion to October 6, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y so that Plaintiff has sufficient time to file an opposition if necessary.

Clerk to give notice.
