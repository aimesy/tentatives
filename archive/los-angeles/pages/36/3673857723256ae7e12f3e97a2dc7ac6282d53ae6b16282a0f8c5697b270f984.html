DEPARTMENT T LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26NNCV01679    Hearing Date: August 12, 2026    Dept: T

Kelyan v. Golden Rose Care Center

Petition for Approval of Compromise of Claim

The Court cannot approve the Petition in its current form given the lack of information concerning the lien.  The Court also cannot approve the Petition without having the contemplated Special Needs Trust being drafted and submitted for review by the Probate Department pursuant to Local Rule 4.115.  The Court also suspects that a conservatorship proceeding may be required given that it appears Sonik Kelyan is not competent to manage her own affairs or to serve at the trustee of the trust.
