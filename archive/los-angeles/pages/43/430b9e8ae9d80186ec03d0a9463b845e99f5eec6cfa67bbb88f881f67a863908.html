DEPARTMENT 729 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

 All parties are urged to meet and confer with all parties concerning this tentative ruling to see if they can reach an agreed-upon resolution of their matter.  If you are able to reach an agreement, please notify the courtroom staff in advance of the hearing if you wish to submit on the tentative ruling rather than argue the motion by notifying the court by e-mailing the court at: [email protected]. Do not click on the email address, either copy and paste it or type it into your email.  Include the word "SUBMITS" in all caps and the Case Number in the Subject line.  In the body of the email, please provide the date and time of the hearing, your name, your contact information, the party you represent, and whether that party is a plaintiff, defendant, cross-complainant, cross-defendant, claimant, intervenor, or non-party, etc.


            Please be advised that if you submit on the tentative and elect not to appear at the hearing, the opposing party may still appear at the hearing and argue the matter, and the court could change its tentative based upon the argument.  Unless you receive a submission from all other parties in the matter, you should assume that others might appear at the hearing to argue.  If you submit, but still intend to appear, include the words "SUBMITS, BUT WILL APPEAR" in the Subject line.    


            If you elect to argue your matter, you are urged to do so remotely, via Court-Connect. If the moving party fails to appear and/or submit to the Court’s tentative ruling, the Court will take the  matter off calendar.
                          
            Note that once the Court has issued a tentative, the Court has the inherent authority not to allow the withdrawal of a motion and to adopt the tentative ruling as the order of the court.   
 

            If you submitted a courtesy copy of your papers containing media (such as a DVD or thumb drive), unless you request the return of the media in your papers, the court will destroy it following the hearing of your matter.

DEPARTMENT 729

 

TENTATIVE RULING

 

U-HAUL CO. OF CALIFORNIA, 

 

         vs.

 

CAISER OWENS, et al.

 Case No.:  24STCV18485

 

 

 

 Hearing Date:  August 10, 2026

 

Plaintiff U-Haul Co. of California’s Request for Default Judgment

 

          Having considered all documents submitted in support of a default judgment, the Court rules as follows:

 

Background

On July 25, 2024, Plaintiff U-Haul Co. of California (“Plaintiff”) filed its operative Complaint against Defendants Caiser Owens, Laurence Reed, and Jacob Stewart [erroneously named James Stewart] (collectively, “Defendants”) alleging nine causes of action: (1) declaratory relief- recission [against Owens]; (2) declaratory relief- no duty to indemnify and defend [against Owens]; (3) declaratory relief- breach of contract [against Owens]; (4) declaratory relief- breach of contract [against Owens]; (5) declaratory relief- breach of contract [against Owens]; (6) declaratory relief- breach of contract [against Owens]; (7) unclean hands [against Owens]; (8) breach of covenant of good faith and fair dealing [against Owens]; and (9) fraud [against Owens].  On October 31, 2024, Plaintiff filed an amendment to the complaint correcting the incorrectly named Defendant James Stewart to the correct name of Jacob Stewart.

On June 20, 2025, the clerk entered default against Defendant Owens. On November 19, 2025, the clerk entered default against Defendant Reed.  On March 10, 2026. The clerk entered default against Stewart.

On July 27, 2026, Plaintiff filed its Request for Court Judgment to be heard on August 10, 2026.

 

Party’s Request

Plaintiff requests the Court enter a default judgment against Defendants Caiser Owens, Laurence Reed, and Jacob Stewart, and award Plaintiff a declaratory judgment stating that the Rental Contract (# 79796352 ) entered between the Plaintiff and Defendant Owens on April 29, 2024, and any liability coverage provided, and any liability coverage purchased are canceled, null, and void ab initio and rescinded; that Owens is not entitled to any benefits or coverage affording liability arising or relating to the collision on May 1, 2024, which is the subject of this action; that Owens materially breached the contract with the Plaintiff and due to the material breach by Owens the Plaintiff’s contractual obligations are void; and that Plaintiff has no duty to indemnify or defend Owens against any claims for injuries or damages by Reed and Stewart related to the alleged incident on or about May 1, 2024. 

 

Discussion

Plaintiff submitted a completed default judgment packet, with all applicable attachments. The Court finds sufficient evidence to justify the requested award and grants Plaintiff’s request in full.

 

Conclusion

The application for default judgment filed on July 27, 2026, is granted.  Declaratory Judgment on the Complaint in favor of Plaintiff is entered against Defendants Caiser Owens, Laurence Reed, and Jacob Stewart.

Plaintiff is ordered to give notice of this ruling.
