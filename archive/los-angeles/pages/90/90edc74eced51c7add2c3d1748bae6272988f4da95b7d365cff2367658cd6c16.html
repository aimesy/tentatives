Case Number: 25NWLC49889    Hearing Date: August 6, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC49889: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs MANUEL NAVA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A.’s (“Plaintiff”) Motion for Summary Judgment (“Motion”), including the separate statement and supporting evidence.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Manuel Nava (“Defendant”).

Plaintiff filed its Motion for Summary Judgment on May 19, 2026, setting it for hearing on August 6, 2026.

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 437c, a motion for summary judgment requires a minimum of 81 calendar days’ notice before the hearing date, with additional time depending on the method of service, including 5 calendar days for service by mail.  The interval from May 19, 2026 to August 6, 2026 is 79 calendar days, which does not satisfy the statutory notice requirement. 

Based on the May 19, 2026 filing date, the earliest compliant hearing date for the motion would have been August 13, 2026. However, Code of Civil Procedure section 437c(a)(3) mandates that a motion for summary judgment be heard at least 30 days before trial. With a trial date of September 8, 2026, the last permissible hearing date for such a motion would be August 7, 2026.

Accordingly, Plaintiff’s Motion for Summary Judgment is denied without prejudice.

The trial date of September 8, 2026 remains on calendar.

Case Number: 25NWLC59960    Hearing Date: August 6, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC59960: BANK OF AMERICA N.A. vs GLEN BARRINGTON SLUSHER

Tentative Ruling - Submitted w/o appearance

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated December 4, 2025.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Glen Barrington Slusher (“Defendant”).

The Court finds that Defendant, in his Answer filed on November 20, 2025, did not deny the statements of the complaint or indicate any affirmative defenses.  He requested a payment plan due to financial hardship.  Although the Court may consider financial hardship of the Defendant, if and when Plaintiff seeks to collect on the judgment, the Court cannot consider “financial hardship” as an affirmative defense to the debt owed.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the Complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action against Defendant and the Answer does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense. 

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. and against Defendant Glen Barrington Slusher for the principal sum of $3,874.93 plus costs in the amount of $414.75, for a total Judgment of $4,289.68.

Plaintiff to give notice.
