ANALYSIS:

 

            CMM, LLP won an uncontested trial against Eric E. Jensen.  It now moves for attorney fees pursuant to Civil Code section 1717. 

 

The Court’s objective is to award those fees at the fair-market rate for the attorney’s work. (Ketchum v. Moses (2001) 24 Cal.4th 1122, 1132.)  That can be done by multiplying the number of hours the attorney reasonably expended by a reasonable hourly rate.  (Id. at pp. 1132, 1134.)  CMM seeks attorney fees based on its counsel’s hourly rate of $400.00, which the Court finds reasonable for an attorney of his experience.  Likewise, the expenditure of 13.7 hours of work to see an action through trial—even an uncontested trial—is reasonable.  Multiplying 13.7 hours by $400 per hour yields a fee award of $5,480.

 

CMM also requests attorney fees for this motion.  Because the fee motion was unopposed the Court reduces the hours requested from 4 to 2, and adds an additional $800 to the fee award.

 

The Court therefore GRANTS CMM’s motion for attorney fees in the reduced amount of $6,280.00.

 

CMM is to give notice.
