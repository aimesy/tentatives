DEPARTMENT SED LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 22STCV26823    Hearing Date: July 29, 2026    Dept: SED

ERIKA CAMACHO V. CHAVEZ TRUCKING, ET AL.

CASE NO.:  22STCV26823

HEARING:  07/29/2026 @ 8:30 a.m.

 

#2

TENTATIVE ORDER

 

Petitioner Octavio Godines’s petition to confirm minor’s compromise on behalf of Claimant Abraham Godines is CONTINUED to September 29, 2026,  at 9:30 a.m.

 

Moving party to give notice.

 

Petitioner Octavio Godines (Petitioner) brings this petition as the Guardian ad litem and parent of the minor Claimant Abraham Godines.

 

Background

 

On January 8, 2026, Petitioner filed a Petition to Confirm Minor's Compromise.

 

On February 24, 2026, the Court continued the hearing to May 8, 2026, to allow Petitioner to submit a new, compliant Special Needs Trust (SNT) instrument and otherwise correct the deficiencies identified in the Court's minute order.  The Court also ordered Petitioner to provide the required notice to all relevant agencies and parties and to file proofs of service.

 

On May 7, 2026, after Petitioner failed to file an amended Petition or the required proofs of service, the Court vacated the May 8, 2026 hearing and set an Order to Show Cause (OSC) re Failure to File Revised Petition to Confirm Minor's Compromise, as well as the OSC re Dismissal (Settlement), for June 24, 2026.

 

On June 24, 2026, the Court found that Petitioner had not filed the amended Petition.  At Petitioner's request, the Court continued both OSCs to July 29, 2026.

 

On June 26, 2026, Petitioner filed the instant Petition.

 

Analysis

 

Since the Court's February 24, 2026 order, Petitioner has filed an amended petition that includes a revised proposed SNT instrument.  However, the record does not show that Petitioner has complied with the Court's separate directive requiring notice to the relevant state agencies and the filing of proofs of service.  Accordingly, a further continuance is appropriate to allow Petitioner to complete the remaining requirements of the Court's prior order.

 

Conclusion

 

The Court CONTINUES this hearing to September 29, 2026, at 9:30 a.m. to allow Petitioner an opportunity to comply with the Court's February 24, 2026 order.  Petitioner must give the required notice (15 days' notice by mail) to all relevant agencies and parties, file proofs of service, and otherwise comply with the Court's prior order.
