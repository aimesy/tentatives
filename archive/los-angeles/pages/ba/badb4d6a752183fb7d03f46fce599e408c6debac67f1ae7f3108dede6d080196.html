Background and Discussion


On December 17, 2021, Judith Rivas filed a Request that Clerk Enter Judgment and Judgment on Final Order, Decision or Award of the Labor Commissioner for $16,469.69 against Behzad Manzoor.

 

The clerk entered judgment on December 20, 2021.

 

On June 12, 2026, Manzoor filed a document entitled “Motion to Lift a Lien.”

 

On July 2, 2026, Judge David J. Cowan issued a minute order taking the motion off-calendar for “failure to provide notice to all parties consistent with Code of Civil Procedure section 1005, subdivision (b).”

 

On July 6, 2026, Manzoor filed a “Motion for Reconsideration” with the hearing set in Department 534.

 

Manzoor similarly failed to provide notice to all parties consistent with Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 in connection with the instant motion. “Unless otherwise ordered or specifically provided by law, all moving and supporting papers must be served and filed in accordance with Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 and, when applicable, the statutes and rules providing for electronic filing and service.” (Cal. R. Ct., rule 3.1300(a).) “Proof of service of the moving papers must be filed no later than five court days before the time appointed for the hearing.” (Cal. R. Ct., rule 3.1300(c).) The motion is not accompanied by a proof of service and the time to file a proof of service has expired.

 

Manzoor’s Motion for Reconsideration filed July 6, 2026 is ordered OFF-CALENDAR.

 

Clerk to give notice.


Case Number: 25STCV25900    Hearing Date: July 30, 2026    Dept: 534

Background

 

On September 4, 2025, Jose Manuel Dulanto filed this action against Archbishop William E. Lori; David B. Simpson; Knights Of Columbus; Steve Owens; Jennifer M. Fontaine; Aaron A. Buckley; Michael P. Linfield; Terry Green; Natalie P. Stone; Gonzalo C. Martinez; Elwood Lui; David W. Slayton; Eva Mcclintock; Wolfflick, Simpson, Kachaturian & Bouayad; Simpson Employment Law APC; Paul-Plevin-Sullivan-&-Connaughton LLP; and Quarles & Brady LLP asserting nine causes of action.

 

On March 6, 2026, Judge Cherol J. Nellon issued an order relating BC656164 and 25STCV25900 and transferring this action to Department 14 [renumbered as of May 4, 2026 to Department 300.]

 

On March 30, 2026 and April 1, 2026, Plaintiff filed motions for reconsideration of the March 6, 2026 order in BC656164. Judge Nellon denied the motions on June 11, 2026.

 

On June 11, 2026, Judge Nellon sustained Defendants the Honorable Elwood Lui, Administrative Presiding Justice of the California Court of Appeal, Second Appellate District; the Honorable Gonzalo C. Martinez, Presiding Justice of the California Court of Appeal, Second Appellate District, Division Seven; the Honorable Natalie P. Stone, Associate Justice of the California Court of Appeal, Second District, Division Seven; the Honorable Terry Green, the Honorable Michael P. Linfield, Judges of the Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles (Ret.); Eva McClintock, Clerk/Executive Officer, California Court of Appeal, Second Appellate District; and David W. Slayton, Court Executive Officer and Clerk, Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles demurrer without leave to amend.

 

On June 18, 2026, Judge Nellon sustained Defendants Quarles & Brady LLP; Paul, Plevin, Sullivan & Connaughton LLP And Aaron A. Buckley’s demurrer without leave to amend.

 

On June 25, 2026, Judge Nellon sustained Defendants Steve Owens, David B. Simpson, and Simpson Employment Law, APC’s demurrer to the complaint without leave to amend.

 

Motion

 

On June 29, 2026, Plaintiff filed the instant motion, with the hearing set in Department 534, for an order vacating the relation order issued on March 6, 2026.

 

Opposition

 

In opposition, Defendants Steve Owens, David B. Simpson, and Simpson Employment Law, APC contend the motion is not properly before this department, there was no underlying fraud, and Plaintiff makes no argument that the ruling was substantively incorrect.

 

Reply

 

The Court did not receive a timely reply. (Code Civ. Proc. § 1005.)

 

Discussion

 

Absent express authority by rule or statute, or the unavailability of the original judge, a trial judge may not vacate a ruling made by another trial judge. (People v. Woodard (1982) 131 Cal.App.3d 107, 111 (“The Superior Court of Los Angeles County, though comprised of a number of judges, is a single court and one member of that court cannot sit in review on the actions of another member of that same court.”); In re Alberto (2002) 102 Cal.App.4th 421, 427 (“For one superior court judge, no matter how well intended, . . . to nullify a duly made, . . . ruling of another superior court judge places the second judge in the role of a one-judge appellate court.”).)

 

Department 534 is solely vested with authority to review related case rulings “[i]n the event that the judge designated under California Rules of Court, rule 3.300(h)(1)(A)(B)(C) to make the decision, does not order related any of the cases set forth in the Notice of Related Cases.” (SCLAC Local Rules, rule 3.3(f)(2); Cal. R. Ct., rule 3.300(h)(D).) These rules do not apply here.

 

Judge Nellon remains available and Department 534 lacks authority to pass judgment on the March 6, 2026 order relating the cases at issue.

 

The motion is DENIED.

 

Conclusion

 

Plaintiff’s Motion to Vacate Order Issued by Dept. 14 Relating Cases, Self-Assignment and Prejudicial Effects as Procured by Defendant Knights of Columbus through Extrinsic Fraud and Active Concealment is DENIED.

 

Counsel for Defendants Steve Owens, David B. Simpson, and Simpson Employment Law, APC is ordered to give notice.


Case Number: 26PDUD01667    Hearing Date: July 30, 2026    Dept: 534

Background

 

On June 3, 2026, Plaintiff Esdras Rodas filed this limited unlawful detainer action against Olivetty Maldonado, erroneously sued as Betty Maldonado, and Bryan Maldonado related to real property located at 11117 Archwood Place, North Hollywood, CA 91606.

 

The case is currently pending in Department R of the Pasadena Courthouse.

 

Motion

 

On July 7, 2026, Defendant Olivetty Maldonado filed the instant motion seeking to transfer this action from the Pasadena Courthouse in the Northeast District to the Van Nuys East Courthouse in the Northwest District.

 

The motion is unopposed. (Code Civ. Proc. § 1005.)

 

Motion to Transfer Between Districts

 

Standard

 

Superior Court of Los Angeles County Local Rules, rule 2.3(b)(2) authorizes Department 1 to transfer civil cases from one judicial district to another via a noticed motion on three enumerated grounds: (1) when the case was filed in an improper district; (2) for the convenience of witnesses; or (3) to promote the ends of justice. (SCLAC Local Rule 2.3(b)(2).) A transfer under the Local Rules is discretionary.

 

The Court Shall Transfer the Action

 

Defendant contends the case was improperly filed pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 392(b), which provides: “the proper court location for trial of a proceeding for an unlawful detainer, as defined in Section 1161, is the location where the court tries that type of proceeding that is nearest or most accessible to where the real property that is the subject of the action, or some part thereof, is situated. Otherwise any location of the superior court designated as the proper court in subdivision (a) is a proper court location for the trial. The court may specify by local rule the nearest or most accessible court location where the court tries that type of case.”

 

“If it appears from the complaint or affidavit, or otherwise, that the superior court or court location where the action or proceeding is commenced is not the proper court or court location for the trial, the court where the action or proceeding is commenced, or a judge thereof, shall, whenever that fact appears, transfer it to the proper court or court location, on its own motion, or on motion of the defendant, unless the defendant consents in writing, or in open court (consent in open court being entered in the minutes of the court), to the keeping of the action or proceeding in the court or court location where commenced.” (Code Civ. Proc., § 396a(b).)

 

Superior Court of Los Angeles County Local Rules, rule 2.3(a)(2) provides “[e]very limited unlawful detainer case must be filed in one of courthouses listed in Appendix 2.D.” Inputting 91606 as the zip code and North Hollywood for the property location, yields a result for the Pasadena Courthouse.

 

Defendant notes the Van Nuys Courthouse East is nearer to the property than the Pasadena Courthouse. Defendant also provides a declaration stating the Van Nuys Courthouse East is nearer and more accessible to their home and they are the primary caregiver for their elderly mother and two minor grandchildren with limited financial resources. (Maldonado Decl. ¶¶ 3-7.) Defendant also notes they suffer from medical conditions that make it painful to sit for long periods and the pain would be lessened by the shorter commute to Van Nuys. (Id. ¶ 8.)

 

The motion is GRANTED.

 

Conclusion

 

Defendant Maldonado’s Motion to Transfer Unlawful Detainer to Northwest District is GRANTED.

 

The action is ordered reassigned to the Van Nuys Courthouse East, Department H. All hearings currently scheduled in 26PDUD01667 are hereby advanced and vacated.

The case is set for a trial setting conference in Department H of the Van Nuys Courthouse East for August 10, 2026 at 8:P30 a.m.

 

Notice of case reassignment shall issue shortly. 

 

Counsel for moving party to give notice.
