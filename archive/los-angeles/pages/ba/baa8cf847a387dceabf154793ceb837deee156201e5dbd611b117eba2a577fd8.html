21STCV20276 Marisela Vazquez, et al. v. Haven Warner Center, et al.

Tuesday September 22, 2026

 

[TENTATIVE] ORDER GRANTING FOUR (4) PETITIONS FOR APPROVAL OF COMPROMISE OF CLAIM OR ACTION OR DISPOSITION OF PROCEEDS OF JUDGMENT FOR MINOR OR PERSON WITH A DISABILITY

·       Manuel Vasquez, age 5 (2214)

·       Luna Vasquez, age 7 (1761

·       Anthony Ixta, age 16 (0314)

·       Anabelle Ixta, age 15 (0268)

      The first amended complaint in the lead matter alleges that residential premises leased by Plaintiffs from Defendants were uninhabitable because of conditions that Defendants allegedly failed to remedy. Plaintiffs allege nine causes of action for breach of warranty of habitability and related claims.

      Plaintiffs request an order approving the settlements of four minor claimants.

      A minor’s settlement is valid if approved by the court after a hearing.  (Prob. Code, §§3505, 3500.) Each of the four claimants has settled their claims for $20,000. From each minor’s settlement, counsel proposes to distribute $8,000 for attorney’s fees and $1,100 for medical expenses incurred. The three claimants who incurred medical expenses have a net settlement of $10,900. Luna Vasquez did not incur medical expenses, and therefore, the net settlement is $12,000. All net settlements will be deposited into a blocked account for each claimant.

      Each minor has recovered from their injuries. The petitions filed July 22, 2026, include a doctor’s report for Anabelle Ixta, Anthony Ixta, and Manuel Vasquez, showing they were treated for bed bugs and released.

      Petitioner and claimants must appear at the hearing, unless the Court finds good cause to excuse their appearance. (Cal. Rules of Court, 7.952.) Subject to a hearing, the Court is inclined to grant each of the four petitions.
