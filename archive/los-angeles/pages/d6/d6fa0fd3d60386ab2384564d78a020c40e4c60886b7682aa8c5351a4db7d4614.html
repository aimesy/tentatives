[TENTATIVE] ORDER RE: 

 

MOTION TO BE RELIEVED AS COUNSEL

 

Lusine Ghazaryan, Esq. of Downtown L.A. Law Group (“Counsel”) moves to be relieved as counsel of record for Plaintiff D’lilah Peace (“Plaintiff”), a minor proceeding by and through her Guardian Ad Litem, Donnell Peace.

The Court finds that Counsel complied with all the relevant procedural requirements. (Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.1362.) Counsel filed MC-051, MC-052, and MC-053 forms, provided sufficient reason for withdrawal, and filed proof of mail and email service. The Court notes that Counsel could not confirm that the address is current but engaged in a myriad of efforts to contact Plaintiff. Thus, the Court finds the evidence and declaration sufficient.

Accordingly, the motion to be relieved as counsel is GRANTED.

Counsel is ordered to give notice of this Order.

[TENTATIVE] ORDER RE:

 

DEFENDANT APERTO PROPERTY MANAGEMENT, INC.’S DEMURRER TO PLAINTIFF’S COMPLAINT;  

 

DEFENDANT APERTO PROPERTY MANAGEMENT, INC.’S MOTION TO STRIKE PORTIONS OF PLAINTIFF’S COMPLAINT

 

Plaintiff Barry Mirmotahari (“Plaintiff”) filed this action on October 8, 2025 against Defendants Cleartheton, LLC and Aperto Property Management, Inc. (“Aperto”) (jointly, “Defendants”), alleging twelve causes of action for (1) general negligence; (2) tortious breach of warranty of habitability; (3) breach of implied warranty of habitability; (4) breach of covenant of quiet enjoyment; (5) premises liability; (6) nuisance; (7) unfair business practice (Business & Professions Code §17200; (8) intentional infliction of emotional distress; (9) negligent infliction of emotional distress; (10) violation of Burbank municipal code; (11) illegal lockout; and (12) elder abuse – Welfare and Institutions code §15610.

On December 10, 2025, Aperto demurred to the eighth and twelfth causes of action of the complaint and moved to strike portions of the complaint relating to attorney fees, punitive damages, and fraud. Plaintiff opposes both. Aperto replied.

On May 1, 2026, the Court ordered Aperto to meet¿and confer¿with Plaintiff within 10 days of the Order, and stated that “[i]f the parties are unable to resolve the pleading issues¿or if the parties are otherwise unable to meet and confer in good faith, Aperto is to thereafter file and serve¿a declaration setting forth the efforts to meet and confer in compliance with Code of Civil Procedure section 430.41, subdivision (a)(3) and Code of Civil Procedure section 435.5, subdivision (a)(3) within 15 days of this Order.” (Order 5/1/26, 3:6-10.) The Court notes that Aperto did not file a declaration. Additionally, Plaintiff filed an amended complaint on May 15, 2026, and Defendants filed their answer on June 16, 2026.

Accordingly, the demurrer and motion to strike are DENIED as MOOT.

Aperto is ordered to give notice of this Order. 

 

DATED:  July 13, 2026
