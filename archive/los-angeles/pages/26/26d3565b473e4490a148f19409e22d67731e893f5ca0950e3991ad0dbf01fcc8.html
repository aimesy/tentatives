Case Number: 24NWLC40958    Hearing Date: June 29, 2026    Dept: Y

24NWLC40958: DISCOVER BANK vs RAUL N LOPEZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Discover Bank (“Plaintiff”), the supporting declaration, and all relevant court records.  

In its motion, Plaintiff requests to set aside and vacate the Default entered by the Clerk on March 25, 2025 and the Default Judgment entered by the Clerk on March 28, 2025.  Plaintiff also requests to dismiss the action without prejudice.   Plaintiff has presented evidence via declaration that Plaintiff received notification that there are grounds to set aside the judgment against Defendant Raul Lopez (“Defendant”). 

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473(b), the Court may set aside any judgment that was obtained by mistake or surprise.  Code of Civil Procedure section 187 also grants jurisdiction to the Court to take appropriate action in the interests of justice.

Based on the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Set Aside and Vacate Default and Default Judgment.   Accordingly, the Judgment entered against Defendant on March 28, 2025 is vacated.

The Court further orders that the Complaint filed by Plaintiff and against Defendant is DISMISSED without prejudice.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC15389    Hearing Date: June 29, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC15389: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs BILLY J HAMLIN, JR

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated May 7, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Billy J. Hamlin Jr. (“Defendant”).

Pursuant to California Evidence Code sections 452 and 453, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s request for the Court to take judicial notice of:

  1. Plaintiff’s Complaint filed on May 7, 2025;
  2. The Court’s Order Deeming Plaintiff’s Requests For Admission Nos. 1-10 as Admitted entered on April 30, 2026; and
  3. Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted, and the Requests for Admission submitted in support of that motion, filed on May 26, 2026.

The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot reasonably be controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff and against Defendant Billy J. Hamlin Jr. for the principal sum of $7,735.68, plus costs in the amount of $425.00 and reasonable attorney’s fees in the amount of $800.00, for a total Judgment of $8,960.68.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

The Court notes that Plaintiff appears to have a duplicative motion set for hearing on July 16, 2026.  Based on the Court’s tentative ruling, such motion is moot.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC71998    Hearing Date: June 29, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC71998: VELOCITY INVESTMENTS LLC vs MATTHEW KIM

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Defendant Matthew Jinhyun Kim (“Defendant”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court has also reviewed all relevant court records.  No opposition has been filed by Plaintiff Velocity Investments LLC (“Plaintiff”). 

The Court notes that default was entered by the Clerk on March 24, 2026.  Although Plaintiff has requested default judgment, no default judgment has been entered by the Court.  

Defendant states in his declaration, which was submitted under penalty of perjury, that he became aware of this lawsuit in April 2026 when he was reviewing his credit report.  Defendant’s Motion was promptly filed on May 22, 2026. 

The Court notes that Proof of Service of Summons by a registered process server by personal service was filed with the Court on December 26, 2025 creating a presumption of service under Evidence Code section 647.  Defendant’s declaration, which was signed under penalty of perjury, states that he was not personally served with the Summons and Complaint.  Defendant also states that the physical description of the person served does not match his physical description (e.g. he is of East Asian descent (Korean) and not Middle Eastern as noted in the Proof of Service).  He believes that the description matches a neighbor, but not anyone that resides at his home.   

Here, the Court finds that Defendant has rebutted the presumption of service and finds that there was no actual notice.  “Actual notice,” within the meaning of Code of Civil Procedure section 473.5, means genuine knowledge by the defendant, and has been strictly construed.  Relief under the statute is liberally granted to defendants so that cases may be resolved on their merits.  Ellard v. Conway (2001) 94 Cal.App.4th 540, 547-548.     

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 437.5, the Court GRANTS Defendant’s Motion and vacates and sets aside the Default. 

The Court grants Defendant 20 days leave to file a responsive pleading.

Clerk to give notice.
