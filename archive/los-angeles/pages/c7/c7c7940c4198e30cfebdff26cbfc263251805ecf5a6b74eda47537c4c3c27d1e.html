DEPARTMENT 517 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 23STCV07721    Hearing Date: July 22, 2026    Dept: 517

The Court is granting Plaintiffs' motion for leave to file a Fourth Amended Complaint ("4th AC").  By July 28, 2026, Plaintiffs shall file as a separate, standalone document the version of the proposed 4thAC that was attached as Exhibit A to the Exhibit List that Plaintiffs filed on June 10, 2026.   

There is a case management conference scheduled for September 2, 2026.  The Court is inclined to continue that conference because Plaintiffs need to serve the 4thAC on more than hundred Defendants named in the 4thAC and file the proofs of service.  That will take time and may not be completed by September 2, 2026.  Thereafter, Defendants have 30 days from the service date to respond to the 4thAC. It is unlikely that responses will have been filed by September 2, 2026.  The Court will discuss a new date for the case management conference at the hearing on motion for leave to amend.

Case Number: 24STCV34036    Hearing Date: July 22, 2026    Dept: 517

The Court is sustaining with leave to amend Defendant's demurrer to the claim for fraudulent inducement-concealment in Plaintiffs' First Amended Complaint that was belatedly filed on March 9, 2026.   The basis for the Court's ruling is that the fraud claim is barred under the three-year statute of limitations for fraud claims.  Plaintiffs have failed to plead within the delayed discovery rule that can fend off accrual of fraud claims.  The Court is declining to reach the other grounds on which Defendant demurred to the fraud claim.  Defendant's motion to strike is denied as moot in light of the ruling on the demurrer.  The Court observes that the Plaintiffs refiled the First Amended Complaint on July 15, 2026.  The Court is not sure why the Plaintiffs did that.  The refiling touches upon the order to show cause regarding sanctions against Plaintiffs' counsel that is on calendar.  The Court will address the order to show cause in light of the declaration of Plaintiffs' counsel regarding the failure of the Plaintiffs to file the First Amended Complaint on time.
