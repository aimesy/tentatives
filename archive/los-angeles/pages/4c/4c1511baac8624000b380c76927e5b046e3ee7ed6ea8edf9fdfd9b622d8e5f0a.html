DEPARTMENT 515 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV30761    Hearing Date: September 2, 2026    Dept: 515

Plaintiff seeks $50,885.85 against Allcarr, Inc., and $96,459.12 against Davit Ghazaryan. The Ghazaryan figure rests on principal of $79,369.13, which is the $41,590.85 owed on the Allcarr note plus the $37,778.28 Ghazaryan separately guaranteed on the Phoenix Auto World note. Ghazaryan is liable for the first sum only as Allcarr's guarantor, and neither proposed judgment says so. As drafted the two judgments total $147,344.97 on a claim whose entire principal is $79,369.13.

Relatedly, the two judgments duplicate the attorney fee calculation.


Finally, the original promissory notes must be presented for cancellation, or, if they are lost, Plaintiff must move for an order permitting copies. 


Case Number: 26STCV01465    Hearing Date: September 2, 2026    Dept: 515

The Complaint and the Request for Default Judgment name the principal owed as $38,478.44.

Iria Ramos's declaration under Code of Civil Procedure section 585(d) similarly claims $38,478.44, the sum of five account balances. However, based on Exhibit 3 to that declaration, it appears that for account 26622161 (DAUGHTRY JAMES / CAINE CYNDIE), Plaintiff inadvertently applied the Dealer proceeds figure ($2,450.14) rather than the remaining balance figure ($1,090.81). The claim amount for every other account matches the remaining balance. Based on Exhibit 3, the supportable principal on this record is $37,119.11.

If Plaintiff agrees with this change, the Court will make the change and sign the proposed judgment.
