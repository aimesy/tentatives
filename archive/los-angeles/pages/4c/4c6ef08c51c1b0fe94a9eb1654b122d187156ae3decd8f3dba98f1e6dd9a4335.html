DEPARTMENT 517 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV05311    Hearing Date: July 27, 2026    Dept: 517

Plaintiff's motion in limine number 8 seeks to preclude Defendant from offering argument, testimony, or evidence that Plaintiff previously made claims or was previously compensated for any claims.  Defendant opposes the motion.  The Court is granting the motion. Evidence on whether Plaintiff suffered injuries in prior automobile accidents or other incidents is relevant.  Plaintiff's motion does not seek to block that evidence.  But whether Plaintiff made clams for compensation or actually was compensated for any claims arising from those accidents/incidents is irrelevant to the issues in this case and would be prejudicial to Plaintiff.

The Court also takes this opportunity to inform counsel that the Court is engage in trial at present and will be through the week of August 3.  So, trial in Saucerman v. Diaz will have to be reset.

The Court will ask counsel today where things stand with respect to efforts to resolve this case.
