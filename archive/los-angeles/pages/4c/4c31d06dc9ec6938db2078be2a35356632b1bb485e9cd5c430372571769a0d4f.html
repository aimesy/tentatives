Case Number: 25NWLC78357    Hearing Date: August 6, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court having considered the application of Plaintiff, Federal Express Corporation ("Plaintiff") and good cause appearing GRANTS Plaintiff's Motion for Order Authorizing Substituted Service on Secretary of State.

The Court finds that Plaintiff has established the requirements set forth in Corporations Code section 1702(a) that an agent for service of process "cannot with reasonable diligence be found at the address designated for personally delivering the process", and it is further shown by the motion that "process against a domestic corporation cannot be served with reasonable diligence upon the designated agent by hand in the manner provided in Section 415.10, subdivision (a) of Section 415.20, or subdivision (a) of Section 415.30 of the Code of Civil Procedure or upon the corporation in the manner provided in Section 416.10 or subdivision (a) of section 416.20 of the code of Civil Procedure".

The Court having considered the application of Plaintiff and good cause appearing makes the following order: Service shall be made, delivering by hand to the Secretary of State, the process for Defendant, Creative Collective, LLC ("Defendant").

Case Number: 26NWLC16052    Hearing Date: August 6, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff, including the declaration of counsel. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on May 11, 2026.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED.  The requested admissions #1-13 are deemed admitted.  The motion is DENIED as to RFA #14 which is compound and vague. 

Plaintiff additionally asks for sanctions in the amount of $810.00, part of which in anticipation of review of Defense Opposition paperwork.  Defense has not filed an Opposition.  The Court has read and reviewed Plaintiff's declaration and determined that two hours for attorney time, $250.00 for attorney rate, and $60.00 for filing fee, totaling $560.00 is appropriate. 

Defendant is ordered to pay Plaintiff $560.00. 

Clerk to give notice.
