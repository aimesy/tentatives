Case Number: 25VECV02943    Hearing Date: September 14, 2026    Dept: T

25VECV02943 Rodriguez v American Honda

On 9/4/2026, Honda objected to having a jury trial because plaintiff did not post fees on time and was willing to have a bench trial.  Plaintiff withdrew her demand for a jury and the notice was filed 9/10/2026 at 12:46 p.m.  Immediately thereafter, Honda filed a notice of posting jury fees at 1 p.m. and at 2:06 pm withdrew its opposition to the late filed fees by plaintiff.

Honda agreed to a bench trial on 9/4/2026 ["At the final status conference on September 4, 2026, eover defense's preference for a bench trial..."]. This is gamesmanship on the part of defendant which the court will not allow.  Honda never demanded a jury in the answer or in a separate pleading until the posting of fees on 9/10/2026.  

The court has considered the factors in TriCoast.  Here, there was no timely notice that defendant requested a jury trial.  Indeed, defendant objected to a jury trial and specifically indicated its preference for a bench trial.  Defendant never filed a demand for a jury trial, nor does it appear in its answer. Here, there was no mistake by Honda for failing to timely demand a jury trial.  Instead, it specifically wanted a bench trial.  Honda's statement that that the failure to timely post fees was not intentional.  Given Honda's objection to a jury trial and expressed preference for a bench trial, it is Honda which made a tactical decision not to demand a jury trial or post fees.

This case will proceed as a bench trial today at 10:00.  

Case Number: 25VECV02950    Hearing Date: September 14, 2026    Dept: T

25VECV02950 Sargsyan v BMW

Ex parte to special set motion to compel depo of defendant’s engineer David Ford: Denied.  The motion was untimely filed.  In order for the motion to have been timely filed, it would have been required to be filed 16 court days+2 calendar days for electronic service so that the potential hearing would be within the discovery cutoff which is 15 days before trial, i.e., Sept. 9. This motion was served on 9/8/2026.  The last date the motion could have been filed to be heard by Sept. 9th is Aug. 24th.  The notice of deposition for Ford was set for Sept. 1 which is less than 30 days before trial and outside the discovery cutoff.

25VECV03586

5150 Shosone v Shalom

Tentative ruling: Defendant’s motion to dismiss or bifurcate

Motion to dismiss: Deny. This is a frivolous motion.  There is no authority to dismiss this case.  Asking the court to dismiss a case based on allegations without a trial, summary judgment motion, or motion for judgment on the pleading is frivolous. 

The court sets an OSC re Sanctions against counsel Mohammad Kamyab Bar #189696 in the sum of $500 payable to the clerk of the court for violation of CCP section 128.5(a)(b)(1)(2) for filing a frivolous motion to dismiss without any merit.

Motion to bifurcate: Deny. It is based on the same facts as the motion to dismiss and the court denies this motion.
