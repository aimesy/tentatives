AUTO FINANCE SOLUTIONS, LLC v. GHOLAM BAAHMADI et al.

CASE NO.: 26NWCV00511

HEARING:  07/08/2026 @ 9:30 a.m.

 

#13

TENTATIVE ORDER

The Court orders supplemental briefing on the following question:

Whether the alleged communications to Pinnacle Financial Partners constitute protected activity under Code of Civil Procedure section 425.16, subdivision (e)(2). Specifically, the parties shall identify authority addressing communications made to private third parties in connection with anticipated or ongoing governmental investigations or litigation, the circumstances under which such communications qualify as protected activity, and whether the communications alleged in this case satisfy those standards.

Moving party shall file supplemental briefing by July 21, 2026, opposing party shall file supplemental briefing by August 4, 2026, and the motion shall be continued to August 11, 2026, at 9:30am.
