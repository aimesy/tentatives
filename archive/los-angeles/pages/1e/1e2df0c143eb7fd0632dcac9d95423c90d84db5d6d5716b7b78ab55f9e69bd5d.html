[TENTATIVE] ORDER RE: DEFENDANT ALEX BASTIEN’S MOTION TO COMPEL PLAINTIFF’S RESPONSES TO SPECIAL INTERROGATORIES, SET ONE

 

Dept. 3

8:30 a.m.

July 2, 2026

 

On November 19, 2025, plaintiff Sergio Estiven Sicaja Bran (“Plaintiff”) filed this action against defendant Alex Bastien (“Defendant”) arising from personal injuries allegedly sustained on Defendant’s property. On February 24, 2026, Defendant served Special Interrogatories (Set One) on Plaintiff. No responses were received. On April 7 and May 1, 2026, defense counsel sent Plaintiff’s counsel a letter requesting responses. However, Plaintiff did not serve responses and Defendant filed this motion on May 5, 2026.

Where a party fails to serve timely responses to discovery requests, the court may make an order compelling responses. (Code Civ. Proc., § 2031.300.) A party that fails to serve timely responses waives any objections to the request, including ones based on privilege or the protection of attorney work product. (Code Civ. Proc., § 2031.300, subd. (a).)

On June 30, 2026, Plaintiff’s counsel filed a declaration stating that responses were served on June 26, 2026. Accordingly, the motion is DENIED as moot.

Moving party to give notice.

Dated this 2nd day of July 2026
