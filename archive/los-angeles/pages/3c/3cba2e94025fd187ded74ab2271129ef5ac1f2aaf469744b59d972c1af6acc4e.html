MOTION TO DEEM REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION ADMITTED; REQUEST FOR SANCTIONS

(CCP § 2033.280)

The Court GRANTS Connect by American Family’s motion to deem matters admitted.  The contents of Connect’s requests for admission are deemed admitted. 

 

Ricardo Perez Luna must pay Connect $360.00 within 30 days of this order.

ANALYSIS:

 

            Connect by American Family filed this subrogation action against Ricardo Perez Luna.  Luna answered.  Connect then propounded four requests for admission on Luna.  Luna failed to respond, so Connect moves to have its requests deemed admitted.

 

A party must respond to requests for admission within 30 days of being served with them.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2033.250, subd. (a).)  If a party fails to do so, it waives the right to object, and the propounding party may move for an order that the genuineness of any documents and truth of any matters specified in the requests be deemed admitted.  (Id., § 2033.280, subds. (a), (b).) 

 

Connect served Luna with requests for admission in November 2025.  To date, Luna hasn’t responded.  Nor has he opposed Connect’s motion.  The Court therefore GRANTS Connect’s motion and.  The contents of Connect’s requests for admission are deemed admitted.

 

Luna must also pay sanctions for causing Connect to move to deem the requests admitted.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2033.280, subd. (c).)  Connect requests $585.00 from Luna, reflecting 3.5 hours of counsel’s work at $150.00 per hour plus $60.00 in filing fees.  The time requested is not reasonable.  The Court reduces it to 2 hours.  Luna must pay Connect $360.00 within 30 days of this order.

 

Connect is to give notice. 


Case Number: 26STCP00458    Hearing Date: August 6, 2026    Dept: 25

HEARING DATE:    Thurs., August 6, 2026                                   JUDGE/DEPT:            Eisenman/25

CASE NAME:           Forward Financing, LLC v. Music Rhapsody, Inc., et al.

PET. FILED:             01-29-26

CASE NUMBER:      26STCP00458                       

NOTICE:                   NO                                                                 

 

 

PROCEEDINGS:      PETITION TO CONFIRM ARBITRATION AWARD

 

MOVING PARTY:   Petitioner Forward Financing LLC 

RESP. PARTY:         None

 

PETITION TO CONFIRM ARBITRATION AWARD

(CCP § 1285)

 

TENTATIVE RULING:

 

The Court previously continued this hearing on Forward Financing LLC’s petition to allow it to serve proper notice of the hearing on Music Rhapsody, Inc. and Deonna Johnson.  Since then, Forward hasn’t filed any proof of service to suggest that it complied.

 

Given that Forward was warned that its “failure to timely file that proof may result in the denial of its petition,” the Court now DENIES the petition.

 

The Court has observed a pattern in this regard with Forward’s petitions.  Presumably the company invested time and resources arbitrating the underlying claims for a reason.  But after filing a petition to confirm, its treatment of these actions does not, in the Court’s view, reflect the diligence that a judicial proceeding deserves.  So the Court ORDERS Forward’s counsel to send this order, and the following three sets of orders, to Alexis Shapiro, the company’s general counsel:

 

·       The Court’s June 5, 2026 and July 16, 2026 orders in Forward Financing LLC v. Vitality Strength and Athletics LLC (26STCP00490), in which Forward’s petition to confirm a $32,379.25 arbitration award was denied because it failed to file proof of service of its petition after being ordered to do so;

 

·       The Court’s April 27, 2026 and May 28, 2026 orders in Forward Financing LLC v. 699Food, Inc. (25STCP04961), in which Forward’s petition to confirm a $34,240.87 arbitration award was taken off the Court’s calendar because counsel failed to appear for hearings set on both of those dates;

 

·       The Court’s March 19, 2026 and May 19, 2026 orders in Forward Financing LLC v. Eric B. Ige, O.D., Inc. (25STCP03711), in which Forward’s petition to confirm a $35,079.10 award was denied for, among other reasons, its failure to file proof of service indicating that the petition was served on the respondent in a manner compliant with California law.

 

Forward’s counsel must file a declaration evidencing Ms. Shapiro’s receipt of this order, as well as the six other orders identified above, on or before Friday, August 14, 2026.  Failure to comply may result in the imposition of sanctions.

 

            Forward is to give notice.
