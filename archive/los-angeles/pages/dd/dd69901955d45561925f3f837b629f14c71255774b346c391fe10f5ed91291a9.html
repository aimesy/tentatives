Judge Kevin C. Brazile

Department 310

Hearing Date: July 31, 2026

Case Name: Hernandez v. New Designs Charter School, et al.

Case No.: 24STCV33432

Matter: Default Judgment 

Ruling: The Default Judgment Application is granted in part.


 Plaintiff, a minor, seeks default judgment against Defendant New Designs Charter School.  Plaintiff alleges that her school failed to protect her from bullying.  

The Default Judgement Application seeks $2 million in damages.

The Court, in the exercise of its discretion, will reduce the damages to $250,000.
