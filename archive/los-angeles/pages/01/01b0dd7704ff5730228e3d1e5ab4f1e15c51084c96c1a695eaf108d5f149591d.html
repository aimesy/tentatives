Case Number: 23STCV31191    Hearing Date: September 22, 2026    Dept: B

Tentative Ruling

Nicholas v. Conningsby & E. Johnson Trust, Case no. 23STCV31191

Hearing date September 22, 2026

Defendant Johnson’s Motion to Compel Plaintiff Nicholas’ Deposition - UNOPPOSED

Plaintiff Nicholas sues defendant Johnson, erroneously sued as Conningsby & E. Johnson Trust, by and through its trustee E. Johnson, for premises liability. Plaintiff alleges she fell, sustaining injury, due to a loose stairwell handrail on defendant’s property. This matter was consolidated with LASC Case no. 23STCV28198, between the same parties, wherein plaintiff alleges she slipped due to a leak in her bathroom. Defendant moves to compel plaintiff’s deposition. The motion is unopposed.

A party may move for an order compelling the attendance and testimony of a deponent where the deponent fails to appear for examination following the service of a deposition notice. Code Civ. Proc. §2025.450.

Defendant request plaintiff’s deposition availability on 8/25/25. Decl. Zverev, para. 9, exh. A. Plaintiff failed to provide dates. Id. Defendant served a deposition notice on 1/9/26. Id. at para. 10, exh. B. Plaintiff objected. Id. at para. 11, exh. C. The parties met and conferred and agreed on an in-person deposition on 4/27/26. Id. at para. 13, exh. D. Plaintiff’s counsel re-affirmed plaintiff’s intent to attend on 4/22/26. Id. at para. 16, exh. G. Plaintiff’s counsel informed defense counsel 13 minutes prior to the scheduled start time that plaintiff would not appear. Decl. Zverev, para. 17, exh. H. Defendant recorded the non-appearance. Id. at para. 18, exhs. I-J. Plaintiff failed to provide new deposition dates. Id. at para. 19. The motion is unopposed; an order compelling plaintiff’s appearance is warranted.

Defendant requests joint and several sanctions of $3,385.80, of $1,325 in attorney’s fees and $2,060.80 in non-appearance deposition costs. Decl. Zverev, paras. 20-21. Plaintiff’s counsel failed to oppose or explain the failure to provide new deposition dates. It is reasonable for sanctions to be imposed jointly and severally. However, plaintiff’s last-minute failure to appear should not be held against her counsel, who did inform defense counsel of plaintiff’s non-appearance as soon as counsel learned of that fact. Id. at exh. H. Sanctions related to plaintiff’s failure to appear are as to plaintiff only.

Defendant requests $1,325 in attorney’s fees and costs. Decl. Zverev, para. 20. Defendant requests a reasonable rate of $265/hour. Id. Defendant requests 5 hours for motion drafting, reply and appearance at hearing. Id. No opposition or reply was filed. The court awards 2 hours for motion drafting and 1 hour for hearing, 3 hours total at $265/hour. The court awards $60 in filing costs. Joint and several sanctions of $855 awarded against plaintiff and plaintiff’s counsel.

Defendant requests $2,060.80 in non-appearance costs. Decl. Zverev, para. 21, exh. K. Plaintiff’s untimely notice and non-appearance caused defendant to incur these costs. Sanctions of $2,060.80 awarded against plaintiff only.

GRANTED; plaintiff to appear at noticed deposition within 30 days. Sanctions payable as above within 30 days.


Case Number: 24VECV05351    Hearing Date: September 22, 2026    Dept: B

Tentative Ruling

Zargaryan v. Sargsyan, Case no. 24VECV05351

Hearing date September 22, 2026

Plaintiff Zargaryan’s Motion to Set Aside Dismissal

Plaintiff Zargaryan sues defendant Sargsyan for defamation. Defendant moved for sanctions pursuant to Code Civ. Proc. §128.5, arguing she is not a proper defendant. The court granted the unopposed motion on 7/7/26, dismissing this action with prejudice and awarding $15,073.10 in attorney’s fees and $595.52 in costs against plaintiff and counsel. See 7/7/26 Min. Order. Plaintiff moves to set aside the dismissal and sanctions order, asserting attorney neglect pursuant to Code Civ. Proc. §473(b). Defendant opposes.

“Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the court shall, whenever an application for relief is made no more than six months after entry of judgment, is in proper form, and is accompanied by an attorney's sworn affidavit attesting to his or her mistake, inadvertence, surprise, or neglect, vacate any... resulting default judgment or dismissal entered against his or her client...” Code Civ. Proc. §473(b).

Plaintiff asserts counsel received notice of the motion for sanctions but failed to calendar the opposition deadline or hearing date, resulting in failure to oppose the motion or appear at the hearing. Decl. Hasic, para. 2. Plaintiff argues relief from dismissal is mandatory where counsel attests to their mistake.

Defendant argues relief from a motion for sanctions is not proper under Code Civ. Proc. §473(b), citing English v. IKON Bus. Sols., Inc. (2001) 94 Cal.App.4th 130, 133 for the proposition that section 473(b) only applies to defaults and default judgements. English concerned a plaintiff who sought relief from a motion for summary judgment that was granted because plaintiff’s counsel failed to oppose. The English court ruled against plaintiff on the basis that summary judgment is neither a default judgment nor a dismissal. Id. at 148-149.

Per Rodriguez v. WNT, Inc. (2025) 116 Cal.App.5th 791, 806, a “terminating sanction qualifies as a dismissal under section 473(b).” Further, the court must grant relief from a dismissal under Code Civ. Proc. §473(b) where counsel has attested to their own neglect, “even if the neglect was inexcusable.” Metro. Serv. Corp. v. Casa de Palms, Ltd. (1995) 31 Cal.App.4th 1481, 1487. As plaintiff’s counsel proffered an affidavit attesting to their own neglect, the court must grant relief from the dismissal.

Defendant argues no relief from the sanctions should be granted. The court granted sanctions on the basis of attorney’s fees and costs incurred by defendant in the course of defending this action, on the premise that this action had been dismissed. As the action is again active, there is no basis to award fees or costs at this time. Per Code Civ. Proc. §473(c)(1)(A), the court imposes a $1,000 penalty on plaintiff’s counsel Hasic only for the admitted neglect, payable within 30 days.

GRANTED; the court’s 7/7/26 order dismissing this action and awarding sanctions is set aside. Sanctions payable within 30 days.
