DEPARTMENT Z LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24NWLC53731    Hearing Date: August 10, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on August 11, 2025.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.    Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED as to RFA's #1-12.  The motion is DENIED as to RFA #13 which is compound and vague.


Case Number: 25NWLC37797    Hearing Date: August 10, 2026    Dept: Z

Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by the Plaintiff.

Court takes judicial notice of the stipulation signed by the Defendant on August 6, 2025.

Defendant has paid $6,812.00. 

No additional payments have been made and Defendant remains in default.

Based on the stipulation and moving papers, Plaintiff's motion is granted.

Notice of Settlement and Dismissal pursuant to CCP 664.6 is set aside. Judgement in favor of Plaintiff in the amount of $15,375.19 for the principal.  Proposed Order will be signed.
