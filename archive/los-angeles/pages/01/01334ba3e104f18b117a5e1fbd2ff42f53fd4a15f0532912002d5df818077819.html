DEPARTMENT F47 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

Counsel wishing to submit on a tentative ruling may inform the clerk or courtroom assisant in North Valley Department F47, 9425 Penfield Ave., Chatsworth, CA 91311, at (818) 407-2247.  Please be aware that unless all parties submit, the matter will still be called for hearing and may be argued by any appearing/non-submitting parties. If the matter is submitted on the court's tentative ruling by all parties, counsel for moving party shall give notice of ruling. This may be done by incorporating verbatim the court's tentative ruling. The tentative ruling may be extracted verbatim by copying and specially pasting, as unformatted text, from the Los Angeles Superior Court’s website, http://www.lasuperiorcourt.org. All hearings on law and motion and other calendar matters are generally NOT transcribed by a court reporter unless one is provided by the party(ies).

Dept. F47

Date: 7/15/26

Case #25CHCV01278

 

MOTION TO BE RELIEVED AS COUNSEL

 

Motion filed on 3/3/26.

 

MOVING ATTORNEY: Peter J. McNulty and Brett L. Rosenthal

CLIENT: Plaintiff Karen Greek

 

RELIEF REQUESTED: An order relieving Peter J. McNulty and Brett L. Rosenthal as counsel for Plaintiff Karen Greek in this action. 

 

RULING:

 

On 3/3/26, attorneys Peter J. McNulty and Brett L. Rosenthal filed the instant motion to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff Karen Greek (Plaintiff) in this action due to a break down in the attorney-client relationship.  No other party has appeared in the action.

 

The proof of service for the motion indicates that on 3/3/26, the motion was served by overnight delivery on Plaintiff at 10827 Gloria Avenue, Granada Hills, CA 91344 which is the same address listed in the proposed order.  However, the Notice of Motion lists Plaintiff’s address as 10829 Gloria Avenue, Granada Hills, CA 91344.  (See Notice of Motion, p.1 (above No.1)).

 

Counsel must explain the discrepancy.  If the typographical error is in the Notice of Motion, counsel must file a declaration explaining such and the motion will be granted.  If the error is in the proof of service and proposed order, the hearing on the motion will be placed off calendar so that the error can be corrected and the motion can be served on the client at the correct address.
