Case Number: 25NWLC27214    Hearing Date: July 28, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC27214: BANK OF AMERICA N.A. vs ELIZABETH TORRES

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. (“Plaintiff”).  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on August 29, 2025 and filed with the Court on September 10, 2025.   The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order on Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Judgment entered by the Court on September 19, 2025.

The Court also notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $450.00, leaving an outstanding amount of $3,471.50 due and owing.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order Dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. and against Defendant Elizabeth Torres for the remaining sum of $3,471.50 plus court costs of $603.61, for a total judgment of $4,075.11.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC49537    Hearing Date: July 28, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC49537: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK N.A. vs JESSICA GARCIA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the declaration of counsel.   The Court finds that Plaintiff mailed Requests for Admission (RFA) to Defendant on December 12, 2025, and provided proof of service by mail.  Plaintiff did not receive any responses from Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed Defendant its Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted on April 22, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Defendant’s address on both proofs of service match the address that Defendant listed on the Answer.

Having considered the moving papers, Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted is GRANTED.

Plaintiff to give notice.
