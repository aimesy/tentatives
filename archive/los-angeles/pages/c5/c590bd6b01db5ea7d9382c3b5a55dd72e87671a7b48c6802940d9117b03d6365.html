Case Number: 23NWCV01361    Hearing Date: July 31, 2026    Dept: C


 

SHAZIA AKMAL vs BMW OF NORTH AMERICA, LLC, et al., Case No. 23NWCV01361

This lemon law action was filed on May 4, 2023 and set for trial on September 2, 2026.  Defendant BMW of North America applies ex parte for an order advancing its Motion for Terminating Sanctions currently set for October 13, 2026, or in the alternative, continuing trial to allow the motion to be heard before trial.  The motion for terminating sanctions is made on the grounds that Plaintiff has failed to comply with the court’s February 5, 2026 order to produce Plaintiff’s expert for deposition on February 27, 2026, and Plaintiff’s vehicle for inspection on February 23, 2026.  Defendant gave Plaintiff another chance after failing to comply with the court’s orders, but Plaintiff then failed to produce the expert file prior to expert deposition on July 14, 2026, rendering Defendant unable to conduct the deposition.  Defendant requests terminating sanctions, exclusion of Plaintiff’s expert from trial and/or monetary sanctions in the amount of $1,995.00.  On February 5, 2026, during a hearing on Defendant’s ex parte hearing to advance its Motion to Compel the Deposition of Plaintiff’s Experts and Motion to Compel Vehicle Inspection, the parties stipulated that the vehicle inspection will occur on February 23, 2026 and the deposition of Plaintiff’s expert will occur on February 27, 2026. The parties further stipulated that trial will be continued to 10:00 a.m. on September 2, 2026.  No sanctions were imposed. (2/5/26 Minute Order.) Since then, Defendant’s efforts to reschedule the expert deposition and the vehicle inspection have proved unsuccessful. (Sheehan Decl., ¶¶18-25.) The ex parte application is GRANTED in part.  The motion for terminating sanctions is ADVANCED from October 13, 2026 to today and GRANTED in part.  Monetary sanctions in the amount of $1,995.00 are imposed upon Plaintiff and Plaintiff’s Counsel, jointly and severally, payable within 30 days.  Plaintiff is ordered to produce Plaintiff’s expert for deposition and Plaintiff’s vehicle for inspection by no later than August 14, 2026.  The August 19, 2026 Final Status Conference date and September 2, 2026 Trial date remain on calendar.  Defendant to give notice.   


Case Number: 23NWCV03412    Hearing Date: July 31, 2026    Dept: C

ESTHER ALICIA ARMENTA vs LOS ANGELES COUNTY METROPOLITAN TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY, et al., Case No. 23NWCV03412

This personal injury action was filed on October 24, 2023.  Plaintiff Esther Alicia Armenta, pro per, alleges she became stuck under a metal ramp of a bus operated by Defendant Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority and driven by Defendant Jane Doe.  Defendant Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority moves for terminating sanctions against Plaintiff on the grounds that Plaintiff has repeatedly failed to comply with Court-ordered alternative dispute resolution proceedings. Specifically, Plaintiff failed to participate in the Court-referred Mediation Volunteer Panel ("MVP") mediation after confirming her attendance.  Additionally, following this Court's May 14, 2026 Order directing the parties to participate in a Mandatory Settlement Conference conducted through the Resolve Law LA Virtual MSC Program, Plaintiff again failed to participate despite expressly confirming her availability and receiving repeated notices, reminders, and instructions regarding the conference.  On July 23, 2026, the motion was advanced from October 6, 2026 to today, the date set for trial.  Plaintiff has not opposed the motion.  Plaintiff has also failed to cooperate with Defendant in the preparation of joint trial documents as ordered by the Court on July 23, 2026. (Declaration of Albert De Blanc, Jr.) The motion for terminating sanctions is GRANTED.  Plaintiff’s repeated failure to comply with the Court’s orders has prejudiced Defendant’s ability to obtain a cost-effective case resolution and to prepare for trial.  Plaintiff’s Complaint is dismissed with prejudice.  Moving party to give notice. 

 


Case Number: 25NWCV04377    Hearing Date: July 31, 2026    Dept: C

 

ALLEN BENJAMIN CIEGO vs BRIAN IVAN ROSAS MEDINA, et al., Case No. 25NWCV04377

This case arises from a motor vehicle accident which occurred on March 24, 2025.  Plaintiff ALLEN BENJAMIN CIEGO was the driver of a vehicle which was rear-ended by a vehicle driven by Defendant BRIAN IVAN ROSAS MEDINA.  DENISE CIEGO was a passenger in the vehicle being driven by Plaintiff ALLEN BENJAMIN CIEGO and suffered injuries to her neck, back, and other damages as a result of the collision.  Plaintiff ALLEN BENJAMIN CIEGO moves for leave to file and amended complaint adding DENISE CIEGO as a Plaintiff because DENISE CIEGO was inadvertently omitted from the original complaint.  No opposition has been filed.  Trial is set for September 17, 2027.  The motion is GRANTED.  Plaintiffs shall separately file and serve the First Amended Complaint within 5 days.  Moving party to give notice. 


Case Number: 25NWCV04634    Hearing Date: July 31, 2026    Dept: C

LISA MARIE VERNOLA-SALAS vs URBAN SOCCER 5 CENTER, LLC, Case No. 25NWCV04634

This commercial unlawful detainer action was filed on December 30, 2025.  Trial is currently set for August 3, 2026.  On July 29, 2026, the Court ordered Defendant Urban Soccer 5 Center to post bond in the amount of $62,228.83 per month on or before the 10th of each month beginning August 10, 2026.  Defendant applies ex parte for an order continuing trial for 30 days to allow Defendant to complete the depositions of previously subpoenaed non-party witnesses and to allow the Court to hear Defendant’s Motion to Compel Further Discovery Responses.  Defendant also requests a briefing schedule for all motions in limine.  The ex parte application is GRANTED in part.  Trial is ADVANCED from August 3, 2026 to today and CONTINUED to September 3, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. in Dept. SE-C.  The Court will hear from the parties regarding a schedule for the remaining depositions.  The Court sets an Informal Discovery Conference for Defendant’s Motion to Compel Further Discovery Responses on August 7, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. in Dept. SE-C.  Counsel is ordered to further meet and confer before the IDC to resolve their discovery disputes.  If any matters remain unresolved after doing so, each party shall file a brief stating their respective positions, not to exceed three pages, no later than August 5, 2026.  All motions in limine are due August 25, 2026.  Oppositions are due September 1, 2026.  The parties are also ordered to file joint trial documents by September 1, 2026.  Moving party to give notice.
