DEPARTMENT O LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24SMCV00775    Hearing Date: September 8, 2026    Dept: O

TENTATIVE RULING

September 8, 2026

24SMCV00775 (Valenzuela v. Mutakabbir)

           

INSTANT MOTION

 

             On February 13, 2026, plaintiff Ashly Valenzuela (Plaintiff) filed the instant motion to compel further responses of defendant Kinyumba Mutakabbir (Defendant).

 

            No opposition has been filed.

 

DISCUSSION

 

            Plaintiff’s motion to compel further is DENIED on procedural grounds. The motion improperly combines motions to compel further responses to various discovery requests and fails to include a separate statement as required by California Rules of Court, rule 3.1345. Additionally, it is not clear that Defendant has been properly served, as the proof of service indicates that the motion was served by electronic service, but there is no indication that the parties agreed to accept electronic service under California Rules of Court, rule 2.251.
