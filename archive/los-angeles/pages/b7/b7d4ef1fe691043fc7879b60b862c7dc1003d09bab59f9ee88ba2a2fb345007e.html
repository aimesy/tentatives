Case Number: 26NWLC10150    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff, including the declaration of counsel. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on May 11, 2026.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED.  The requested admissions #1-13 are deemed admitted.  The motion is DENIED as to RFA #14 which is compound and over broad. 

Plaintiff additionally asks for sanctions in the amount of $810.00, part of which in anticipation of review of Defense Opposition paperwork.  Defense has not filed an Opposition.  The Court has read and reviewed Plaintiff's declaration and determined that two hours for attorney time, $250.00 for attorney rate, and $60.00 for filing fee, totaling $560.00 is appropriate. 

Defendant is ordered to pay Plaintiff $560.00. 

Clerk to give notice.
