AUTO FINANCE SOLUTIONS, LLC vs GHOLAM BAAHMADI, et al.

CASE NO.:  26NWCV00511

HEARING:  7/9/2026 @ 9:30 a.m.

 

#13

TENTATIVE ORDER

 

The Court strikes the tentative posted in error on July 8, 2026 as the supplemental briefing schedule stated is incorrect. The Court orders supplemental briefing on the following question:

 

Whether the alleged communications to Pinnacle Financial Partners constitute protected activity under Code of Civil Procedure section 425.16, subdivision (e)(2). Specifically, the parties shall identify authority addressing communications made to private third parties in connection with anticipated or ongoing governmental investigations or litigation, the circumstances under which such communications qualify as protected activity, and whether the communications alleged in this case satisfy those standards.

 

Moving party’s supplemental briefing must be filed no later than July 30, 2026

Opposing party’s supplemental briefing must be filed no later than August 20, 2026.

Reply to opposing party’s supplemental briefing must be filed no later than August 27, 2026.

 

The Motion is continued to September 1, 2026, at 9:30am.
