DEPARTMENT P LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 23NWCV00035    Hearing Date: June 25, 2026    Dept: P

Johnsrud v. Guzman, M.D., et al., 23NWCV00035 

This medical malpractice action was filed on January 5, 2023. Trial is scheduled to begin on August 10, 2026. Defendant AHMC Whittier Hospital Medical Center LP (Defendant) now applies ex parte to continue the trial date to January 11, 2027, or a date thereafter. Defendant states that counsels for both parties have trial conflicts. Defendant provides a joint stipulation (Joint Stipulation) in which the parties agree to continue trial to January 11, 2027, or a date thereafter, and for related deadlines to correspond with the new trial date.

 

Defendant’s ex parte application to continue trial is GRANTED pursuant to the Joint Stipulation and California Rules of Court, rule 3.1332(c)(3) – “[t]he unavailability of trial counsel because of…other excusable circumstances.” The Court will confer with the parties to select new trial and FSC dates on or after January 11, 2027. The discovery cut-off dates and related deadlines will correspond with the new trial date. Moving party to give notice.
