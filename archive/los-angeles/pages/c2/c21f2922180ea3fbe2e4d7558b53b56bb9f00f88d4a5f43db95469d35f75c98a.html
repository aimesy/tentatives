ORDER RE: DISTRIBUTION OF SURPLUS FUNDS FROM TRUSTEE’S SALE

 

 

 

 

 

Dept. 3

8:30 a.m.

July 13, 2026

 

)

 

 

This special proceeding arises from a trustee’s sale of real property, located at 540 N. Marengo Avenue, Apt. 3, Pasadena, CA 911011 (“Property”). The trustor on the deed of trust was Robert G. May, who passed away on March 31, 2012. A trustee’s sale was held on September 19, 2022, which resulted in a surplus of funds that were deposited with the Court to be distributed to the claimants.  

Pursuant to Civil Code section 2924k, the surplus funds shall be distributed to the State Controller in the amount of $3,250.87, with the remaining amount going to the Estate of Robert G. May. If the funds are not claimed by the Estate within three years from the date of this order, they shall become unclaimed property and escheat to the State of California under Code of Civil Procedure section 1519.

Edward Morales of Shumener, Odson & Oh LLP seeks to be relieved as counsel of record for defendants Luis D. Rojas, DelTerra Construction Company, Inc., and DelTerra Real Estate Services, Inc. (collectively, “Defendants”) on grounds that irreconcilable differences have arisen. Absent a showing of resulting prejudice, an attorney’s request for withdrawal should be granted. (People v. Prince (1968) 268 Cal.App.2d 398, 406.)

Counsel’s Motion complies with California Rules of Court, Rule 3.1362. The Court notes that trial in this matter is not yet set and no prejudice will result from granting this motion.

Accordingly, this unopposed motion to be relieved is GRANTED and effective upon filing a proof of service showing service of this Order on Defendants and all parties who have appeared.

The Court additionally sets an OSC re: Proof of Service of Order Relieving Counsel for ______ at 8:30 a.m. THE COURT EMPAHASIZES THE IMPORTANCE OF THIS OSC DEADLINE AS COUNSEL WILL NOT BE RELIEVED UNTIL THIS PROOF OF SERVICE IS FILED.

[TENTATIVE] ORDER RE: MOTION FOR ATTORNEY FEES

 

Dept. 3

8:30 a.m.

July 13, 2026

 

)

 

 

Plaintiff Samuel Zoquiapa (“Plaintiff”) moves for an award of attorney fees, costs, and expenses in the amount of $17,349.79, consisting of $12,862.50 incurred in attorney fees, $2,262.29 in costs and expenses, and an additional $2,250 in anticipated costs related to this motion. Defendant General Motors, LLC (“Defendant”) did not oppose this motion.

The award for incurred attorney fees is based on: (1) the attorneys’ hourly rates ranging from $430 to $530 and (2) the paralegals’ hourly rate of $215. These rates are reasonable given each individual’s relative experience and qualifications set forth in the supporting declaration of Arbre Kornely. (Kornely Decl., ¶¶ 12-18.) Plaintiff’s counsel’s fee award is also based on 31.5 hours of work which, according to the submitted time records, is a reasonable amount of time given the factual and procedural background of the case.

Defendant did not oppose this motion and Plaintiff’s counsel did not have to review any opposition brief or draft any reply brief. Therefore, Plaintiff’s attorney fee award is limited to the $12,862.50 in fees that were actually incurred and $2,262.29 in uncontested costs, for a total of $15,124.79.
