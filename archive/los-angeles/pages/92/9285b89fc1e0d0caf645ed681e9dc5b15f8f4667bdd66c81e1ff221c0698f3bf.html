ANALYSIS:

 

I.                Background

 

On August 26, 2025, Plaintiff Whisper N. Windsor (“Plaintiff”) filed the instant PAGA action against Defendant Volunteers of America Los Angeles (“Defendant”) and Does 1-100.

 

On December 10, 2025, Defendant filed an Answer.

 

On July 20, 2026, Counsel Eric D. Tims of Lavi & Ebrahimian, LLP (“Counsel”) filed the instant Motion to be Relieved as Counsel for Plaintiff (the “Motion”).

 

No Opposition to the Motion has been filed.

 

II.              Procedural Issues

 

As an initial matter, the Court finds the Motion was untimely filed and served. The Motion was served by mail on Plaintiff and email on Defendant on July 20, 2026. The service by mail on Plaintiff occurred less than 16 court days and five calendar days prior to the instant hearing. (Code Civ. Proc., § 1005.) As such, the Motion was untimely filed and served on Plaintiff.

 

The Court may not rule on the Motion absent proper service and notice of hearing.

 

Additionally, the Court notes that the MC-053 form entitled “Order Granting Attorney's Motion to Be Relieved as Counsel-Civil” does not include all accurate dates and future dates.

 

Counsel must resubmit the MC-53 Form with the Court providing all the accurate dates and future dates.

 

III.            Conclusion & Order

 

Counsel Eric D. Tims of Lavi & Ebrahimian, LLP’s Motion to be Relieved as Counsel for Plaintiff Whisper N. Windsor is CONTINUED to Monday, September 14, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. in Department 512 of the Stanley Mosk Courthouse.

 

At least 16 court days prior to the next scheduled hearing, Counsel is to file and serve proper proof of service of the Motion and notice of hearing on the parties.

 

Counsel must also resubmit the MC-53 Form with the Court providing all the accurate dates and future dates.

 

Plaintiff’s Counsel is ordered to give notice.
