Case Number: 25NWLC51200    Hearing Date: June 25, 2026    Dept: Z

25NWLC51200: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs DANIEL E HERNANDEZ
The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff in support of the Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including a meet and confer letter dated April 6, 2026.
The Court notes that Plaintiff has filed proof of service of this motion on Defendant at the same address Defendant listed on their Answer.
Pursuant to Cal. Evid. Code § 452 and § 453, the Court GRANTS the request for the Court to take judicial notice of:
  1. The Complaint filed by Plaintiff on September 8, 2025;
  2. The Order granting Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted as to RFA # 1-8, entered on March 30, 2026; and
  3. Request for admissions filed February 13, 2026, in support of Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted.
The Court also takes judicial notice of:
  1. Defendant’s Answer filed on October 7, 2025.
The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot be reasonably controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions admitted are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff. 
The Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.
The Court orders Judgement in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $8,631.67 and $425.00 for costs.  The Court will also award Plaintiff attorneys fees.  Plaintiff has failed to establish the reasonable hours spent on the case and the reasonable hourly rate as required by the "lodestar method" pursuant to Civil Code section 1717.  The Court will award fees pursuant to Los Angeles Superior Court Local Rules, Rule 3.214, in the amount of $760.53.  For a judgment of $8,631.67, the schedule dictates the following:
  • **First $1,000.00:** 15% ($150.00)
  • **Remaining $7,631.67:** 8% ($610.53)
  • Total Scheduled Fee: $760.53
The total judgment ordered is $8,631.67 for the principal, $425.00 for the costs, and $760.53 for attorney's fees, for a total judgment of $9,817.20.

Case Number: 25NWLC54473    Hearing Date: June 25, 2026    Dept: Z

 25NWLC54473: NAVY FEDERAL CREDIT UNION vs ERIC R. ESPIRITU
The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff, including the declaration of counsel. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on November 5, 2025.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED.  The requested admissions #1-7 are deemed admitted.  
Plaintiff additionally asks for sanctions in the amount of $360.00.  The Court has read and reviewed Plaintiff's declaration and determined that one hour for attorney time, $300.00 for attorney rate, and $65.00 for filing fee, totaling $360.00 is appropriate. 
Defendant is ordered to pay Plaintiff $360.00.
