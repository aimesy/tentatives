DEPARTMENT Z LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25NWLC69396    Hearing Date: September 14, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court finds Plaintiff has met the initial burden of showing that there is no defense to the causes of action by proving each element of the causes of action entitling Plaintiff to judgment.  

On November 17, 2025 , Plaintiff WELLS FARGO BANK filed this credit card collections action against Defendant CARSYN M DENNEY.  Plaintiff extended credit to Defendant through a loan and Defendant breached the agreement by failing to pay as agreed.  Plaintiff's Complaint alleges Breach of Written Contract. 

The Court has read and reviewed Plaintiff's Motion for Summary Judgment, Separate Statement of Undisputed Facts, Declaration of Plaintiff's Most Qualified Witness and attached exhibits. The Court finds that Plaintiff has met its initial burden of establishing all elements in support of the breach of contract claim.  There is no triable issue of material fact.  

The burden having shifted to Defendant, the Court finds Defendant has failed to set forth specific facts showing that a triable issue of material fact exists as to the causes of action or a defense thereto as required by CCP 437c(p)(1).

The court finds that Plaintiff is entitled to judgment as a matter of law and GRANTS the Motion for Summary Judgment. Judgment to enter in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $12,989.99, plus court costs and reasonable attorney's fees. Plaintiff to file a proposed judgment, memorandum of costs, and declaration of attorney's fees.  If the memorandum of costs or the declaration of attorney's fees is not filed with the court by the next court date, the Court will order judgment for the principal only.
