The court orders that the vehicle inspection take place on September 2, 3, or 4.  Monetary sanctions are assessed pursuant to CCP section 2031.300 in the sum of $1,000 against plaintiffs for their discovery abuse in not proving ANY dates for six months, then only giving one and two day notice of availability, and then not making September dates available until 8 months after the first request.  No substantial justification.  No reasonable dates provided until after the motion was filed.  Sanctions to be paid to the client trust account of defendant no later than 8/28/2026.  Clerk to give notice.
