Case Number: 24NWLC33044    Hearing Date: August 3, 2026    Dept: Y

24NWLC33044: SYNCHRONY BANK vs CLAUDIA GONZALEZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Synchrony Bank (“Plaintiff”), the supporting declaration, and all relevant court records.  

In its motion, Plaintiff requests to vacate the Default Judgment entered on November 21, 2025 and dismiss the action with prejudice.   Plaintiff has presented evidence via declaration that Plaintiff received notification that Defendant Claudia Gonzalez (“Defendant”) claimed fraud on the account.  Plaintiff has reviewed and confirmed the fraud claim. 

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473(b), the Court may set aside any judgment that was obtained by mistake or surprise.  Code of Civil Procedure section 128 also grants jurisdiction to the Court to take appropriate action in the interests of justice.

Based on the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Set Aside and Vacate Default Judgment.   Accordingly, the Default and Default Judgment entered against Defendant on November 21, 2025 is vacated. 

The Court further orders that the Complaint filed by Plaintiff and against Defendant is DISMISSED with prejudice.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC10719    Hearing Date: August 3, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC10719: WELLS FARGO BANK, NA vs SARAH W ESCOBAR, AN INDIVIDUAL

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed all moving documents filed by Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its motion for summary judgment, including the separate statement and supporting evidence. 

Plaintiff’s motion for summary judgment was filed on November 10, 2025 and set for hearing on February 9, 2026.  On December 8, 2025, the Court continued the hearing to August 3, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Sarah W. Escobar (“Defendant”).

The Court finds that Plaintiff has met the initial burden of showing that there are no defenses to its cause of action for breach of contract by proving each element of the cause of action entitling Plaintiff to summary judgment. 

Plaintiff has shown with admissible evidence that Defendant applied for and executed a personal loan agreement with Plaintiff; Defendant agreed to repay the loan pursuant to the terms of the agreement; Plaintiff performed under the terms of the agreement including funding the loan; the loan statements for the subject account were sent to Defendant; and there is no record of any unresolved disputes on subject personal loan account.  (Plaintiff’s Separate Statement, Undisputed Material Facts Nos. [UMF] 1-7.)  The evidence also shows that Defendant’s last payment on the subject account was on July 18, 2024 and that Defendant owes Plaintiff the unpaid balance on the subject account of $15,024.98.  (Id., UMF Nos. 8-11.) 

The burden having shifted to Defendant, the Court finds Defendant has failed to set forth specific facts showing that a triable issue of material fact exists as to any of the elements of the causes of action for breach of contract or that there is any defense thereto required by Code of Civil Procedure section 437c(p)(1). 

The Court finds that Plaintiff is entitled to judgment as a matter of law and GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion for Summary Judgment as to its cause of action for breach of contract. 

Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. and against Defendant Sarah W. Escobar in the principal sum of $15,024.98, plus court costs in the amount of $920.00 for a total judgment in the sum of $15,944.98. 

The Proposed Order and Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Case Number: 25NWLC48271    Hearing Date: August 3, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC48271: LVNV FUNDING LLC vs MALERIE GOMEZ

Tentative Ruling:

Defendant Malerie M. Gomez (“Defendant”) has filed and served the following four motions:

(1) Motion for Order Deeming Matters Admitted (RFA Motion). On July 9, 2026, Defendant filed and served the RFA Motion on Plaintiff via U.S. Mail.  The hearing for this Motion is scheduled on August 3, 2026.

(2) Motion to Compel Response to Special Interrogatories (Motion to Compel – Special Interrogatories). On July 13, 2026, Defendant filed her Motion to Compel – Special Interrogatories.  Defendant served this motion on Plaintiff via U.S. Mail on July 12, 2026.  The hearing for this Motion is also scheduled on August 3, 2026.

(3)  Motion for Order Compelling Responses to Request for Production (Motion to Compel – Document Requests). On July 13, 2026, Defendant filed her Motion to Compel – Document Requests.  Defendant served this motion on Plaintiff via U.S. Mail on July 12, 2026.  The hearing for this Motion is also scheduled on August 3, 2026.

(4) Motion for Continuance of Trial Date and Extension of Discovery Cutoffs (Motion for Trial Continuance). On July 15, 2026, Defendant filed her Motion For Trial Continuance.  Defendant served this motion on Plaintiff via U.S. Mail on July 14, 2026.  The hearing for this Motion is scheduled on August 5, 2026.

Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 requires that “all moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. . . . if the notice is served by mail, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by five calendar days if the place of mailing and place of address are within the State of California.” 

It does not appear that proper notice has been given on the four motions filed by Defendant.  For the motions set for hearing on August 3, 2026, Defendant should have served the motions on Plaintiff on or before July 2, 2026.  For the motion set for hearing on August 5, 2026, Defendant should have served that motion on Plaintiff on or before July 10, 2026.  Accordingly, the Court will continue the hearing on the motions so that proper notice of the motions can be given to Plaintiff.   The motions will be continued and scheduled as follows:

The hearing on the RFA Motion will be scheduled on September 3, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y.

The hearing on the Motion to Compel – Document Requests will be scheduled on September 10, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y. 

The hearing on the Motion to Compel – Special Interrogatories will be scheduled on October 2, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y. 

On its own motion, the Court will also advance and vacate the trial date on August 28, 2026.  The Court sets a new trial date on May 7, 2027 at 8:30 a.m. in Dept. Y.   The Court’s continuance of the trial date moots Defendant’s Motion for Trial Continuance, which is scheduled for hearing on August 5, 2026.  The Court expects that Defendant will take that motion off calendar.

Clerk to give notice.
