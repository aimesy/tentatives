DEPARTMENT 9 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24STCV04294    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: 9

The escalator clause in the Settlement Agreement at ¶ 8 provides that the Gross Settlement Amount is based on an estimated 15,873 pay periods during the PAGA Period but that if the actual number of pay periods exceeds this by more than 10%, the total Gross Settlement Amount will increase by a corresponding amount in excess of 10%.  However, Plaintiff has not indicated in the moving papers whether this escalator clause has been triggered. 

 

The Parties must be prepared to address at the hearing whether the escalator has been triggered.
