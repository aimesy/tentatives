DEPARTMENT 25 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

 *** The Judicial Officer Presiding in Department 25 is JUDGE JONATHAN H. EISENMAN ***

Tentative Rulings are posted before the hearing. If you desire to submit on the tentative ruling, you may do so by e-mailing Dept. 25 at the Spring Street Courthouse up until the morning of the motion hearing. The e-mail address is [email protected]. The heading on your e-mail should contain the case name, number, hearing date, and that you submit. The message should indicate your name, contact information, and the party you represent. The party submitting to the tentative must include to the e-mail any other party to the action.

Please note that if a party fails to appear at a law and motion hearing without having given notice under California Rules of Court, rule 3.1204, subd. (c), the court may take the matter off calendar, to be reset only upon motion, or may rule on the matter. If only one party has submitted, the Court may still hear the motion at the hearing or adopt the tentative ruling. 
   
Please note, the above e-mail address is ONLY to inform the court of your submission on the tentative ruling. All other inquiries will not receive a response. 

All parties may appear remotely for law and motion hearings.  A party may register to attend remotely by accessing the court’s website at www.lacourt.ca.gov and by registering through LACourtConnect. This service is accessed via the “Online Services” tab at the top of the webpage, and by selecting “Remote Hearings” below the “Courtroom Services,” then by selecting “Civil.”

PROCEEDINGS:      PETITION FOR CERTIFICATION OF FACTS FOR CONTEMPT SANCTIONS AND REQUEST FOR COURT TO ISSUE AN ORDER TO SHOW CAUSE RE: CONTEMPT [GOV. CODE, § 11455.20]

 

MOVING PARTY:   Petitioner Department of General Services

RESP. PARTY:         None

 

PETITION FOR ORDER TO SHOW CAUSE RE: CONTEMPT

(Gov. Code, § 11455.20) 

 

TENTATIVE RULING:

 

            The Department of General Services petitions this Court to order Ozelia Harris and Monique Harris to appear and show cause why they should not be subject to contempt sanctions for disobeying lawful orders in the administrative proceeding OAH No. 2024110192.

 

            Under Government Code section 11455.10, a person may be subject to contempt sanctions for engaging in certain proscribed conduct in an adjudication before a state agency.  To impose sanctions, the head of the agency or the officer presiding at the adjudication may certify to the superior court the facts amounting to contempt.  (Gov. Code, § 11455.20, subd. (a).)  The court shall then issue an order directing the person to show cause why they should not be punished for contempt.  (Ibid.) 

 

            Cindy F. Forman, an administrative law judge in the Office of Administrative Hearings, certified to this Court facts to justify holding Ozelia Harris and Monique Harris in contempt.

 

            Accordingly, the Court ORDERS Ozelia Harris and
Monique Harris TO SHOW CAUSE, on August 12, 2026, at 10:30 a.m. in Department
25 of the Spring Street Courthouse, why they should not be subject to contempt
sanctions.



 

            The Department is to serve this order and a copy of the certified statement of facts on Ozelia Harris and Monique Harris in compliance with Government Code section 11455.20, subdivision (a).
