Case Number: 25NWLC29569    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff in support of the Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including a meet and confer letter dated August 15, 2025, as well as the Complaint and Answer.

The Court finds Defendant, in the Answer, did not deny the statements of the complaint or indicate an affirmative defense but rather reported a recent accident and financial hardship.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $8,592.58 and costs in the amount of $358.61, for a total judgment of $8,951.19.

Case Number: 25NWLC47956    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff in support of the Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including a meet and confer letter dated May 14, 2026.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has filed proof of service of this motion on Defendant at the same address Defendant listed on the Answer.

Pursuant to Cal. Evid. Code § 452 and § 453, the Court GRANTS the request for the Court to take judicial notice of:

  1. The Complaint filed by Plaintiff on August 22, 2025;
  2. The Order granting Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted as to RFA # 1-8, entered on May 14, 2026; and
  3. Request for admissions filed January 30, 2026, in support of Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted.

The Court also takes judicial notice of:

  1. Defendant’s Answer filed on November 14, 2025.

The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot be reasonably controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions admitted are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff. 

The Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgement in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $7,473.44, and $285.00 for costs, for a total judgment of $7,758.44.
