Tentative Ruling

Judge Kevin C. Brazile

Department 310

Hearing Date: September 9, 2026

Case Name: Kim v. Faal, et al.

Case No.: 21STCV14560

Matter: Default Judgment Application

Ruling: The Default Judgment Application is denied without prejudice.


The Default Judgment Application is denied without prejudice because Plaintiff did not provide forms CIV-100 and JUD-100. Plaintiff is also instructed to provide briefing within 15 days explaining the propriety of entering a default judgment against the Law Offices of Edi Faal when Edi Faal was already dismissed from this action.
