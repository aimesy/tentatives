415

MOTION 

Motion to Confirm Arbitration Award

MOVING PARTY

Defendant American Honda Motor Co., Inc.

OPPOSING PARTY

None

 

MOTIONS

            Defendant American Hondo Motor Co., Inc. (“Defendant”) moves to confirm the arbitration award it obtained on January 24, 2026 through an arbitration proceeding against Plaintiff Edgar Flores (“Plaintiff”).  The arbitrator ruled Plaintiff had not met his burden of proof to establish a breach of an express warranty, or any, claim and thereby found in favor of Defendant and determined Plaintiff was to take nothing.  (See Declaration of Stefanie G. Jo, Exhibit C (Final Arbitration Award.)  

On June 25, 2026, Plaintiff, through his Counsel, filed a notice of non-opposition to the instant motion.  (See Plaintiff’s Statement of Non-Opposition to Defendant’s Motion to Confirm the Arbitration Award.) 

Consequently, the Court grants the motion, as stipulated, and orders judgment to be entered pursuant to the terms of the arbitration award.

Defendant is ordered to provide notice of the Court’s ruling and to file proof of service of same.
