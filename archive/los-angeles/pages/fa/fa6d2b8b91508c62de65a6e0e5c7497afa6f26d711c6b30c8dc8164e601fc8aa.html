Case Number: 25NWLC56444    Hearing Date: September 21, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC56444: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs JAIME PEREIDA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A.’s ex parte application and the supporting declaration.

Plaintiff seeks either: (1) to have its motion for summary judgment heard on September 24, 2026, which is within 30 days of the current trial date of September 29, 2026, or (2) to continue the trial date so that the summary judgment motion can be heard. Plaintiff filed the motion for summary judgment on July 1, 2026, and noticed it for hearing on September 24, 2026, five days before trial. Plaintiff provides no explanation for selecting a hearing date within 30 days of trial or for waiting until days before the hearing and trial to seek ex parte relief. Any claimed exigency is self-created.

The ex parte application fails to comply with the California Rules of Court. Accordingly, the ex parte application is DENIED. The September 29, 2026 trial date remains on calendar.

Case Number: 25NWLC74176    Hearing Date: September 21, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC74176: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs JESUS A SERRANO, AN INDIVIDUAL

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated July 17, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Jesus A. Serrano (“Defendant”).

Pursuant to California Evidence Code sections 452 and 453, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s request for the Court to take judicial notice of:

  1. Plaintiff’s Complaint filed on December 10, 2025;
  2. The Court’s Order Deeming Plaintiff’s Requests for Admission as Admitted entered on July 16, 2026; and
  3. Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted, and the Requests for Admission submitted in support of that motion, filed on June 1, 2026, with the Amended Notice of Motion filed on June 24, 2025.

The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot reasonably be controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. and against Defendant Jesus A. Serrano for the principal sum of $7,939.89 plus costs in the amount of $285.00 for a total Judgment of $8,224.89.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 26NWLC39831    Hearing Date: September 21, 2026    Dept: Y

26NWLC39831: CAVALRY SPV I, LLC AS ASSIGNEE OF CAPITAL ONE, N.A. vs LAQUITO THOMAS

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered Defendant Laquito Thomas’s (“Defendant”) Motion to Quash Service of Summons (“Motion”) and the Opposition filed by Plaintiff Calvary SPV I, LLC (“Plaintiff”).  The Court has also reviewed the relevant Court records. 

Defendant specially appears and moves to quash service of summons pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 418.10.  Defendant argues that she was not properly served in compliance with the law.

Evidence Code § 647 creates a rebuttable presumption of valid service when, as here, a registered process service files a Proof of Service with the Court.  The individual questioning the validity of service must present sufficient evidence to demonstrate that service did not occur as stated in the Proof of Service.  Evid. Code §§ 647, 660.

The Court notes that Proof of Service of Summons by a registered process server by personal service was filed with the Court on July 13, 2026 creating a presumption of service under Evidence Code section 647.  The Proof of Service indicates personal service on July 13, 2026 at 4:38 p.m. at “368 W. 8th St., Apt 42, San Pedro, CA 90731-3722.”

Defendant’s declaration, which was signed under penalty of perjury, states that she was not home when the alleged service occurred; she did not speak with the process server through the call box as alleged by the process server; and she did not accept service of any documents or authorize them to be left at her door.  She states that she found the legal papers on July 15, 2026.   She has provided evidence that she was not in the State of California at the time of the alleged service.  The Driver’s Daily Log attached to her Motion demonstrates that she was in Las Vegas at the time of the alleged service on July 13, 2026.  The Court finds that Defendant has rebutted the presumption of personal service. 

Having considered the moving papers, Defendant’s Motion to Quash Service of Process is GRANTED. 

Clerk to give notice.
