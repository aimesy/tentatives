LEAVE TO FILE CROSS-COMPLAINT

 

MOVING PARTY: Defendant, Dr. Mitra Razipour Chiropractic Group, Inc. dba Natures Remedy Clinic

RESPONDING PARTY: Unopposed/Plaintiff, Jane DB Doe

 

RELIEF REQUESTED

Motion for Leave to File Cross-Complaint

 

SUMMARY OF ACTION

Plaintiff Jane DB Doe sued Defendant Dr. Mitra Razipour Chiropractic Group, Inc. dba Natures Remedy Clinic alleging she was sexually assaulted by a provider Defendant assigned to her. Plaintiff asserts claims for: (1) Sexual Assault; (2) Gender Violence; (3) Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress; (4) Sexual Harassment; (5) Negligence; (6) Negligent Supervision; (7) Negligent Hiring/Retention; and (8) Negligent Failure to Warn, Train, or Educate.  

 

RULING: Granted.

Defendant moves for leave to file a cross-complaint for indemnification against Hernan Bonilla, claiming that he is the alleged perpetrator of the assault on Plaintiff. Plaintiff does not oppose the motion. Defendant’s motion is granted.

 

Defendant is granted leave to file the cross-complaint. A defendant can cross-complain against a codefendant or a third party not yet a party to the action if the cause of action asserted: (1) “arises out of the same transaction, occurrence, or series of transactions or occurrences as the cause brought against him” or (2) “asserts a claim, right, or interest in the property or controversy which is the subject of the cause brought against him.” (Code Civ. Proc., § 428.10, subd. (b).) The indemnification claim arises out of the same alleged assault for which Plaintiff seeks to hold Defendant responsible. Defendant’s motion is therefore granted.

 

Defendant to separately file the cross-complaint within 10 days of this order.

 

Defendant to give notice.
