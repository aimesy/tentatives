Case Number: 25STCV08551    Hearing Date: July 7, 2026    Dept: 517

Pending before the Court is Defendant's motion for an order compelling Plaintiff to comply with the early deposition requirement of Code of Civil Procedure section 871.26. In response to the motion, Plaintiff's counsel filed a declaration stating that counsel has lost contact with Plaintiff so as to set up the early deposition.  Counsel requested a continuance of the hearing on the motion in order for counsel to reestablish contact with Plaintiff.  The Court will continue the hearing to July 31, 2026 at 8:30 a.m.  If Plaintiff's counsel has not reestablished contact with Plaintiff by July 27, 2026, counsel must file a motion to be relieved as counsel; otherwise, the Court will at the July 31, 2026 hearing grant Defendant's motion, including the motion's request for sanctions on Plaintiff's counsel due to the failure of Plaintiff to comply with the early deposition requirement.

Case Number: 25STCV25385    Hearing Date: July 7, 2026    Dept: 517

 Plaintiff Jane Doe sued Defendant Target Corporation and two individual Defendants for sexual harassment in the workplace, failure to prevent harassment, and retaliation in violation of the Fair Employment and Housing Act, and for whistleblower retaliation in violation of section 1102.5 of the Labor Code. Pending before the Court is Plaintiff's motion to proceed under a pseudonym.   The Court is denying the Plaintiff's motion.  The Court will set forth the basis for this ruling at the hearing on the motion.
