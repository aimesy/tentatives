ANALYSIS:

 

I.                Background

 

On February 17, 2026, Plaintiff Zhengfeng Bo (“Plaintiff”) filed the instant action against Defendant The Madison Collection LLC (“Defendant”) and Does 1-10, alleging causes of action for Account Stated, Breach of Written Contract, and Common Count. (Compl., p. 1.)  

 

On March 30, 2026, default was entered against Defendant.

 

On July 20, 2026, Defendant filed and served the instant Motion to Set Aside and/or Vacate Default Pursuant to Code Civil Procedure section 473 (the “Motion”).

 

To date, no Opposition has been filed.

 

II.              Procedural Issues

 

As an initial matter, the Court finds that the Motion and notice of hearing was untimely filed and served. Indeed, the Motion was electronically filed and served by email on July 20, 2026, which is less than 16 court days and two calendar days prior to the instant hearing date. (Code Civ. Proc., § 1005.)

 

Notably, there is no Opposition filed to the Motion.

 

The Court may not rule on the Motion absent proper service and notice of hearing.

 

Thus, the Motion is CONTINUED to allow Defendant to file proper proof of service and notice of hearing of the Motion.

 

III.            Conclusion & Order

 

For the foregoing reasons, Defendant The Madison Collection LLC’s unopposed Motion to Set Aside and/or Vacate Default Pursuant to Code Civil Procedure section 473 is CONTINUED to Thursday, September 10, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. in Department 512 of the Stanley Mosk Courthouse.

 

At least 16 court days prior to the next scheduled hearing, Defendant is to file and serve proper proof of service and notice of hearing of the Motion.

 

Moving party is ordered to give notice.
