Case Number: 19NWLC39774    Hearing Date: July 2, 2026    Dept: Y

19NWLC39774: PORTFOLIO RECOVERY ASSOCIATES, LLC vs SYLVIA RIVERA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Defendant Sylvia Rivera (“Defendant”), including the supporting declaration.   It is unclear whether Defendant’s Motion to Vacate Void Judgment (“Motion) has been served on Plaintiff Portfolio Recovery Associates, LLC (“Plaintiff”).  Defendant did not file a Proof of Service of the Motion on Plaintiff and no opposition was filed by Plaintiff.    

Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 requires that “all moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. . . . if the notice is served by mail, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by five calendar days if the place of mailing and place of address are within the State of California.”  It is unclear whether proper notice of the motion has been given.

The Court continues the hearing on the motion to August 3, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y so that proper notice of the motion can be given to Plaintiff.

Defendant is ordered to serve a copy of her Motion on counsel for Plaintiff and file Proof of Service.  To the extent the Motion was served on counsel for Plaintiff, Defendant may file a Proof of Service.

Case Number: 25NWLC00089    Hearing Date: July 2, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC00089: DISCOVER BANK vs CHRISTIAN FLORES

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Discover Bank (“Plaintiff”), the supporting declaration, and all relevant court records.  

Plaintiff requests to vacate the Default Judgment entered against Defendant Christian Flores (“Defendant”) on October 10, 2025 because it has brought to Plaintiff’s attention that Defendant may not have been properly served with the Summons and Complaint.  Plaintiff argues that in the interests of justice and pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473(b), the judgment should be set aside and vacated.  Plaintiff asks that the case be restored to active status.  

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473 (b), the Court may set aside any void judgment or order.  Code of Civil Procedure section 187 also grants jurisdiction to the Court to take appropriate action in the interests of justice.   

Based on those statutes, and the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Vacate Default Judgment.   Accordingly, the Default Judgment entered against Defendant on October 10, 2025 is vacated.  The Court, on its own motion, also sets aside and vacates the Writ of Execution issued by the Court on February 3, 2026.

The Court grants Defendant 20 days leave to file a responsive pleading.

The Court sets an Order to Show Cayse Re: Status of Case on January 7, 2027.  The Court expects that this date will be converted to the trial date after the responsive pleading is filed by Defendant.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC59538    Hearing Date: July 2, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC59538: LVNV FUNDING LLC vs ANAZIA JONES

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff LVNV Funding LLC (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated April 30, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Anazia Jones (“Defendant”).

Pursuant to California Evidence Code sections 452 and 453, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s request for the Court to take judicial notice of:

  1. Plaintiff’s Complaint filed on October 8, 2025;
  2. The Court’s Order Deeming Plaintiff’s Requests for Admission as Admitted entered on April 30, 2026; and
  3. Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted, and the Requests for Admission submitted in support of that motion, filed on May 8, 2026.

The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot reasonably be controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff and against Defendant Anazia Jones for the principal sum of $1,994.37 plus costs in the amount of $442.41, for a total Judgment of $2,436.78.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.
