DEPARTMENT 56 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

DEPARTMENT 56 JUDGE STEVEN A. ELLIS, LAW AND MOTION RULINGS. The court makes every effort to post tentative rulings by 5.00 pm of the court day before the hearing. The tentative ruling will not become the final ruling until the hearing [see CRC 3.1308(a)(2)], and are also available in the courtroom on the day of the hearing [see CRC 3.1308(b)]. If the parties wish to submit on the tentative ruling and avoid a court appearance, all counsel must submit on the Court’s tentative. Counsel submitting on the tentative, must email the department at [email protected] and state which party they represent.. If any party declines to submit on the tentative ruling, all parties should appear at the hearing in person or by LACourtConnect. Court reporters are not provided, and parties who want a record of motions and other proceedings must hire a privately retained certified court reporter.
