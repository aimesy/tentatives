DEPARTMENT 8 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26IWUD00649    Hearing Date: July 8, 2026    Dept: 8


The demurrer is overruled. The complaint states a cause of action for unlawful detainer based upon nonpayment of rent. (See Code Civ. Proc., § 1161(2). The Defendant's arguments concerning his tenancy cannot be resolved via this demurrer, as they rely upon facts beyond the face of the complaint of which the court cannot take judicial notice.  (County of Santa Clara v. Superior Court (2023) 14 Cal.5th 1034, 1041.) The Defendant is ordered to file an answer within five days of notice of this ruling.
