DEPARTMENT I LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

If the parties wish to submit on the tentative ruling and avoid a court appearance on the matter, the moving party must contact the opposing party and all other parties who have appeared in the action and confirm that each will submit on the tentative ruling. Please call the court no later than 4:30 p.m. on the court day before the hearing, leave a message with the court clerk at (310) 260-3629 advising her that all parties will submit on the tentative ruling and waive hearing, and finally, serve notice of the Court's ruling on all parties entitled to receive service. If any party declines to submit on the tentative ruling, then no telephone call is necessary, and all parties should appear at the hearing.

Case Number: 25SMCV00131    Hearing Date: June 30, 2026    Dept: I

This is a motion for leave to file a compulsory cross-complaint.  Plaintiff is defendant’s tenant and sues for various issues including claims that there was mold in the apartment and that plaintiff was eventually constructively evicted by virtue of the living conditions.  Defendant seeks to file a cross complaint, also relating to the tenancy.  The parties seem to agree that the cross complaint is compulsory.  The law on this favors granting the motion.  Leave to file a compulsory cross complaint must be granted unless the motion is brought in bad faith.  For example, where a cross complainant waited until the eve of trial and deliberately delayed in order to further delay the trial, that could be bad faith.  (County of Los Angeles v. Quinn Emmanuel Urquhart & Sullivan, LLP (2025) 115 Cal.App.5th 489.)  But a mere delay generally is not bad faith.  It might be neglect or the like, but that is not bad faith.  The court agrees with plaintiff here that this ought to have been brought sooner.  But that is not bad faith.  The court sees no tactical advantage from the delay here, and plaintiff argues none.  The trial is not until November 2027, so this will have no effect on that.  As such, the motion is GRANTED.  The cross complaint will be filed as a standalone document within 5 court days.  Cross defendant will have the time allowed by law to answer or otherwise respond.
