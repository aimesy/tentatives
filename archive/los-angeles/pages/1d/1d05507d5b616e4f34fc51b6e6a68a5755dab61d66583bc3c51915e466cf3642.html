Case Number: 25NWLC45364    Hearing Date: August 7, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC45364: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK, N.A. vs ANGELIKA TOTH

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed Defendant Angelika Toth’s (“Defendant”) Motion to Compel Arbitration (“Motion”) and supporting declaration and exhibits.  No opposition was filed by Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”).  The Court takes judicial notice of the Complaint filed on August 14, 2025 and the attached Cardmember Agreement.

Defendant has elected to resolve Plaintiff’s claims by binding arbitration and has made this request in his Answer and by filing this motion pursuant to the terms of the Cardmember Agreement.  The Cardmember Agreement states that “all disputes between you and Chase must be resolved by BINGIND ARBITRATION whenever you or we choose to submit or refer a dispute to arbitration.”  (Complaint, Agreement, pg. 10)  The Cardmember Agreement states that the party filing a Claim must select either Judicial Arbitration and Mediation Services (“JAMS”) or the American Arbitration Association (“AAA”) as the arbitration administrator.

Code of Civil Procedure section 1281.2 provides that a court shall order arbitration if it determines that an agreement to arbitrate exists and that a party to the agreement refuses to arbitrate.  Once arbitration is compelled, the court must stay the action pending the outcome of the arbitration pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 1281.4.

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure sections 1281.2 and 1281.4, Defendant's Motion to Compel Arbitration and Stay Proceedings is GRANTED.  The proceedings are stayed pending arbitration with AAA or JAMS as identified in the agreement. 

The Court advances and vacates the current trial date of August 20, 2026.  The Court also sets a Status Conference Re: Arbitration on February 8, 2027 at 8:30 a.m. in Department Y. 

If the parties have not initiated Arbitration by the date of the Status Conference Re: Arbitration, the Court will reset the trial date at the request of either party and find that the right to arbitrate has been waived.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC65913    Hearing Date: August 7, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC65913: CAPITAL ONE, N.A. SUCCESSOR BY MERGER TO DISCOVER BANK vs MELCHOR ARIZMENDI

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Capital One, N.A., the supporting declaration, and all relevant court records.  

In its motion, Plaintiff requests to vacate the Default Judgment entered by the Court on April 17, 2026 and dismiss the action without prejudice.   Plaintiff has presented evidence that Defendant Melchor Arizmendi (“Defendant”) has filed for bankruptcy. 

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473(d), the Court may set aside any void judgment or order.  Code of Civil Procedure section 128 also grants jurisdiction to the Court to take appropriate action in the interests of justice.

Based on the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Vacate Default Judgment.   Accordingly, the Default Judgment entered against Defendant on April 17, 2026 is vacated.

The Court further orders that the Complaint filed by Plaintiff and against Defendant is DISMISSED without prejudice.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC70729    Hearing Date: August 7, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC70729: DNF ASSOCIATES LLC vs RICARDO VAZQUEZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered Defendant Ricardo Vasquez’s (“Defendant”) Motion to Dismiss with Prejudice (“Motion”), which was filed on April 9, 2026 and set for hearing on August 7, 2026.   The Court has also reviewed Plaintiff DNF Associates, LLC’s (“Plaintiff”)  Opposition to Defendant’s Motion to Dismiss.  In addition, the Court notes that Defendant filed a Motion to Compel Arbitration, currently set for hearing on October 30, 2026.

The Court declines to rule on the Motion to Dismiss at this time. Plaintiff’s Complaint does not attach Synchrony’s Cardmember Agreement containing the arbitration terms, and neither party has submitted that agreement. The terms of the agreement may be relevant to the Motion to Dismiss.

The Court disagrees with Plaintiff’s assertion that “the forum question is therefore settled: arbitration is unavailable, and this action proceeds in this Court on its merits.” The forum question is not settled. Defendant has elected arbitration and filed a Motion to Compel Arbitration, set for hearing on October 30, 2026. The Court advances that hearing to September 11, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Department Y. Oppositions and replies shall be filed and served pursuant to the Code of Civil Procedure. Plaintiff is ordered to submit a copy of the subject agreement with its Opposition.

The Court notes that the letter attached by Defendant to the Motion to Dismiss states that “Claimant has filed a Demand for Arbitration.” The letter further states that “[b]efore the filing of this arbitration, DNF Associates, LLC failed to comply with the American Arbitration Association’s (AAA) policies regarding consumer claims, set forth in the Consumer Due Process Protocol and the Consumer Arbitration Rules, including the Costs of Arbitration, and/or removed themselves from the Consumer Clause Registry, which can be found on our website, www.adr.org/clauseregistry. Therefore, we must decline to administer the claim and any other claims between this business and its consumers at this time.”

The letter also states that the “AAA will follow any court order regarding how the arbitration should proceed.”

The hearing on Defendant’s Motion to Dismiss is continued to September 11, 2026, to be heard concurrently with Defendant’s Motion to Compel Arbitration. At that hearing, the Court will determine whether a further continuance is warranted or whether a ruling on the Motion to Dismiss is appropriate.

Plaintiff to give notice.
