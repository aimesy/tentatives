DEPARTMENT P LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26NWCV02419    Hearing Date: August 3, 2026    Dept: P


Ceja, et al. v. Salguero, 26NWCV02419

This motor vehicle-personal injury action was filed on July 21, 2026. Plaintiffs Salvador Rodríguez Ceja, Graciela Gonzalez Vargas, and Adriana Rodríguez Gonzalez (Plaintiffs) now apply ex parte for a nunc pro tunc order to backdate the filing date of their complaint. Plaintiffs move for relief pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473, subdivision (b) “based upon grounds of inadvertence, excusable neglect, or neglect of the third-party vendors and/or the plaintiff’s counsel, as explained in the attached Declaration of James A. Allaire, Esq.” (Ex Parte, 2:24-25.)

 

Plaintiffs’ ex parte application for a nunc pro tunc order is DENIED without prejudice. While Plaintiffs refer to a declaration by Plaintiffs’ counsel as the basis for their relief, no such declaration has been filed. Moving party to give notice.
