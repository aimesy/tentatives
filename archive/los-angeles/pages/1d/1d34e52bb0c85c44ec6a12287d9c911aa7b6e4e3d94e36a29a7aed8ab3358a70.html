ANALYSIS:

           

            Cross River Bank sued Richard Lang for breach of contract.  Lang answered.  The parties then settled and entered a stipulation that required Lang to pay $398.00 on February 26, 2026, make 22 monthly payments of $398.00 starting in March 2026, and to make a final payment of $396.00 in January 2028.  The Court dismissed this action without prejudice and retained jurisdiction pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 to enforce the stipulation.  Lang made payments totaling $796.00 but has failed to make any payments since April 2026.  Cross River therefore moves to set aside the dismissal and enter judgment against Lang pursuant to the stipulation.  Lang has not opposed the motion.

 

            Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6, subdivision (a), allows a court to enter judgment pursuant to the terms of a written, signed settlement.  If the parties agree, a court may dismiss the case without prejudice and retain jurisdiction to enforce the settlement until full performance is made. (Ibid.) 

 

            The stipulation between Cross River and Lang is valid and enforceable under Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6.  In the event of breach, the stipulation provides that Lang is liable for the principal sum of $17,939.19, plus $502.00 in court costs, minus any payments Lang made before breaching. 

 

            Cross River requests a judgment of $17,645.19 against Lang, reflecting $17,939.19 in principal, $502.00 in court costs, less $796.00 for payments received from Lang.  That amount is supported by Cross River’s motion, stipulation, and memorandum of costs.

 

            Accordingly, the Court GRANTS Cross River’s motion to set aside the dismissal entered on March 23, 2026, and enters judgment pursuant to the stipulation in the amount of $17,645.19 against Lang.

 

            Cross River is to give notice.
