DEPARTMENT F47 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

Counsel wishing to submit on a tentative ruling may inform the clerk or courtroom assisant in North Valley Department F47, 9425 Penfield Ave., Chatsworth, CA 91311, at (818) 407-2247.  Please be aware that unless all parties submit, the matter will still be called for hearing and may be argued by any appearing/non-submitting parties. If the matter is submitted on the court's tentative ruling by all parties, counsel for moving party shall give notice of ruling. This may be done by incorporating verbatim the court's tentative ruling. The tentative ruling may be extracted verbatim by copying and specially pasting, as unformatted text, from the Los Angeles Superior Court’s website, http://www.lasuperiorcourt.org. All hearings on law and motion and other calendar matters are generally NOT transcribed by a court reporter unless one is provided by the party(ies).

Case Number: 25CHCV01621    Hearing Date: September 16, 2026    Dept: F47

Dept. F47

Date: 9/16/26

Case #26CHCV01621

 

MOTION TO SET ASIDE DEFAULT

 

Demurrer filed on 5/18/26.

 

MOVING PARTY: Defendant Claudia Marie

RESPONDING PARTY: Plaintiff MU Holding Enterprises, Inc.

 

RULING: The motion is placed off calendar. 

 

On 4/22/26, Plaintiff MU Holding Enterprises, Inc. (Plaintiff) filed this unlawful detainer action against Defendants Michael Collins (Collins); Claudia Marie (Marie); Brett Mosloy (Mosloy); Gordon Leverett (Leverett); J. Erin (Erin); John Doe (John); Kim Doe (Kim); Brett Doe (Brett); and Jamie Doe (Jamie) regarding residential property located at 15023 Devonshire Street, Mission Hills, CA 91345.

 

On 5/18/26, Marie filed (served on 5/11/26) a demurrer to the complaint.  On 6/30/26, the Court overruled Marie’s demurrer and ordered that Marie had 10 days, excluding Saturdays and Sundays and other judicial holidays, plus 5 additional court days from the mailing of the notice of ruling by the court to file and serve an answer to the complaint.  (See 6/30/26 Minute Order).  On 6/30/26, the Court mailed notice of the ruling to Marie.  (See 6/30/26 Certificate of Mailing). 

 

Marie did not file an answer within the time allotted.  On 7/24/26, the Court entered Marie’s default.

 

On 7/27/26, Marie filed the instant motion to set aside default.  The motion was originally scheduled for hearing on 12/23/26.  On 7/28/26,  the Court granted Marie’s ex parte application to advance the hearing on the motion.  The hearing on the instant motion was set for 9/16/26.   (See 7/28/26 Minute Order).  The clerk gave notice of the ruling on the ex parte application on 7/28/26.  (See 7/28/26 Certificate of Mailing).

 

Although the Court gave notice of the ex parte ruling advancing the hearing on the motion from 12/23/26 to 9/16/26, there is no proof of service for the underlying motion.  No proof of service is attached to the motion and no proof of service for the motion has been separately filed.

 

Since there is no evidence that the underlying motion was ever served on Plaintiff and there is no opposition or other response to the motion to cure the defect in notice, the motion is placed off calendar.
