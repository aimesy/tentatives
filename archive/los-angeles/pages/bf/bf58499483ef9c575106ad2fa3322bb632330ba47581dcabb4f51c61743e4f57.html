CASE:                         American Express National Bank v. Eric Elizarraraz

 

CASE NO.:                 26STCV08873

 

 

DEFAULT JUDGMENT APPLICATION

 

MOVING PARTY:               Plaintiff American Express National Bank  

 

RESPONDING PARTY(S): None

 

PROOF OF SERVICE:

  • Correct Address: Yes
  • 16/21 (CCP § 1005(b)): OK. Request for Entry of Default and Request for Court Judgment form (CIV-100) mailed to Defendant on July 8, 2026.

 

CASE HISTORY:

  • 03/19/26: Complaint filed.
  • 05/14/26: Defendant served with, inter alia, the summons and the complaint via substituted service.
  • 07/09/26: Default entered against Defendant and default judgment application filed.

 

STATEMENT OF MATERIAL FACTS AND/OR PROCEEDINGS:

           

            This is a breach of contract action. On March 19, 2026, plaintiff American Express National Bank (“Plaintiff”) filed a complaint against defendants Eric Elizarraraz (“Defendant”) and DOES 1 to 10, inclusive, alleging a cause of action for Breach of Contract.

 

            Plaintiff alleges, inter alia, that “Plaintiff extended credit to Defendants for purchases and/or cash advances . . . [and] Defendant promised to repay Plaintiff for all charges, fees, and interest on the [a]ccount by paying the balance in full every month or by making monthly payments.” (Compl. at p. PLD-C-001(1).) Defendant is alleged to have failed to make payments owed on the account. (Id.) Plaintiff alleges damages in the sum of $63,938.64. (Compl. at p. 2.)

 

            On July 9, 2026, at the request of Plaintiff, default was entered against Defendant. Also, on such date, DOES 1 to 10, inclusive, were dismissed at the request of Plaintiff.

 

            Plaintiff now moves for the entry of default judgment against Defendant.

 

TENTATIVE RULING:

 

            Plaintiff’s default judgment application is GRANTED.
