DEPARTMENT 1 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS



Case Number: 26STCV01338    Hearing Date: August 18, 2026    Dept: 1

Before the Court is a motion by Albright, Yee & Schmit APC to be relieved as counsel for defendant Growth Management, Inc.  The motion is largely in order, except that counsel has not checked the box on the motion indicating confirmation “within the past 30 days that the [client’s] address is current” and, thus, was used by counsel to serve their client with the motion.  The Court will inquire at the hearing on the motion whether counsel had indeed confirmed within 30 days before filing the motion that the address at which the client was served was current, as well as whether counsel has received the return receipt for the mail service.  Provided that both of those things are so, the Court will grant the motion. 

Counsel’s proposed order is not filled out.  If the Court grants the motion, counsel is to fill out a proposed order completely and lodge it with the Court for signature.  The order will not become effective until proof of service of the order on the client is filed with the Court.  The November 2, 2026 status conference remains on calendar, and the Court will also issue an order to show cause to be considered at that hearing why Growth Management, Inc.’s answer should not be stricken for failure to retain new counsel.  Albright, Yee & Schmit APC to give notice of all of these matters and include them in the revised proposed order.  The Court sets a non-appearance case review for August 21, 2026 to review and sign the proposed order.
