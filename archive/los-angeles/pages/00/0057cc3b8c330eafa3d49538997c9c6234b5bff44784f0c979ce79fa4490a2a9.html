DEPARTMENT 517 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV17754    Hearing Date: July 13, 2026    Dept: 517

Defendant's motion for an order (1) compelling Plaintiff to provide responses to the request for production of documents ("RFPs") that Defendant propounded on Plaintiff, and (2) imposing monetary sanctions against Plaintiff, is denied in part and granted in part.

The motion is denied to the extent it sought an order compelling Plaintiff to respond to the RFPs. After Defendant filed the motion, Plaintiff provided Defendant with responses to the RFPs.  Defendant does not contest that Plaitniff's responses comply with the requirements of the Code of Civil Procedure.  At this stage, there is nothing to compel Plaintiff to provide.

The motion is granted to the extent that it sought an order imposing monetary sanctions against Plaintiff.   Plaintiff's responses to the RFPs were untimely.  Plaintiff did not respond until after Defendant filed the motion.  Plaintiff has failed to show substantial justification for delaying that long before providing the responses to the RFPs. Nor under the circumstances does the Court discern any injustice that would result from imposing monetary sanctions against Plaintiff.  The Court is awarding Defendant $918, which, per the motion, reflects the amount of the attorney's fees that Defendant incurred in having to file the motion to force Plaintiff to provide responses to the RFPs.  The sanctions are payable to Defendant within 30 days of this order.

At the case management conference that is on calendar on July 13, 2026, the Court will inquire of Plaintiff's counsel whether Plaintiff is still pursuing a claim against the "Doe" manager of Defendant, who has yet to be served with the summons and complaint in this action.

Case Number: 25STCV35158    Hearing Date: July 13, 2026    Dept: 517

Defendant 1032 N. Sycamore Owner (LA) LLC ("1032") has filed an ex parte application to stay the civil action that Plaintiff Zeus Realty Group LLC ("Zeus") brought against it pending resolution of the unlawful detainer action that 1032 brought against Zeus.  The unlawful detainer action has been stalled because of the inability of 1032 to effectuate valid service on Zeus of the summons and complaint in that action.  Today, in the unlawful detainer action, the Court is denying Zeus's motions to quash service on Zeus that 1032 says it effectuated through two different means.  With the denial of those motions, the Court has concluded that 1032's service of the summons and complaint in the unlawful detainer action is valid.  The Court is thus granting 1032's ex parte application to stay the civil action pending resolution of the unlawful detainer action. 

The motion for protective order, motion to compel further responses to discovery, and motion to bifurcate that Zeus filed in the civil action and that are fully briefed are continued to August 7, 2026.  The civil action is on calendar that day for discovery motions that 1032 filed.  Those other discovery motions will not be heard that day. 

By August 7, 2026, the Court and parties likely will know if the unlawful detainer action is proceeding apace or whether, a per a demurrer to it that Zeus filed and is set for hearing on July 29, 2026, that action has been dismissed or stalled again.  If the unlawful detainer action proceeds past that demurrer, then the Court will discuss with counsel a trial date and a schedule for discovery in that action.   

Zeus's own ex parte application that is on calendar today in the civil action is denied without prejudice in light of the Court's decision to grant 1032's ex parte applicaiton to stay the civil action pending resolution of the unlawful detainer action. 

Case Number: 25STCV35945    Hearing Date: July 13, 2026    Dept: 517


Pending before the Court are two motions that Defendant Zeus Realty Group LLC ("Zeus") to quash Plaintiff 1032 N. Sycamore Owner (LA) LLC's ("1032") service of the summons and complaint on Zeus in this unlawful detainer action. 

The first-filed motion is directed at service that 1032 said was made on Zeus's managing member Patrizio Moi ("Moi") on a street by one of 1032's attorneys, Michael Shakouri ("Shakouri").  The Court is denying this motion.  The Court has concluded that Shakouri's service on Moi was fully effectuated and was valid.  The Court will explain the bases for this conclusion at the hearing.  In reaching the conclusion, the Court considered and is overruling Zeus's objections to the declaration that Skakouri submitted in support of 1032's opposition to the motion.

The second-filed motion is directed at the service of the summons and complaint on Zeus by posting and mailing that 1032 says it carried out following the Court's June 16, 2026 authorizing service via this method under Code of Civil Procedure section 415.45.  The Court is denying this motion as well.  For the reasons that the Court will set forth at the hearing, the Court has concluded that 1032's service by posting and mailing complied with section 415.45.

The Court will continue the case management conference in the case to July 29, 2006, which is the date of Zeus's demurrer.
