Case Number: 24AHCV00309    Hearing Date: August 11, 2026    Dept: X

#14 - JENNIFER WILLIAMS vs CITY OF PASADENA, et al.

On its own motion, the August 13, 2026 Trial Setting Conference is continued to September 17, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. in Department X.

MOTION FOR PROTECTIVE ORDER

Defendant’s motion is granted in part and denied in part.

Ms. Gonzales’s deposition shall not be taken before November 17, 2026.  Within 10 calendar days of this order, counsel shall meet and confer by telephone or videoconference regarding available dates.  The deposition shall proceed remotely by videoconference unless all parties stipulate otherwise.  The first session of the deposition shall be limited to three hours of on-the-record examination, with reasonable breaks at least every 45 minutes.  If additional time is reasonably necessary, the parties shall meet and confer regarding a second remote session, and any unresolved dispute may be presented by informal discovery request or noticed motion.

Absent further order, the deposition shall be completed no later than January 15, 2027.

If CSC seeks any further continuance of the deposition, the application must be supported by competent evidence specifically addressing Ms. Gonzales’s ability to sit for a remote deposition and the anticipated duration of any inability.

The motion is granted without prejudice to Plaintiff seeking a continuance of Defendant's September 17, 2026 Motion for Summary Judgment pursuant to a showing sufficient under Code of Civil Procedure section 437c.

Sanctions

Plaintiff’s opposition requests sanctions.

The motion was not entirely without justification, because Ms. Gonzales did make a sworn showing of present hardship, and the Court had expressly invited a supported application for protective relief in its June 24, 2026 order. At the same time, CSC did not establish entitlement to the full stay requested.

Both sides prevailed in part, and the circumstances make a sanctions award unjust. Accordingly, the Court finds sanctions unwarranted on this record and denies Plaintiff’s request for sanctions.

Unless waived by the parties, Moving Party is to give notice.

#15 - GUOJIE ZHAO, et al. vs ROBERT CHIEN

The motion is GRANTED IN PART and DENIED IN PART.  The motion seeks two distinct forms of relief: a trial continuance and a wholesale reset of discovery and related deadlines. The first request is supported in part by the medical showing. The second is not. A limited continuance best addresses the health showing without rewarding the insufficiently supported request to restart all pretrial deadlines.

The trial date of September 15, 2026, is advanced and vacated.  The parties are to meet and confer and submit a stipulation proposing three agreed-to trial dates (trials commence on Tuesdays) prior to April 2027.  The Court will sign a proposed order selecting the trial date.

Defendant’s request to reset or reopen all discovery, expert, and law-and-motion deadlines as though the new trial date were the initial trial date is DENIED.

The Court sets an Order to Show Cause re: Submission of Stipulation for Trial for September 15, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. in Department X.

Unless waived by the parties, Defendant is to give notice.

Case Number: 25NNCV04389    Hearing Date: August 11, 2026    Dept: X

#17 - JESUS PALOMINO, et al. vs KIA AMERICA, INC.

Motion to Compel Plaintiffs’ Depositions

The motion is denied as moot as to plaintiff Rebeka Palomino because the record states that she already appeared for deposition on July 27, 2026.

As to plaintiff Jesus Palomino, the record submitted with the opposition states that the parties agreed to a July 31, 2026 deposition date and that the deposition remained on calendar as of July 29, 2026.  To the extent the deposition was completed as agreed, no further order compelling plaintiff Jesus Palomino’s deposition is necessary.  The motion is, therefore, denied as moot.

The request for sanctions is denied.

Motion to Compel Vehicle Inspection

Unlike the deposition motion, the vehicle inspection had not yet occurred as of the filing of the opposition, although the parties had agreed on an August 24, 2026 inspection date and defendant had already served a Second Amended Demand setting that date, time, and place.

The Court grants the motion in part for the limited purpose of enforcing the agreed inspection. Plaintiffs shall produce the subject vehicle, identified as the 2023 Kia Soul bearing VIN KNDJ53AU6P7884620, for inspection and non-destructive testing on August 24, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. at Van Nuys Kia, 6115 Van Nuys Blvd., Van Nuys, California 91401, consistent with defendant’s Second Amended Demand for Production of Vehicle for Inspection and Testing, No. 1.

The request for sanctions is denied. Plaintiffs represented an intent to produce the vehicle on the noticed August 24, 2026 date and the scheduling issue was resolved after further communications between parties.

Unless waived by the parties, Defendant is to give notice.
