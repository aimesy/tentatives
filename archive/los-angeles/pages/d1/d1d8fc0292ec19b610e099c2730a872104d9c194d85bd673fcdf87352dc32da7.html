Case Number: 24STLC06882    Hearing Date: August 11, 2026    Dept: 25

PROCEEDINGS:      MOTION FOR AWARD OF ATTORNEYS’ FEES TO BE INCLUDED IN JUDGMENT

 

MOVING PARTY:   Plaintiff CMM, LLP

RESP. PARTY:         None

 

MOTION FOR ATTORNEY FEES

(Civ. Code § 1717; Code Civ. Proc., §§ 1032, 1033.5)

 

TENTATIVE RULING:

 

The Court GRANTS CMM’s motion for attorney fees in the reduced amount of $6,280.00.

 

CMM is to give notice.  

 

SERVICE:

[X] Correct Address (CCP §§ 1013, 1013a)                                      OK

[X] 16/21 Court Days Lapsed (CCP §§ 12c, 1005(b))                       OK

 

OPPOSITION:          None filed as of August 6, 2026                     [   ] Late                      [X] None

REPLY:                     None filed as of August 6, 2026                     [   ] Late                      [X] None

 

ANALYSIS:

 

            CMM, LLP won an uncontested trial against Eric E. Jensen.  It now moves for attorney fees pursuant to Civil Code section 1717. 

 

The Court’s objective is to award those fees at the fair-market rate for the attorney’s work. (Ketchum v. Moses (2001) 24 Cal.4th 1122, 1132.)  That can be done by multiplying the number of hours the attorney reasonably expended by a reasonable hourly rate.  (Id. at pp. 1132, 1134.)  CMM seeks attorney fees based on its counsel’s hourly rate of $400.00, which the Court finds reasonable for an attorney of his experience.  Likewise, the expenditure of 13.7 hours of work to see an action through trial—even an uncontested trial—is reasonable.  Multiplying 13.7 hours by $400 per hour yields a fee award of $5,480.

 

CMM also requests attorney fees for this motion.  Because the fee motion was unopposed the Court reduces the hours requested from 4 to 2, and adds an additional $800 to the fee award.

 

The Court therefore GRANTS CMM’s motion for attorney fees in the reduced amount of $6,280.00.

 

CMM is to give notice.


Case Number: 25STLC00332    Hearing Date: August 11, 2026    Dept: 25

HEARING DATE:    Tues., August 11, 2026                                   JUDGE /DEPT:            Eisenman/25

CASE NUMBER:      25STLC00332

PROCEEDINGS:      MOTION TO VACATE ORDER OF DISMISSAL

 

MOVING PARTY:   Plaintiff Emmanuel Beltran Ponce

RESP. PARTY:         None

 

MOTION TO VACATE DISMISSAL

(Code Civ. Proc., § 473(b))

 

TENTATIVE RULING:                                                                       

 

The Court GRANTS Emmanuel Beltran Ponce’s motion to vacate the court-ordered dismissal for failure to appear at trial on July 16, 2026.

 

The July 16, 2026 dismissal is hereby SET ASIDE AND VACATED.

 

            Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473, subdivision (c)(1)(A), the Court imposes a penalty of $500.00 on Ponce’s counsel, payable to the Court within 30 days of this order.

 

            Ponce is to give notice.

 

SERVICE: 

 

[X] Proof of Service Timely Filed (CRC, rule 3.1300)                     OK

[X] Correct Address (CCP §§ 1013, 1013a)                                      OK

[X] 16/21 Court Days Lapsed (CCP §§ 12c, 1005(b))                       OK

 

OPPOSITION:          None filed as of August 6, 2026                     [   ] Late                      [X] None

REPLY:                     None filed as of August 6, 2026                     [   ] Late                      [X] None

 

ANALYSIS:

 

Emmanuel Beltran Ponce sued Linda Sue Durham in January 2025.  He never filed proof of service on Durham.  (Contra Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.110(b).)  And then his counsel failed to appear at the July 16, 2026 trial of his action.  The Court dismissed Ponce’s complaint without prejudice pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 581, subdivision (b)(3).  Ponce now moves to have the dismissal vacated pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473, subdivision (b)’s mandatory attorney-fault provision. 

 

            A motion under that provision must be (1) timely, (2) in proper form, and (3) accompanied by an attorney’s sworn affidavit attesting to the attorney’s mistake, inadvertence, surprise, or neglect.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 473, subd. (b).)  A court must grant relief if the party satisfies those requirements, unless the court finds that the default or dismissal was not actually caused by the attorney’s fault.  (Ibid.)

 

Ponce’s motion, filed the same day as the dismissal, satisfies each of those requirements.  His counsel declares that the failure to appear was not Ponce’s fault but the result of counsel’s “sole mistake and clerical neglect.”  Ponce’s counsel further attests that his “firm’s recent and comprehensive transition to a digitized, cloud-based data management system caused . . . the deadlines for service on [Durham] and the hearing date for the trial [to be] inadvertently omitted from the digital tracking system.”  Just two weeks ago, the same attorney offered the same excuse for his failure to appear at trial in another matter—Richardson v. Lewis (24STLC09462)—and when he did it then, it wasn’t the first time he’d done so in recent memory.

 

Nevertheless, mandatory is mandatory.  The Court GRANTS Ponce’s motion.  The July 16, 2026 dismissal is hereby SET ASIDE AND VACATED.

 

            Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473, subdivision (c)(1)(A), the Court imposes a penalty of $500.00 on Ponce’s attorney, Eugene Fu, payable to the Court within 30 days of this order.

 

            Additionally, pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473, subdivision (c)(1)(C), the Court ORDERS Eugene Fu personally to review the calendar in every single pending action in which he represents a client before Department 25 of this Court.  Within 30 days of this order, Fu is to file a declaration in this action (1) listing each of those actions by name and case number, and (2) attesting under penalty of perjury that he has ensured his calendar accurately reflects all upcoming court dates in those matters.

 

            Because no proof of service has been timely filed in this action, the Court further ORDERS Ponce to SHOW CAUSE, on Thursday, September 10, 2026 at 9:30 a.m. in Department 25 of the Spring Street Courthouse, why he has failed to file proof of service on the defendant.  The Court also ORDERS Ponce to SHOW CAUSE, at the same date, time, and place, why his counsel should not be sanctioned in an amount up to $250.00 for failure timely to file proof of service in this matter.

 

            Ponce is to give notice.
