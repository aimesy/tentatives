Case Number: 24STCV25392    Hearing Date: September 4, 2026    Dept: 514

The three motions to compel initial responses and the motion for a deemed-admitted order are denied.

Plaintiff served responses to the discovery at issue before these motions were filed.  If Plaintiff's objections are without merit (as Defendant contends), Defendant's remedy is to file a timely motion to compel further responses (after meeting and conferring in person, by telephone, or video conference, as required by statute). 

Moving party is ordered to give notice.
