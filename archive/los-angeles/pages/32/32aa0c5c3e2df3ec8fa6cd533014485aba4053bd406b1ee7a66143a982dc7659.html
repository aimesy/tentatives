Case Number: 23NWCV01916    Hearing Date: August 14, 2026    Dept: P

MONROY-ROMAN, ET AL. v. GENERO, ET AL.

CASE NO.: 23NWCV01916

HEARING: 08/14/2026 @ 9:30 AM

 

#7

TENTATIVE ORDER

 

Plaintiffs Noemi Monroy-Roman and Yeizon Rodriguez’s motion to compel Defendant Auto Company XXIII dba Autonation Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram Valencia to produce its person most qualified is taken OFF-CALENDAR as MOOT.

 

Moving party to give notice.

 

Plaintiffs Noemi Monroy-Roman and Yeizon Rodriguez (Plaintiffs) move to compel Defendant Auto Company XXIII dba Autonation Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram Valencia (Defendant) to produce a person most qualified (PMQ) for deposition.

 

Background

 

Plaintiffs filed this action against Defendants Rosita Genero and Diego Genero on June 22, 2023 and Does 1 to 50. The complaint alleges that on or about October 21, 2022, Plaintiffs were travelling Northbound on I-710 approaching Frontage Road and Miller Way when Defendant Rosita Genero, driving behind Plaintiffs, struck Plaintiffs’ vehicle from behind, resulting in injuries. (Complaint, ¶¶ 8-9.) The complaint asserts two causes of action against all defendants: (1) negligence and (2) negligence per se.

 

On December 6, 2024, Plaintiffs requested dismissal without prejudice as to Defendant Diego Genero. On the same date, Defendant Diego Genero was dismissed without prejudice.

 

On May 5, 2025, Plaintiffs filed two Amendments to Complaint replacing Doe 1 with Autonation.com, Inc and Doe 2 with Autonation, Inc.

 

Discussion

 

Plaintiffs move pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 2025.480 to compel Defendant to produce its PMQ for deposition.

 

This is a continuation. On February 3, 2026, this motion first came on for hearing. The Court continued the hearing on this motion based on an insufficient meet and confer process and resulting uncertainty as to which issues were remaining. (Order, 2/3/2026.) The Court recognized that the parties themselves recognized the need to further meet and confer on this issue:

 

Counsel for Defendant states that the deposition of Defendant’s PMQ proceeded as to the categories for which the PMQ was produced, and then, rather than ask the PMQ about other categories, Plaintiffs’ counsel “cancelled the deposition and the Parties agreed to meet and confer on the categories of examination and the issues surrounding the deposition.” (Crosby Decl., ¶¶ 7-9.)

 

(Order, 2/3/2026.) Acknowledging the need to meet and confer, the Court continued the hearing to today’s date and stated:

 

The parties are ORDERED to meet and confer. The Joint Separate Statement must be filed on or before February 18, 2026. The Joint Separate Statement shall the Court on the remaining issues. Any supplemental opposition is due by February 24, 2026 and any supplemental reply is due by March 2, 2026. If there is no Joint Separate Statement filed by February 18, 2026, the Court will presume the issues in these motions are moot.

 

(Order, 2/3/2026.)

 

There was no separate statement filed by February 18, 2026. Also, there were no supplemental oppositions filed by February 24, 2026 nor supplemental replies filed as of March 2, 2026. Pursuant to the Court’s February 3, 2026 Order, the Court presumes the issues raised in this motion are moot.  

 

Accordingly, Plaintiffs’ motion to compel Defendant to produce its person most qualified is taken OFF-CALENDAR as moot.

 

 


Case Number: 23NWCV03670    Hearing Date: August 14, 2026    Dept: P

LOURDES BELTRAN v. CARLOS OCHOA

CASE NO.: 23NWCV03670 

HEARING: 08/14/2026 @ 9:30 AM

 

#8

TENTATIVE ORDER

 

Counsel LA Century Law and their attorneys of record’s motion to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff Lourdes Beltran is GRANTED. This Order will take effect upon the filing of proof of service of the signed order upon the client and on all parties that have appeared in the case.

 

 

Moving party to give notice.

 

Counsel LA Century Law and their attorneys of record (Counsel) moves to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff Lourdes Beltran (Plaintiff).

 

Background

 

On November 13, 2023, Plaintiff Lourdes Beltran (Plaintiff) filed this action against Defendant Carlos Ochoa and Does 1 to 25. On January 22, 2025, Plaintiff filed the operative FAC against Carlos Ochoa, Emily Mercado, Richard Mercado, and Does 1 to 25 (collectively, Defendants).

 

This action arises out of a dog bite. The FAC alleges that on April 27, 2022, Plaintiff was at 5225 Wood Avenue, South Gate, California 90280, to deliver food to Defendants. (FAC, ¶¶ 9-11.) While on the property, a dog owned by Defendants (Dog) “charged Plaintiff and bit him” resulting in injuries to Plaintiff. (FAC, ¶¶ 12-13.) The FAC asserts two causes of action: (1) strict liability and (2) negligence.

 

On July 13, 2026, the Court granted Defendant Carlos Ochoa’s motion for summary judgment as to the FAC.

 

On July 31, 2026, the Court denied Counsel’s ex parte application to advance the instant motion. The Court granted Counsel’s alternative request, to continue the trial date to October 26, 2026.

 

Legal Standard

 

“The attorney in an action or special proceeding may be changed at any time before or after judgment or final determination, as follows: ¶ 1. Upon the consent of both client and attorney, filed with the clerk, or entered upon the minutes; ¶ 2. Upon the order of the court, upon the application of either client or attorney, after notice from one to the other.” (Code Civ. Proc., § 284.)

 

California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362 requires that the following be submitted in support of an attorney’s motion to be relieved as counsel pursuant Code of Civil

Procedure section 284, subd. (2): (1) a notice of motion and motion directed to the client (Form MC-051); (2) a declaration stating in general terms and without compromising the confidentiality of the attorney-client relationship why a motion under Code of Civil Procedure section 284, subd. (2) is brought instead of filing a consent under Code of Civil Procedure section 284, subd. (1) (Form MC-052); (3) a proof of service evidencing service of the notice of motion and motion, declaration, and proposed order on the client and on all other parties who have appeared in the case; and (4) a proposed order relieving counsel (Form MC-053). (Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.1362, subds. (a), (c), (d), (e).)

 

Discussion

 

Counsel moves to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff.

 

Here, in compliance with California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362, Counsel submits a notice of motion and motion on Form MC-051 directed to Plaintiff. Counsel also submits a declaration on Form MC-052 demonstrating good cause to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff. Specifically, Counsel states that there has been a breakdown of the attorney-client relationship and Plaintiff has advised Counsel that she no longer wishes to be represented by their firm. Counsel provides a proposed order on Form MC-053. Counsel attests to serving Plaintiff via mail with the moving papers after confirming Plaintiff’s address within the last thirty days. Therefore, the Court finds that Counsel has complied with the requirements under California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362.

 

Accordingly, Counsel’s motion to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff is GRANTED. This Order will take effect upon the filing of proof of service of the signed order upon the client and on all parties that have appeared in the case.

Moving party to give notice.

 

Counsel Benjamine Sasounian (Counsel) moves to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff Destiny Clisby (Plaintiff).

 

Background

 

On February 27, 2026, Plaintiff filed this premises liability action against Defendants Big Fun Foods, Jack in the Box Inc., and Does 1 through 50. This action arises out of injuries Plaintiff sustained on March 2, 2024, after slipping and falling on a liquid in a restroom at the restaurant located at 5747 Lakewood Blvd, in the City of Lakewood, County of Los Angeles, State of California. (Complaint, ¶¶ 8-11.) The complaint asserts two causes of action: (1) premises liable and (2) general negligence.

 

Legal Standard

 

“The attorney in an action or special proceeding may be changed at any time before or after judgment or final determination, as follows: ¶ 1. Upon the consent of both client and attorney, filed with the clerk, or entered upon the minutes; ¶ 2. Upon the order of the court, upon the application of either client or attorney, after notice from one to the other.” (Code Civ. Proc., § 284.)

 

California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362 requires that the following be submitted in support of an attorney’s motion to be relieved as counsel pursuant Code of Civil

Procedure section 284, subd. (2): (1) a notice of motion and motion directed to the client (Form MC-051); (2) a declaration stating in general terms and without compromising the confidentiality of the attorney-client relationship why a motion under Code of Civil Procedure section 284, subd. (2) is brought instead of filing a consent under Code of Civil Procedure section 284, subd. (1) (Form MC-052); (3) a proof of service evidencing service of the notice of motion and motion, declaration, and proposed order on the client and on all other parties who have appeared in the case; and (4) a proposed order relieving counsel (Form MC-053). (Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.1362, subds. (a), (c), (d), (e).)

 

Discussion

 

Counsel moves to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff.

 

Here, in compliance with California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362, Counsel submits a notice of motion and motion on Form MC-051 directed to Plaintiff. Counsel also submits a declaration on Form MC-052 demonstrating good cause to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff. Specifically, Counsel states that there has been a breakdown of the attorney-client relationship and communication. Counsel provides a proposed order on Form MC-053 and proof of service of each document to the parties in this action. Therefore, the Court finds that Counsel has fully complied with the requirements under California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362.

 

Accordingly, Counsel’s motion to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff Destiny Clisby is GRANTED. This Order will take effect upon the filing of proof of service of the signed order upon the client and on all parties that have appeared in the case.
