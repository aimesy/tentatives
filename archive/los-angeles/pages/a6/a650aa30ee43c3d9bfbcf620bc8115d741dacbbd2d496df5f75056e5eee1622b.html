DEPARTMENT Z LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25NWLC57800    Hearing Date: July 14, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on April 22, 2026.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed to Defendant a meet and confer letter on May 28, 2026.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED as to RFA's #1-9.
