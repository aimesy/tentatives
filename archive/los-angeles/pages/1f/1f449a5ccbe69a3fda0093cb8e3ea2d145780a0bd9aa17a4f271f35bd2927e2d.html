Defendants Janus Development, LLC, Habitat Partners Inc., and Joshua Krom seek to deem their RFAs served on Plaintiff admitted.

Plaintiff appears willing to provide responses. Therefore, in the exercise of the Court’s discretion, the Motion to Deem Admitted is denied, but Plainitff is compelled to provide initial responses, without objections, within 20 days.

Plaintiff is sanctioned $585, payable within 30 days.

Case Number: 24STCV10544    Hearing Date: July 22, 2026    Dept: 310

Tentative Ruling

Judge Kevin C. Brazile

Department 310

Hearing Date: July 22, 2026

Case Name: Avalos v. USC, et al.

Case No.: 24STCV10544

Matter: Motions to Compel Further Responses (2x)

Ruling: The Motions are denied.


Moving party to give notice.


The Court encourages all parties to appear remotely via LA CourtConnect.  If submitting on the Court's tentative ruling, please follow the instructions provided above.


On October 8, 2024, Plaintiff Oswaldo Hasbun Avalos filed the operative First Amended Complaint (“FAC”) against Defendants University of Southern California (“USC”), LA County, and Pacific Healthworks, LLC for (1) whistleblower retaliation, (2) FEHA retaliation, (3) FEHA harassment, and (4) FEHA failure to prevent retaliation and harassment.

Defendant USC now seeks to compel further responses to its Requests for Admission and Requests for Production from Plaintiff Oswaldo Avalos.

Because Plaintiff served supplemental responses, the Motions are denied as moot. While USC contends the responses are again deficient, the proper course is to further meet and confer as it seems that process never ended.

No sanctions at this time.

Moving party to give notice.

Tentative Ruling

Judge Kevin C. Brazile

Department 310

Hearing Date: July 22, 2026

Case Name: Ayala v. VW Group of America, Inc., et al.

Case No.: 25STCV33963

Matter: (1) Motions to Compel (2x)

(2) Motion to Deem Admitted

Moving Party: Defendant VW Group of America, Inc.

Responding Party: Unopposed

Notice: OK

Ruling: The Motions are granted.


Moving party to give notice.


The Court encourages all parties to appear remotely via LA CourtConnect.  If submitting on the Court's tentative ruling, please follow the instructions provided above.


Defendant VW Group of America, Inc. now seeks to compel initial responses to its special interrogatories and requests for production from Plaintiffs Daniela A. Ayala Garcia and Nemecio Ayala. Defendant also seeks to deem its Requests for Admission (RFAs) admitted.

Because there is no opposition, the Motions are granted. The RFAs are deemed admitted. Responses, without objections, are to be served for the special interrogatories and requests for production within 30 days.

Plaintiffs are sanctioned $1000, payable within 30 days.

Moving party to give notice.
