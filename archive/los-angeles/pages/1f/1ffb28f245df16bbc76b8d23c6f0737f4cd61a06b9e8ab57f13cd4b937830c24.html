DEPARTMENT C LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26NWCV01029    Hearing Date: June 24, 2026    Dept: C

GUSTAVO GONZALEZ WILLER vs BENITO GARCIA, Case No. 26NWCV01029

Plaintiff Gustavo Gonzalez Willer moves for Nunc Pro Tunc Order to Deem Plaintiff’s Summons and Complaint filed on March 19, 2026.  Counsel for Plaintiff states that counsel electronically filed the Summons, Complaint and Civil Case Cover Sheet on March 190, 2026. (Avila Decl., ¶2.) The clerk rejected the filing because it was submitted to the Stanley Mosk Courthouse, which was the incorrect location. (Id., ¶3.) On March 20, 2026, counsel resubmitted the documents to the Norwalk Courthouse, which accepted the filing. (Id., ¶¶4-5.) The initial submission on March 19 was within the applicable statute of limitations, but the filing on March 20 occurred after the statute of limitations had expired. (Id., ¶6.) Plaintiff asserts that the Summons and Complaint should be deemed filed on March 19, 2026 because the clerk improperly rejected the documents for filing on that date.  CCP § 402, subd. (a)(3) states: “A superior court may not dismiss a case, and the clerk may not reject a case for filing, because it is filed, or a person seeks to file it, in a court location other than the location specified by local rule. However, the court may transfer the case on its own motion to the proper court location.”  The motion is GRANTED.  The Summons and Complaint are deemed to have been filed on March 19, 2026 instead of March 20, 2026.  Notice is not required.
