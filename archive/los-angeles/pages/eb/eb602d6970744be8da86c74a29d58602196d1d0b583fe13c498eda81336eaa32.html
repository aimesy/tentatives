Case Number: 25NWLC51298    Hearing Date: September 4, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC51298: KINECTA FEDERAL CREDIT UNION vs JARLENE MARQUEZ, et al.

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered Plaintiff Kinecta Federal Credit Union’s (“Plaintiff”) Motion for Summary Judgment, or in the alternative, Summary Adjudication of Issues (“Motion”), which was filed on May 19, 2026.  The Court has also read and considered the Opposition of Defendants Jarlene Marquez and Ricardo Marquez (“Defendants”), which was filed on August 4, 2026.  No reply has been filed by Plaintiff.

A motion for summary judgment under Code of Civil Procedure section 437c(a)(3) must be heard no later than 30 days before trial, unless the court orders otherwise for good cause.  The trial in this case is scheduled on September 15, 2026 – 11 days after the date of the hearing for the motion for summary judgment.  Plaintiff did not request a continuance of the trial date or leave of the Court to schedule its motion for summary judgment within the 30-day period before trial.  Accordingly, Plaintiff’s Motion is denied without prejudice. 

The trial date of September 15, 2026 remains on calendar.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC63842    Hearing Date: September 4, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC63842: CAPITAL ONE, N.A. vs SAMORIA MARTIN

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Defendant Samoria Martin (“Defendant”), including the supporting declaration.   Defendant filed and served its Motion on August 12, 2026 along with a Proof of Service by mail on Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”).  No opposition has been filed by Plaintiff.   

Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 requires that “all moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. . . . if the notice is served by mail, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by five calendar days if the place of mailing and place of address are within the State of California.”   

For proper notice to have been given by Defendant, this motion should have been served no later than August 8, 2026 for a hearing date on September 4, 2026.  The Court continues the hearing on the Motion to September 24, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y so that Plaintiff has sufficient time to file an opposition if necessary.

Clerk to give notice. 

Case Number: 26NWLC28451    Hearing Date: September 4, 2026    Dept: Y

26NWLC28451: TOYOTA MOTOR CREDIT CORPORATION vs JORGE BANDA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Toyota Motor Credit Corporation (“Plaintiff”), and the supporting attorney declaration.  Plaintiff’s Motion to Set Aside and Vacate Dismissal(“Motion”) has been served on Defendant Jorge Banda and no opposition has been filed.  

In its Motion, Plaintiff requests to vacate and set aside the dismissal entered on May 28, 2026 and restore the case to active status.   Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 473(b), the Court has authority to relieve, on such terms as are just, a party or party’s legal representative from a judgment taken against the party because of mistake, inadvertence, surprise and/or excusable neglect.   Here, Plaintiff’s counsel has presented evidence that the dismissal was entered through Plaintiff’s counsel’s mistake.

Based on the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Set Aside and Vacate Dismissal.  This case is returned to active status.

Plaintiff to give notice.
