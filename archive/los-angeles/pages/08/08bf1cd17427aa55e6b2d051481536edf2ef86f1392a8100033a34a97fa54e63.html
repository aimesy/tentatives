DEPARTMENT Z LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25NWLC44396    Hearing Date: June 24, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court finds Plaintiff has met the initial burden of showing that there is no defense to the causes of action by proving each element of the causes of action entitling Plaintiff to judgment.
The burden having shifted to Defendant, the Court finds Defendant has failed to set forth specific facts showing that a triable issue of material fact exists as to the causes of action or a defense thereto as required by CCP 437c(p)(1).
The court finds that Plaintiff is entitled to judgment as a matter of law and GRANTS the Motion for Summary Judgment. Judgment to enter in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $10,939.81, plus court costs of $960.00, for a total judgment of $11,899,81.

Case Number: 25NWLC55510    Hearing Date: June 24, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on November 18, 2025.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.   Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED as to RFA's #1-5.
