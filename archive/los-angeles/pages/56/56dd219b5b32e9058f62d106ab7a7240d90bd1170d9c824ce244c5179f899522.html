Case Number: 25NWLC23889    Hearing Date: September 2, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by TD Bank USA ("Plaintiff") in support of the Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including a meet and confer letters dated June 16, 2026 and July 16, 2026, as well as the Complaint and Answer.

The Court finds Diana Navarro ("Defendant"), in the Answer, did not deny the statements of the complaint or indicate an affirmative defense but rather reported circumstances of current financial hardship.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $2,657.39 and costs in the amount of $435.00, for a total judgment of $3,092.39.

Case Number: 25NWLC44387    Hearing Date: September 2, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel, the Declaration in Support of the Motion, and the Proof of Service, filed August 20, 2026.  The Court finds that Defendant was served by mail and that Counsel submitted a declaration establishing the service requirements of California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362, have been satisfied.  The Court GRANTS the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure 284. Defense Counsel to give notice to Plaintiff and Defendant. Counsel is relieved effective upon filing the proof of service upon the defendant.
