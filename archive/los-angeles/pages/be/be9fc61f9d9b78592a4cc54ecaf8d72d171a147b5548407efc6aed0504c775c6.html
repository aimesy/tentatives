DEPARTMENT 408 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24STCV27560    Hearing Date: August 12, 2026    Dept: 408

Petitioner Yasmin Chavarria’s Petition to Approve Minor’s Compromise of Pending Action [i.e., as to Isabel Franchesca Lira] is GRANTED.

 

I.                BACKGROUND

 

On October 22, 2024, Plaintiffs Yasmin Chavarria, Jacob Leon, and Isabel Lira (“Plaintiffs”) filed a complaint against Defendants Yosemite Apartment Investments, LLC  (“Yosemite”) and I.C. Property Management & Investment Corp. (“I.C.”, collectively “Defendants”) alleging causes of action for:

 

1.     Breach of Warranty of Habitability (Violation of Civil Code § 1941.1);

2.     Breach of Warranty of Habitability (Health & Safety Code § 17920.3);

3.     Breach of Warranty of Habitability (Violation of Civil Code § 1942.4);

4.     Negligence – Premises Liability/Failure to Warn/ Negligence Per Se;

5.     Nuisance;

6.     Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress;

7.     Breach of Contract;

8.     Unfair Business Practices (Violation of Business and Professions Code § 17200, et. seq.); and

9.     Fraudulent Concealment.

 

On December 6, 2024, Yosemite filed an answer to Plaintiffs’ complaint.

 

On January 6, 2025, I.C. filed an answer to Plaintiffs’ complaint.

On May 13, 2026, Plaintiffs filed a Notice of Settlement of Entire Case. 

 

On June 1, 2026, Plaintiffs filed this Petition to Approve Minor Compromise. No opposition or other responsive pleading has been filed.

 

II.             LEGAL STANDARD

 

Court approval is required for all settlements of a minor’s claim.  (Prob. Code §§ 3500, 3600, et seq.; Code Civ. Proc., § 372.)  California Rules of Court Rule 7.950 provides that a petition “must be verified by the petitioner and must contain a full disclosure of all information that has any bearing upon the reasonableness of the compromise.” (Cal. Rules of Court, rule 7.950.)  Rule 7.950 states that the Petition must be filed on a completed Petition for Approval of Compromise of Claim or Action or Disposition of Proceeds of Judgment for Minor or Person with a Disability (Form MC-350). (Ibid.)

 

III.          DISCUSSION

 

          Petitioner Yasmin Chavarria (“Petitioner”) seeks court approval of a settlement between minor claimant Isabel Franchesca Lira (“Claimant”) (DOB: 2/22/11) and Defendants in the total amount of $10,000.00. (Petition, ¶ 10.) Petitioner is Claimant’s parent and guardian ad litem.

 

          The petition is executed by Petitioner. The action arose from bed bug bites at Plaintiffs’ rental property. (Id., ¶ 4.) Claimant sustained injuries for bed bug bites. (Id., ¶ 6.) Claimant has recovered completely. (Id., ¶ 8.)

 

          Petitioner seeks approval of $2,500.00 in attorney’s fees to be taken from Claimant’s settlement. (Petition, ¶ 13a.) This equates to 25% of Claimant’s total recovery. Petitioner has attached a declaration from Counsel Brain J. Virag. (Virag Decl., ¶¶ 3-10; Petition, ¶ 13A.) The court has reviewed the declaration of Counsel and believes that 25% is an appropriate percentage for attorney’s fees here.

 

          The net balance of proceeds for Claimant is $7,500.00, which will be deposited into a blocked account incurring interests until Claimant’s 18th birthday when payment will be disbursed. (Petition, ¶ 18(b).) The court finds the settlement is fair and reasonable.

 

          Accordingly, the petition is granted.

 

IV.          CONCLUSION

 

Petitioner Yasmin Chavarria’s Petition to Approve Minor’s Compromise of Pending Action [i.e., as to Isabel Franchesca Lira] is GRANTED.
