[TENTATIVE] ORDER: OSC RE WHY A RECEIVER SHOULD NOT BE APPOINTED

 

Plaintiff Red Oak Capital Fund VI, LLC (“Plaintiff”) and Defendant McKinney Capital Hermosa Partners, LLC (“Defendant”) seek Court approval of a stipulation to appoint a receiver for Defendant. The Court set the instant OSC after denying Plaintiff’s ex parte application for substantially the same relief. The Court identified several concerns with the proposed stipulation and directed the parties to provide supplemental briefing addressing those issues. (See Min. Order, June 4, 2026.)

The Court has reviewed Plaintiff’s supplemental briefing and the revised stipulated proposed order attached as Exhibit A to the Declaration of David Root filed on June 12, 2026. (Defendant opted to not file any briefing; Plaintiff’s counsel attests that Defendant’s counsel agrees to the revised stipulated proposed order.) The revisions materially address the concerns identified in the Court’s June 4, 2026 minute order and appropriately limit the proposed receiver’s authority.

The Court is therefore prepared to approve and sign the revised stipulated proposed order. Within 5 calendar days, Plaintiff shall file a clean, fully executed copy of the revised stipulated proposed order (i.e., the version attached as Exhibit A to the Root Declaration) for the Court’s review and signature.

The Court sets a Status Conference re Receivership for September 22, 2026 at 9:30 a.m. in this Department. Plaintiff or the receiver (when appointed) shall file a status report three court days before the status conference.

Plaintiff is ordered to give notice.

 

Date: June 24, 2026
