DEPARTMENT Z LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24NWLC54358    Hearing Date: August 18, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Velocity Investments Plaintiff in support of the Motion for Default Judgment and Request for Reconsideration.

Court notes that the Defendant has a FinWise Bank account which was serviced by Upstart Network, Inc., and the August 31, 2023 Bill of Sale declaring that the records were kept and maintained in the regular course of business, signed by Simon Darchis, VP for FinWise Bank, a qualified witness, authenticating the account records.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Default Judgment is GRANTED.  The Court orders judgment in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $2,887.25, $327.49 for costs, for a total judgment of $3,214.74. 


Case Number: 25NWLC32994    Hearing Date: August 18, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff in support of the Motion for Default Judgment and Request for Reconsideration.

The request for default judgment is GRANTED.

Civil Code section 1788.206, subdivision (a), states “(a) In an action initiated by a private education lender or private education loan collector, no default or other judgment may be entered against a defendant unless documents are submitted by the plaintiff to the court to establish the facts required to be alleged by paragraphs  (1) and (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 1788.205.  The documents shall be properly authenticated and each shall be in a form that would be admissible as a business record under Section 1271 of the Evidence Code."

The Court notes the Affidavit of Sale of Account by Original Creditor by Kristine D. Bennett, Senior Manager of Sallie Mae Bank the Original Creditor, Exhibit 1, to Plaintiff's Memorandum of Points and Authorities filed July 15, 2026, which properly authenticates and establishes the requirements of Evidence Code Section 1271.

The Court orders judgment in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $13,159.02 for the principal, and $478.25 for costs, for a total judgment of $13,637.27.
