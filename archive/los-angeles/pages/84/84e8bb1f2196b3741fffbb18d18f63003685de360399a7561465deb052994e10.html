DEPARTMENT 517 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 19STCV04540    Hearing Date: July 30, 2026    Dept: 517

The Court is denying the motion of judgment debtor Mark Buchheim for an order recalling, quashing, or limiting the Writ of Execution issued on June 24, 2026, and taxing or correcting the Memorandum of Costs After Judgment, Acknowledgment of Credit, and Declaration of Accrued Interest filed on or about June 18, 2026. (Motion, p. 2.)

Buchheim argues that the Judgment Creditors obtained the Writ of Execution while his motion for equitable offset in the amount of $22,700 was pending before the Court.  That fact does not support recalling, quashing or limiting the Writ of Execution as of the time that Buchheim sought that order, which was June 29, 2026.  Indeed, a writ of execution cannot be quashed in its entirety even when a claimed offset against the judgment is valid. (Turell v. Basic Investments, Inc. (1974) 36 Cal.App.3d 618, 621.)  On July 27, 2026, the Court granted Buchheim's motion for an equitable offset in the amount of $22,700.  At this point therefore, Code of Civil Procedure section  699.520, subdivision (f) requires that the Writ of Execution account for the partial offset.  To that end, United Executive Home, the entity to which the Judgment Creditors assigned their rights under the judgment in June 2026, shall apply pursuant to section 699.510 for an amended Writ of Execution.

 

Buchheim also moved under section 685.070 to tax the accrued interest line of the memorandum of costs on form MC-012 that the Judgment Creditors filed by the amount by which the Judgment was offset. However, the MC-012 form claims no post-judgment costs; thus, there is nothing to tax under this section of the Code of Civil Procedure. 

 


Case Number: 25STCV18971    Hearing Date: July 30, 2026    Dept: 517

The Court is granting Defendant's motion to compel arbitration of Plaintiff's claims in this action.  Through the supplemental declaration of Janice Ide submitted with Defendant's reply brief in support of the motion, Defendant carried its burden of proving the authenticity of Plaintiff's electronic signatures on the two arbitration agreements on which Defendant's motion rests.  The Court is overruling Plaintiff's evidentiary objections to the Ide supplemental declaration.  At the hearing, the Court can provide a further explanation for its conclusion regarding the authenticity of Plaintiff's electronic signatures.  

In light of the decision granting Defendant's motion, proceedings in this Court are stayed pending completion of the arbitration.  The Court is setting a hearing on the status of the arbitration for July 30. 2027 at 8:30

Case Number: 25STCV34861    Hearing Date: July 30, 2026    Dept: 517

Pending before the Court are four discovery motions Plaintiff filed seeking orders compelling Defendant to provide further responses to discovery that Plaintiff propounded on Defendant.  In its oppositions to the motions, Defendant stated that, after the motions were filed, it provided Plaintiff with the supplemental responses that Plaintiff sought in the motions.  Plaintiff did not file reply briefs to contest Defendants' statement.  Accordingly, the Court is denying Plaintiffs' motions as moot.  The Court also observes that Plaintiff did not seek in the motions orders imposing monetary sanctions in the form of attorney's fees and costs that Plaintiff incurred in connection with the motions.

Case Number: 26STCV04536    Hearing Date: July 30, 2026    Dept: 517

Defendants' demurrer to the claims for violation of the Los Angeles Tenant Anti-Harassment Ordinance and Business and Professions Code Section 17200 in Plaintiff's Complaint are overruled.   Defendants' demurrer to the remaining six claims in the Complaint is sustained with leave to amend.  In reaching these conclusions, the Court considered and is sustaining the Defendants' objections to the declaration of the Plaintiff that he submitted in support of his opposition to the demurrer.  At the hearing today, the Court can provide a more detailed explanation for its rulings on each aspect of the demurrer.

Defendants' motion to strike is granted with leave to amend to the extent that it targets requests in the Complaint for an award of punitive damages on all claims, except the claim for violation of the Los Angeles Tenant Anti-Harassment Ordinance -- the allegations in the Complaint are sufficient to support an award of punitive damages on that claim.  The motion to strike is also granted as to the prayer in the Complaint for an award of attorney's fees.  Plaintiff is self-represented.  Therefore, he cannot recover attorney's fees.   If Plaintiff' retains counsel, he may renew his request for attorney's fees.
