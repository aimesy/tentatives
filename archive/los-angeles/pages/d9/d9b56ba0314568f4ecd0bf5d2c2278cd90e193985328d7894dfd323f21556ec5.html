DEPARTMENT 224 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV07986    Hearing Date: August 6, 2026    Dept: 224

Dusty Costa Inc. v. Creative Distribution Services

TENTATIVE RULING

The OSC is CONTINUED to November 24, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. for Plaintiff to remedy the deficiencies. Plaintiff shall submit its updated default judgment package 10 days before the hearing.

The Court also sets an Order to Show Cause Re: Sanctions on November 24, 2026, at 9:00 a.m., in the amount of $500 for Plaintiff's Counsel's 1) Failure to Comply With the 5/7/26 Order to File a Corrected Default Judgment Package CCP 177.5; and 2) Failure to Obtain a Default Judgment CRC 3.110(g)-(i).  

The Court also gives notice it is also setting an OSC Re Dismissal for Failure to Prosecute pursuant to CRC 3.1340(a), on November 24, 2026, at 9:00 a.m., and intends to dismiss this action for failure to obtain a default judgment if a default judgment package is not filed by December 1, 2026. 

ANALYSIS

The Complaint arises out of a breach of contract wherein Defendants breached the Sales Agreement by failing to pay amounts due under the Agreement                                                            

PROOF OF SERVICE/DEFAULT:

Default was entered on 8/5/2025

PRINCIPAL REQUESTED:                                                              $  89,947.29

COSTS:                                                                      $  705.25

ATTORNEY FEES:                                                                           $  2,688.94

 

TOTAL:                                                                                             $  93,341.48

Discussion 

Amounts in complaint and request are aligned.

585 Declaration has been provided. (Costa Decl.)

Attorney’s fees are requested by the Complaint.

Attorney’s fees requested are in accordance with LASC Local Rule 3.214.

Costs are requested in the amount of $705.25 as enumerated in the Memorandum of Costs.

Proof of proper address for Declaration of Mailing has been provided.

PROBLEMS:

No proposed form of judgment is provided or filed. (JUD-100).

Apart from the copy of the original agreement, there is no documentary evidence proving up the amounts owed. (Costa Decl., Exh. A) The Complaint alleges Defendant’s failure to pay 10 invoices totaling $89,947.29 but Plaintiff has not provided copies of these invoices, or any other documentary evidence showing that the amount is owed.

The case caption lists DOES 1-10 as Defendants. The Doe Defendants have not been dismissed.

On May 7, 2026, the Court ordered counsel to correct these deficiencies ten days prior to the hearing on August 6, 2026. (Minute Order 5/7/26.) Counsel has not complied with the Court's Order. No corrected documents have been filed.

Conclusion

The OSC is CONTINUED to November 24, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. for Plaintiff to remedy the deficiencies. Plaintiff shall submit its updated default judgment package 10 days before the hearing.

The Court also sets an Order to Show Cause Re: Sanctions on November 24, 2026, at 9:00 a.m., in the amount of $500 for Plaintiff's Counsel's 1) Failure to Comply With the 5/7/26 Order to File a Corrected Default Judgment Package CCP 177.5; and 2) Failure to Obtain a Default Judgment CRC 3.110(g)-(i).  


The Court also gives notice it is also setting an OSC Re Dismissal for Failure to Prosecute pursuant to CRC 3.1340(a), on November 24, 2026, at 9:00 a.m., and intends to dismiss this action for failure to obtain a default judgment if a default judgment package is not filed by December 1, 2026.
