DEPARTMENT 730 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV21043    Hearing Date: August 14, 2026    Dept: 730

TENTATIVE

 

The request for default judgment submitted on August 7, 2026, is DENIED for the following reasons:

 

 Plaintiff Resolution Economics, LLC (“RE”) did not submit a declaration by a percipient witness in support of the updated request for default judgment that sufficiently addresses the Court’s previously identified issues. RE submitted a declaration of Natalia A. Minassian (“Minassian”), one of RE’s attorney’s of record. An attorney is generally not a percipient witness unless that attorney has some reason to have personal knowledge of the incidents that occurred outside of custody and control of litigation files in this matter. The Court cannot grant default judgment absent a percipient witness declaration that addresses all issues pertaining to damages.

 

The OSC re entry of default judgment is continued to the court’s next available date in approximately 60 days. No later than 15 days before the new hearing date, RE is to submit a new default judgment package correcting these defects. Failure to do so may result in the imposition of sanctions, including monetary sanctions and/or dismissal.
