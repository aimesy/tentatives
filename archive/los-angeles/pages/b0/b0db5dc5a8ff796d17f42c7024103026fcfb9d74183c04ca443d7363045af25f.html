DEPARTMENT B LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

IMPORTANT

Communicating with the Court Staff re the Tentative Ruling

1. Please notify the courtroom staff by email not later than 3:30 p.m. on the day before the hearing if you wish to submit on the tentative ruling rather than argue the motion. The email address is BurDeptB@lacourt.org.  
2. If you submit on the tentative, you must immediately notify the other side whether you will or will not appear at the hearing. You must include the other parties on the email by "cc."  
3. Include the word "SUBMISSION" in all caps in the Subject line and include your name, contact information, the case number, and the party you represent in the body of the email. If you submit on the tentative and elect not to appear at the hearing, the opposing party may nevertheless appear at the hearing and argue the motions.
4. Include the words "SUBMISSION BUT WILL APPEAR" if you submit but one or both parties will nevertheless appear.
5. PLEASE MAKE SURE THAT ALL COMMUNICATIONS WITH COURT STAFF DEAL ONLY WITH SCHEDULING AND ADMINISTRATIVE MATTERS AND DO NOT DISCUSS THE MERITS OF ANY CASE.



Case Number: 26NNCV02885    Hearing Date: September 29, 2026    Dept: B

DEFAULT JUDGMENT

OSC Hearing Date: 9/29/26

CASE NO.: 26NNCV02885

CASE NAME: American Express National Bank vs. Kenneth Santos

Moving Party: Plaintiffs American Express National Bank

Defaulting Party: Defendant Kenneth Santos

Ruling: GRANT

 

BACKGROUND

This is a collections action. Defendant Kenneth Santos a/k/a Kenneth A. Santos (Defendant) applied for and received two lines of credit from plaintiff American Express National Bank (Plaintiff). Defendant defaulted on monthly payments for both accounts.

On July 24, 2026, default was entered against Defendant. On the same day Plaintiff filed the instant request for entry of default judgment.

 

Plaintiff has complied with requirements outlined below. The request for default judgment submitted on July 24, 2026, is GRANTED for the following reasons:

·       Plaintiff justifies amount sought in principal damages and costs pursuant to the account statements between the parties and cost memorandum. (Rhoades Decl., ¶¶ 11-12; Exh. B; Exh. D; CIV-100, ¶ 7.)

·       Plaintiff waives attorney’s fees on the JUD-100 form. Thus, Plaintiff will not be awarded attorney’s fees.

Accordingly, Court judgment is entered in the amount of $54,077.50.

ORDER

Plaintiff American Express National Bank’s request for entry of Default Judgment against Kenneth Santos is GRANTED.  

MOVING PARTY is to give notice.
