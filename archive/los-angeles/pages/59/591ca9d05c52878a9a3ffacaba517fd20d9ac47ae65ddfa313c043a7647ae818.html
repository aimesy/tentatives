DEPARTMENT

415

HEARING DATE

CASE NUMBER

22STCV38758

MOTIONS 

Motions to Compel Discovery Responses

MOVING PARTIES

Plaintiffs Lisa Berghagen and Tamara Harris

OPPOSING PARTY

Defendant 548 S Spring Owner LLC

 

MOTIONS

            Plaintiffs Lisa Berghagen (“Plaintiff”) and Tamara Harris (“Harris”) move to compel responses from Defendant 548 S Spring Owner LLC (“Defendant”) to Form Interrogatories, set one (“FROG”), Special Interrogatories, set one (“SROG”), and Requests for Production, set one (“RPD”).  Plaintiff and Harris also move to deem admitted matters specified in Requests for Admissions, set one (“RFA”).  After filing this motion, Harris dismissed all claims against Defendant. 

As such, the Court considers the motions with respect to Plaintiff.  Defendant opposes the motions.

ANALYSIS

In its October 27, 2025 order, the Court entered the parties’ stipulation to continue trial.  With certain exceptions, the stipulation did not continue the discovery cut-off dates.  (See Declaration of T. Kasun Wijeyewickrema, Exhibit A.)  As such, the stipulation did not move the discovery motion cut-off date with respect to this discovery.  Given the prior trial date of December 9, 2025, Plaintiff’s deadline to have these motions heard was November 24, 2025.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2024.020, subd. (a).)  Accordingly, the Court denies the motion as untimely. 

Furthermore, the Court notes Plaintiff served the FROG, SROG, RPD, and RFA on Defendant on June 29, 2023 by email on Mark Hartney of Allen Matkins.  Defendant did not appear in the action until August 8, 2023.  As such, Plaintiff’s purported service on Defendant was improper, as Defendant had not appeared through counsel at the time of service. 

Plaintiff is ordered to provide notice of the Court’s ruling and to file proof of service of same.

 


Case Number: 23STCV27584    Hearing Date: July 16, 2026    Dept: 415

    TENTATIVE RULING

 

DEPARTMENT

415

HEARING DATE

July 16, 2026

CASE NUMBER

23STCV27584

MOTION

Motion for Sanctions

MOVING PARTIES

Plaintiffs Jane LA Doe and Jane MA Doe

OPPOSING PARTY

Defendant Los Angeles Unified School District

 

MOTION

             Plaintiffs Jane LA Doe and Jane MA Doe (“Plaintiffs”) move for sanctions under Code of Civil Procedure sections 128.7 and 2023.030 against Defendant Los Angeles Unified School District (“Defendant”).  Defendant opposes the motion.

ANALYSIS

Plaintiffs have lodged the entirety of their motion and all exhibits thereto under seal.  Plaintiffs cite no authority which permits them to do so.  Civil court proceedings are presumptively open to the public.  (Richmond Newspapers, Inc. v. Virginia (1980) 448 U.S. 555, 580, fn. 17.)  Before sealing records, “a trial court must hold a hearing and expressly find that (i) there exists an overriding interest supporting closure and/or sealing; (ii) there is a substantial probability that the interest will be prejudiced absent closure and/or sealing; (iii) the proposed closure and/or sealing is narrowly tailored to serve the overriding interest; and (iv) there is no less restrictive means of achieving the overriding interest.”  (NBC Subsidiary (KNBC-TV), Inc. v. Superior Court (1999) 20 Cal.4th 1178, 1217-1218.)  

Sealing the entirety of their motion and all exhibits on which Plaintiffs rely as a basis for sanctions is not narrowly tailored to protecting Plaintiffs’ privacy interests, especially as to matters that are already part of the public record of this case.  (See Savaglio v. Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. (2007) 149 Cal.App.4th 588, 601.)  As such, the Court cannot consider Plaintiffs’ motion, as Plaintiffs improperly lodged the motion under seal without leave to do so.  Thus, the motion is denied without prejudice.

The only authority Plaintiffs cite for lodging the motion and the exhibits in support thereof under seal is Evidence Code section 783.  But the provision does not concern sealing of exhibits.  As such, it is irrelevant.  

In any event, the Court cannot grant Plaintiff’s motion based on authority other than the authority plaintiffs cite in its notice of motion. (See Code Civ. Proc., § 1010 [“Notices must be in writing, and the notice of a motion, other than for a new trial, must state when, and the grounds upon which it will be made, and the papers, if any, upon which it is to be based”]; Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.1110(a) [“A notice of motion must state in the opening paragraph the nature of the order being sought and the grounds for issuance of the order”]; People v. American Surety Insurance Co. (1999) 75 Cal.App.4th 719, 726 [court concluded notice of motion made no reference to the fact of what the movant sought or the grounds for such relief, as required by rule 311(a) of the California Rules of Court, the predecessor to Rules of Court, rule 3.1110(a)].) 

Plaintiffs are ordered to provide notice of the Court’s ruling and to file proof of service of same.

 


Case Number: 24STCV09144    Hearing Date: July 16, 2026    Dept: 415

  TENTATIVE RULING

 

DEPARTMENT

415

HEARING DATE

July 16, 2026

CASE NUMBER

24STCV09144

MOTION

Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings

MOVING PARTY

Defendant Toyota Motor Sales, U.S.A., Inc.

OPPOSING PARTY

None

 

MOTION

Defendant Toyota Motor Sales, U.S.A., Inc. (“Defendant”) moves for judgment on the pleadings on the operative complaint of Plaintiff Esmeralda Hernandez (“Plaintiff”).  Unfortunately, Plaintiff is deceased.  Accordingly, Plaintiff’s claims must proceed through Plaintiff’s personal representative or successors-in-interest.  (See Code Civ. Proc., §§ 377.30, 377.34.)  This action cannot reasonably proceed without such substitution “because of lack of notice, lack of proper representation, or . . . other disadvantage.”  (Sacks v. FSR Brokerage, Inc. (1992) 7 Cal.App.4th 950, 957.) 

The Court must continue Defendant’s motion pending substitution of Plaintiff.  Upon substitution, Defendant may re-notice the motion as against Plaintiff’s personal representative or successor-in-interest.

Therefore, at that the hearing of this matter, the Court will set an order to show cause why the Court should not impose sanctions under Code of Civil Procedure section 177.5 against Plaintiff’s counsel, My Lemon Lawyer Group, for failure to comply with this Court’s March 9 and June 3, 2026 orders in which the Court expressly ordered counsel of record to provide a declaration detailing the efforts counsel had made to locate any personal representative for or successors-in-interest of Plaintiff.  Plaintiff’s counsel may file a response to the OSC regarding sanctions no later than five court days prior to the hearing.

The Court will also set a further order to show cause regarding substitution of Plaintiff.  

Defendant is ordered to provide notice of the Court’s ruling and to file proof of service of same.

 


Case Number: 25STCV00019    Hearing Date: July 16, 2026    Dept: 415

   TENTATIVE RULING

 

DEPARTMENT

415

HEARING DATE

July 16, 2026

CASE

25STCV00019

MOTION 

Motion to Interplead Funds

MOVING PARTY

Defendant Austin Jay Curtis

OPPOSING PARTIES

Plaintiffs Eman Yashar and Davood Yashar

 

MOTION

 

Defendant Austin Jay Curtis (“Defendant”) contends Defendant filed a cross-complaint in interpleader with Plaintiffs Eman Yashar and Davood Yashar (“Plaintiffs”) and Plaintiffs’ former counsel, RK Law Group, as claimants.  In their opposition to the motion, Plaintiffs reference “Defendant USAA General Indemnity Company’s”  motion for leave to deposit settlement funds through interpleader. The Court’s file does not reflect either a cross-complaint having been filed by Defendant nor an interpleader motion filed by Defendant’s purported carrier USAA. 

Nonetheless, Defendant now moves to interplead settlement funds with the Court, pursuant to the purported cross-complaint.  However, as no such cross-complaint appears in the Court’s file, the Court cannot grant this relief.  Furthermore, according to Plaintiffs, Defendant disbursed the settlement funds.  As such, the evidence before the Court suggests the ultimate issue is moot.

             Defendant is ordered to provide notice of the Court’s ruling and to file proof of service of same.
