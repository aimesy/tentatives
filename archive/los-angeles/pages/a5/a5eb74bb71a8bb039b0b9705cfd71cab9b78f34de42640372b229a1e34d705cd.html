DEPARTMENT 8 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26IWUD00564    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: 8


The demurrer is overruled. The complaint states a cause of action for unlawful detainer based upon nonpayment of rent, as the three-day notice to pay or quit appears on its face to comply with Code of Civil Procedure § 1161(2), and Plaintiff properly alleges service of the notice in conformity with Code of Civil Procedure § 1162. The Plaintiff also alleged the property is subject to the Tenant Protection Act, Civil Code section 1946.2. The Defendant is ordered to file an Answer only within five days notice of the court’s ruling.  

Case Number: 26IWUD01055    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: 8


The demurrer is overruled. The Defendants argue the complaint is defective because "the Three-day Notice was not properly served to the Defendant[s]." The complaint, however, alleges the Plaintiff served a three-day notice on the Defendants in a manner specified in the parties' written commercial lease. (See Cplt., ¶ 10.a(5).) Whether service was effected properly cannot be resolved via this demurrer, as that argument relies on facts beyond the face of the complaint of which the court cannot take judicial notice. (See Blank v. Kirwan (1985) 39 Cal.3d 311, 318; Donabedian v. Mercury Ins. Co. (2004) 116 Cal.App.4th 968, 994.) The Defendants are ordered to file an Answer only within five days' notice of the court’s ruling.
