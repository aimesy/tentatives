DEPARTMENT 534 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25NNCV03210    Hearing Date: July 2, 2026    Dept: 534

Background

 

On May 9, 2025, Plaintiffs Vardan Gevorgyan, Elmira Mkrtumian:, and Ervand Dilanian sued Yervant Sarain arising out of an alleged motor vehicle collision.

 

Motion

 

On June 4, 2026, Plaintiffs filed a motion seeking to transfer this action to the Chatsworth or Pasadena Courthouse.

 

Defendant filed a notice of non-opposition.

 

Motion to Transfer Between Districts

 

Standard

 

The Local Rules of the Superior Court of Los Angeles County govern the assignment of cases between its districts and departments. (Code Civ. Proc. § 402.) SCLAC Local Rule 2.3(b)(2) authorizes the Supervising Judge of the Civil Division to transfer civil cases from one judicial district to another via a noticed motion on three enumerated grounds: (1) when the case was filed in an improper district; (2) for the convenience of witnesses; or (3) to promote the ends of justice. (SCLAC Local Rule 2.3(b)(2).)

 

The Case Was Filed in an Incorrect District

 

Defendant seeks to transfer this action from the Glendale Courthouse to the either the Chatsworth Courthouse in the North Valley District or the Pasadena Courthouse in the Northeast Judicial District of the Superior Court of Los Angeles County. Plaintiffs contend there was a miscommunication regarding the location of the collision and the case was not filed in the correct location based upon the applicable Local Rules.

 

The original complaint alleged the collision at issue took place on the 134 Freeway at the San Fernando Road off ramp in Glendale, CA 91206, which is the addressed used on the civil case cover sheet and addendum.

 

On February 9, 2026, the parties filed a stipulation to transfer the action noting the collision took place on the San Fernando Road off ramp of the I-5 freeway in Mission Hills. Judge Ashfaq G. Chowdhury granted the parties’ stipulation.

 

On May 11, 2026, Judge Chowdhury issued an order stating, in part: “the Court finds proper jurisdiction is in Chatsworth Courthouse. Court orders the case transferred to Chatsworth Courthouse Pursuant to Local Rule 2.3(b)(2), and (a)(1)(A), with no objection. Case is referred to Stanley Mosk Courthouse, Department 1 for reassignment to Chatsworth Courthouse.”

 

In the instant motion, Plaintiffs allege the collision took place at the “I-5 freeway at the San Fernando Road offramp in the city of Sun Valley (zip code 91352).” (Mot. at 3:8-9.)

 

“Every unlimited civil tort action for bodily injury, wrongful death, or damage to personal property (hereinafter referred to as ‘Personal Injury Action’) must be filed in the judicial district where the incident arose. The filing court locator on the Los Angeles Superior Court website (www.lacourt.org) should be used to determine the appropriate filing location.” (SCLAC Local Rules, rule 2.3(A)(1)(A).) Plaintiffs’ case is a Personal Injury Action and subject to the mandatory filing rules. The filing locator indicates the Chatsworth Courthouse is an appropriate filing location for this action, is where the accident occurred, and where Judge Chowdhury found was the proper filing location.

 

The unopposed motion is GRANTED.

 

Conclusion

 

Motion to Reassign this Matter to the Proper Judicial District is GRANTED and this action is ordered reassigned to the Chatsworth Courthouse in the North Valley District.

 

All hearings currently set in 25NNCV03210 are hereby advanced and vacated.

 

Notice of case assignment shall be issued shortly.
