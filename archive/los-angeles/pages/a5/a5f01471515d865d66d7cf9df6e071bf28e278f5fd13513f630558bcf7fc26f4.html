DEPARTMENT 6 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26PSCV01765    Hearing Date: July 28, 2026    Dept: 6

CASE NAME:  Monique Caroline Valdivia v. Pick Five Imports, Inc. d/b/a Maxi-Matic U.S.A., Inc. 

Application of Zackary S. Kaylor to Appear Pro Hac Vice 

TENTATIVE RULING 

The Court GRANTS the application of Zackary S. Kaylor to appear pro hac vice. 

             Plaintiff is ordered to give notice of the Court’s ruling within five calendar days of this order. 

BACKGROUND 

This is a products liability case. On May 18, 2026, plaintiff Monique Caroline Valdivia (Plaintiff) filed this action against defendant Pick Five Imports, Inc. d/b/a Maxi-Matic U.S.A., Inc (Defendant) and Does 1 to 100, alleging causes of action for strict products liability, negligent products liability, breach of implied warranty of merchantability, and breach of implied warranty of fitness for a particular purpose. 

On June 4, 2026, Plaintiff submitted the verified application of Zachary S. Kaylor to appear pro hac vice for Plaintiff. The application is unopposed. 

LEGAL STANDARD 

Any attorney licensed to practice before the bar of any United States court or the highest court in any state, and who has been retained to appear in a particular cause pending a court of this State, but who is not a licensee of the California State Bar may apply to appear pro hac vice in this State by submitting his written application and mailing notice to all interested parties, as well as notice and an application fee to the State Bar of California in San Francisco.¿(Cal. Rules of Court, rule 9.40, subds. (a), (c), (e).)¿ An applicant may not reside or work in California, and may not perform regular or substantial business, professional, or other activities here. (Id., rule 9.40, subd. (a).) 

DISCUSSION 

 Plaintiff submitted the verified application of Zachary S. Kaylor (Kaylor) to be admitted pro hac vice as counsel. Kaylor resides in Minnesota and has an office in St. Paul, Minnesota. (Kaylor App., ¶ 1.) Kaylor is admitted to practice in the state of Minnesota, the state of North Dakota, the U.S. District Court of Minnesota, the Eastern District of Washington, and the Eastern District of Michigan. (Kaylor App., ¶ 2.) Kaylor is in good standing with these state bars and courts, and is not currently suspended or disbarred in any court. (Kaylor App., ¶¶ 3-4.) Kaylor is not regularly engaged in the practice of law in California. (Kaylor App., ¶ 5.) Kaylor has not applied to appear pro hac vice in California in the past two years. (Kaylor App., ¶ 6.) The California attorney of record is Jordon R. Harlan (Harlan), and Kaylor provided Harlan’s contact information. (Kaylor App., ¶ 7; Harlan Decl., ¶ 1.) Kaylor provided proof of electronic payment of the necessary fee and submission of the application to the State Bar of California. (Kaylor App., ¶ 9.) The proof of service for the application also indicates it was mail served on Defendant and the State Bar of California. (Proof of Service (6/4/26).) 

The Court finds Kaylor’s application complete and satisfactory. Based on the foregoing, the Court GRANTS the application. 

CONCLUSION 

The Court GRANTS the application of Zackary S. Kaylor to appear pro hac vice. 

             Plaintiff is ordered to give notice of the Court’s ruling within five calendar days of this order.
