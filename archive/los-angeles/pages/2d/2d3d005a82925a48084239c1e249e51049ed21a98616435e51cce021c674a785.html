Case Number: 25NWLC57285    Hearing Date: July 27, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC57285: TD BANK USA, N.A. vs DANIELA SANTANA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff TD Bank USA, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings (“Motion”), including the meet and confer letter dated January 22, 2026, as well as the Complaint and Answer.

Plaintiff’s Motion was initially set for hearing on June 16, 2026.  At the initial hearing, Defendant requested a continuance of the hearing to discuss settlement.  The Court granted that request and continued the hearing on the Motion to July 27, 2026.  The Court has not been advised of the status of any settlement between the parties and thus rules as follows: 

The Court finds that Defendant, in her Answer filed on November 12, 2025, admitted all of the statements of the complaint, but requested consideration due to financial hardship. 

Although the Court may consider financial hardship of the Defendant, if and when Plaintiff seeks to collect on the judgment, the Court cannot consider “financial hardship” as an affirmative defense to the debt owed.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the Complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action against Defendant and the Answer does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense. 

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff TD Bank USA, N.A. and against Defendant Daniela Santana for the principal sum of $4,974.09 plus costs in the amount of $413.00 for a total judgment of $5,387.09. 

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 26NWLC03166    Hearing Date: July 27, 2026    Dept: Y

26NWLC03166: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK N.A. vs GABE CAPETILLO

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on March 2, 2026 and filed with the Court on March 13, 2026.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order on Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Judgment entered by the Court on April 24, 2026.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments in the amount of $1,476.00, leaving an outstanding amount of $6,840.42 due and owing.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and against Defendant Gabe Capetillo for the remaining sum of $6,840.42 plus court costs of zero, for a total judgment of $6,840.42.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.
