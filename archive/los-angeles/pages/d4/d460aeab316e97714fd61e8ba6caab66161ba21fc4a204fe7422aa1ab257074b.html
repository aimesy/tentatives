Defendant’s Motion to CompeL PLaintiffs’ Further Responses to Special Interrogatories

 

MOVING PARTY:            Defendant Renewal by Andersen, LLC

 

RESPONDING PARTY:   No opposition.

Motion to Compel Further Responses to Special Interrogatories

The court considered the moving papers filed in connection with this motion. No opposition was filed.  

DISCUSSION

Defendant Renewal by Andersen, LLC (“Defendant”) moves the court for an order compelling plaintiffs Tom Thomas and David Duyck (“Plaintiffs’) to serve further responses to Special Interrogatories, Set Two, and for sanctions in the amount of $1,532.00 for Defendant and against Plaintiffs.

The court grants Defendant’s motion to compel Plaintiffs to serve further responses to Special Interrogatory No. 36 because the objections to the interrogatory are without merit.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.300, subd. (a)(3).)

The court grants Defendant’s motion to compel Plaintiffs to serve further responses to Special Interrogatory Nos. 59, 61, 62, 65, 66, 69, 70, 96, 105, 106, 107, 108, 123, and 124 because (1) Plaintiffs’ answers to the interrogatories are evasive and incomplete, and (2) the objections to the interrogatories are without merit.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.300, subd. (a)(1)(3).)

The court denies Defendant’s motion to compel Plaintiffs to serve further responses to Special Interrogatory Nos. 91-93 because the interrogatories are unintelligible.

The court grants Defendant’s request for monetary sanctions.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.300, subd. (c).)  The court finds that $842.00 ($230 per hour x 3.4 hours = $782 attorney’s fees + $60 motion filing fee) is a reasonable amount of sanctions to impose against Plaintiffs in connection with this motion.  (Hrabar Decl. ¶¶ 8-9.)

 

ORDER

The court grants in part defendant Renewal by Anderson LLC’s motion to compel further responses to Special Interrogatories.

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 2030.300, the court orders plaintiffs Tom Thomas and David Duyck to serve further, full and complete answers to defendant Renewal by Anderson LLC’s Special Interrogatories Nos. 36, 59, 61, 62, 65, 66, 69, 70, 96, 105, 106, 107, 108, 123, and 124, without objections and which comply with Code of Civil Procedure sections 2030.210-2030.250, within 20 days of the date of service of this order.

The court orders plaintiffs Tom Thomas and David Duyck to pay monetary sanctions to defendant Renewal by Andersen, LLC. in the amount of $842.00 within 30 days of the date of service of this order.

The court orders defendant Renewal by Anderson LLC to give notice of this ruling.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
