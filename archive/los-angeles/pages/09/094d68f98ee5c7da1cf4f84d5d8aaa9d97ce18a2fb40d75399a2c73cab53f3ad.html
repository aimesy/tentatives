TENTATIVE RULING

 

MELINTON ROLANDO MENCOS

 

 

         vs.

 

TAM THI THANH VO

 

 Case No.: 25STCV29533

 

 

 

 Hearing Date: August 27, 2026

 

 

Defendant’s motion to transfer venue to Orange County is GRANTED.

 

            On 10/8/2025, pro per Plaintiff Melinton Rolando Mencos (Plaintiff) filed suit against Tam Thi Thanh Vo (Defendant), alleging breach of contract.

 

            On 1/15/2026, pro per Defendant moved for change of venue.

 

            The motion is unopposed.

 

Discussion

 

            Defendant moves to transfer venue to the Central Justice Center —Santa Ana, in the  County of Orange on the grounds that this action concerns real property located at 2208 Maple St, Santa Ana CA 92707.

 

            CCP section 392 provides that actions for the recovery of real property, or for injuries to real property, or actions arising out of rights or obligations related to real property, must be tried in the county where the property is situated.

 

            Here, this action clearly arises out of rights or obligations related to real property, and thus must be tried in Orange County.

 

            Based on the foregoing, Defendant’s motion to transfer venue to Orange County is granted.

 

It is so ordered.
