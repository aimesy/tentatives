DEPARTMENT 517 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 21STCV42883    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: 517

The Court is granting the Defendants' motion for leave to conduct a mental examination of the Plaintiff.  The examination shall be conducted by Dr. Dean C. Delis, Ph.D, pursuant to the protocols set forth in Dr. Delis's declaration in support of the Defendants' motion.   Those protocols provide as follows: (1) Dr. Delis will exercise his professional judgment to select which tests to administer to Plaintiff from the list of possible tests identified in his declaration.  (2)  Dr. Deliss will send the raw test data to a psychologist retained by Plaintiff's attorney.  (3) Plaintiff's attorney may separately record the testing part of the exam and also send that to the retained psychologist.  (4) If Plaintiff's retained psychologist finds that Dr. Delis made material mistakes, the raw test data and audio may be released to Murillo’s attorney pursuant to a protective order and able to be used on cross-examination. 

The Court agrees with Defendants that Randy's Trucking v. Superior Court (2023) 91 Cal.App.5th 818 does not foreclose the use of Dr. Delis's protocol in connection with the mental examination of the Plaintiff.

The Court will enter the proposed order that Defendants submitted with their motion.  The order sets forth, among other things, the date and location of the mental examination of the Plaintiff that Dr. Delis will conduct.


 

Case Number: 25STCV08551    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: 517


The Court is granting the motion of Defendant for an order compelling Plaintiff Chana Hayden's compliance with Code of Civil Procedure section 871.26, which requires plaintiffs in Song-Beverly Consumer Warranty Act cases to sit for an early deposition.  The deposition shall occur by October 5, 2026.  The case management conference on calendar today is continued to October 20, 2026.  The Court is also setting for that date an order to show cause as to why Hayden's action should not be dismissed on the Court's own motion.  If Hayden fails to appear for the early deposition, the Court will dismiss the action on October 20, 2026.  Defendant is entitled under section 871.26 to recover attorney's fees from Plaintiff's counsel Strategic Legal Practices ("SLP") due to Hayden's failure to appear for the early deposition as required by that statute.  The sanctions are payable to Defendant's counsel within 30 days of this order. 

The motion of SLP to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff is granted.   SLP shall give notice to Hayden of the ruling on the motion to be relieved.   SLP also shall file an amended proposed order that notifies Hayden of the October 5, 2026  deposition date and October 20, 2026 hearing dates.

Case Number: 25STCV16552    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: 517

Pending before the Court is Plaintiff's motion for relief from the waiver of his objections to Special Interrogatories Numbers 1, 2, and 3 ("the SROGs") that Defendant propounded on Plaintiff. Plaintiff waived the objections.  That is because his responses to the SROGs were untimely. (Code of Civil Procedure section 2030.290, subdivision (a)(1).  Plaintiff is not entitled to relief from waiver of the objections.  That is because Plaintiff has not provided Defendant with supplemental responses to the SROGs that substantially comply with applicable requirements of the Code of Civil Procedure.  

Defendant requested that the Court impose monetary sanctions against Plaintiff for having brought the unsuccessful motion for relief from waiver of objections. The requested sanctions take the form of attorney's fees and costs Defendant incurred in connection with its opposition to the motion.  Defendant's request is denied on the ground that Defendant failed to state the amount of fees and costs that it is seeking.

Case Number: 25STCV37199    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: 517

The Court is granting Defendants' motion to stay proceedings in this action pending resolution of a federal action that is styled Castaneda v. Total Renal Care, Inc., et al., Case No. 2:25-cv-12378-JGB-SP (C.D. Cal.)   Application of the factors set forth in Caiafa Professional Law Corporation v. State Farm Fire and Casualty Co. (1993) 15 Cal.App.4th 800, 804, support the exercise of the Court's discretion to enter that stay.  In reaching this decision, the Court considered and is overruling Plaintiff's evidentiary objections to the declaration of Gregory Knopp that accompanied Defendant's motion.

The case management conference on calendar today in this action is vacated.  The Court is setting a hearing on the status of the federal action for August 5, 2027 at 8:30 a.m.
