Case Number: 24STCV13291    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: 506




On May 27, 2026, Defendant filed a motion to compel Plaintiffs' depositions and a motion to compel inspection of the vehicle.

The unopposed motion to compel responses is GRANTED.  Defendant Four-Season Bus Specialist LLC is ORDERED to provide verified responses, without objections, to Plaintiff's Special Interrogatories, Set Two within 30 days.  Defendant is also ORDERED to pay total sanctions of $1,340.00 to Plaintiff within 30 days.
