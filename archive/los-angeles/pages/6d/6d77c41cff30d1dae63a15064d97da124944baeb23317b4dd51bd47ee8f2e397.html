Tentative Ruling

On July 20, 2026, a number of matters came on for hearing.  Plaintiff (self-represented) appeared, as did counsel for Defendant.

At the conclusion of the proceedings, the Court issued a Minute Order that (among other things): (1) set an Order to Show Cause re Why the First Amended Complaint Should not be Stricken on the Ground That it is Unsigned for hearing on August 17, 2026; (2) granted Plaintiff leave to file, by August 10, 2026, a corrected, signed First Amended Complaint; and (3) ordered counsel for Defendant to give notice and to file proof of service of notice with the Court by no later than July 24, 2026.

Plaintiff did not file a corrected, signed First Amended Complaint or otherwise respond to the Order to Show Cause.  Defendant did not file a proof of service with the Court give notice.

The Court CONTINUES all hearings set for August 17, 2026, to September 11, 2026, at 8:30 a.m., in Department 514 in the Stanley Mosk Courthouse.

Any response to the Order to Show Cause re Why the First Amended Complaint Should not be Stricken on the Ground That it is Unsigned must be in writing and must be filed by no later than September 1, 2026.

The Court GRANTS Plaintiff leave to file, by no later than September 1, 2026, a corrected First Amended Complaint that is signed but otherwise makes no changes from the FAC filed on October 6, 2025.  (If Plaintiff wishes to amend the pleading, she must file a motion seeking leave to do so.)

The Court advances and CONTINUES the hearing on Plaintiff’s motion for sanctions from August 20, 2026, to September 11, 2026, at 8:30 a.m., in Department 514 of the Spring Street Courthouse.

The Court ORDERS counsel for the defendant to give notice and to file proof of service of written notice with the Court by no later than August 19, 2026.
