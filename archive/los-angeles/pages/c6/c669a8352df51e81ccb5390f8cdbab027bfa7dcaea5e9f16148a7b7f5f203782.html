MOTION

              Petitioner Forward Financing LLC (“Petitioner”) petitions the Court to confirm an arbitration award Petitioner obtained against Respondents the Suhan Family Corp. and Esteban Suhun (“Respondents”). 

In its February 25, 2026 order, at petitioner’s request, the Court continued the matter to March 26, 2026, as Petitioner had not filed a notice of hearing, as required.  (See Code Civ. Proc., § 1290.2.)  Petitioner then gave notice of the March 26, 2026 hearing on March 2, 2026 by mail.  But that was not sufficient notice.  (See Code Civ. Proc., § 1005, subd. (b) [when served by mail, the required notice is 16 court days and an additional five calendar days].)  Accordingly, for due process purposes, the Court continued the matter once more to this date for Petitioner to give proper notice to Respondent.  Petitioner failed to do so. 

Accordingly, the petition is denied without prejudice as procedurally defective.

The Court sets an order to show cause hearing on August 5, 2026at 8:30 a.m. concerning why the Court should not impose sanctions pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 177.5 against Petitioner’s counsel, Adam Brumage of the Moore Law Group, for failure to comply with this Court’s February 25 and March 26, 2026 orders.  Petitioner’s counsel may file a response to the OSC regarding sanctions no later than five court days prior to the scheduled hearing.

Petitioner is ordered to provide notice of the Court’s ruling and to file proof of service of same.
