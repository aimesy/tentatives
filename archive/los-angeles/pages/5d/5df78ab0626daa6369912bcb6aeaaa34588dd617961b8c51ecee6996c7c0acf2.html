21NWLC16142: BANK OF AMERICA N.A. vs EFFIE A DOMINGUEZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on July 2, 2021 and filed with the Court on August 31, 2021.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order on Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Judgment entered by the Court on September 2, 2021.

Case Number: 25NWLC49178    Hearing Date: June 24, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC49178: SYNCHRONY BANK vs SERGIO MARTINEZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Synchrony Bank, the supporting declaration, and all relevant court records.  

In its motion, Plaintiff requests to vacate the Default Judgment entered on January 2, 2026 and dismiss the action without prejudice.   Plaintiff has presented evidence via declaration that Plaintiff received notification that Defendant Sergio Martinez (“Defendant”) claimed fraud on the account.  Plaintiff has reviewed and confirmed the fraud claim. 

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473(b), the Court may set aside any judgment that was obtained by mistake or surprise.  Code of Civil Procedure section 187 also grants jurisdiction to the Court to take appropriate action in the interests of justice.

Based on the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Set Aside and Vacate Default and Default Judgment.   Accordingly, the Judgment entered against Defendant on January 2, 2026 is vacated.

The Court further orders that the Complaint filed by Plaintiff and against Defendant is DISMISSED with prejudice.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC58027    Hearing Date: June 24, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC58027: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs GLEN J HYNDS, AN INDIVIDUAL

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated May 8, 2026.  No opposition has been filed.

Pursuant to California Evidence Code sections 452 and 453, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s request for the Court to take judicial notice of:

  1. Plaintiff’s Complaint filed on September 30, 2025;
  2. The Court’s Order Deeming Plaintiff’s Requests For Admission Nos. 1- 8 as Admitted entered on May 6, 2026 (and the Nunc Pro Tunc Order issued by the Court to correct a clerical error in the Order on June 23, 2026); and
  3. Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted, and the Requests for Admission submitted in support of that motion, filed on May 28, 2026.

The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot reasonably be controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff and against Defendant Glen Hynds for the principal sum of $24,540.08 plus costs in the amount of $430.00, for a total Judgment of $24,970.08.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.
