DEPARTMENT X LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

Unless
otherwise indicated in the tentative ruling, counsel/parties wishing to submit
on a tentative ruling may send an email to [email protected] advising the
Court and all counsel of their intention to submit.  Prior to sending an
email to Department X, counsel/parties should confer with any opposing or
appearing parties in the action to determine whether all parties submit. 
Unless all parties submit, the matter will still be called for hearing and may
be argued by any appearing/non-submitting parties.  If all parties submit
on the Court’s tentative ruling, no appearance is necessary and counsel for
moving party shall give notice of ruling unless otherwise ordered by the
Court.  This may be done by incorporating verbatim the Court’s tentative
ruling. 


Case Number: 24AHCV00222     Hearing Date: July 7, 2026    Dept: X

PAOLO MENDEZ, et al. vs FIRST TOP, INC., et al.

The four petitions to approve the compromises of the claims of Aiko Dasha Mendez Vazquez, Svana Anezka Mendez Vazquez, Kane Donovan Mendez Vazquez, and Daren Noland Mendez Vazquez are GRANTED.

1. The Court approves a gross settlement allocation of $10,000 for each minor.

2. The Court approves attorney’s fees of $2,500 from each minor’s share. 

3. The net balance payable for the benefit of each minor is $7,500. 

4. The net balance for each minor shall be deposited in a blocked, federally insured account in the sole name of the respective minor, subject to withdrawal only upon written order of this Court.

5. Petitioner and counsel are authorized to execute all releases, receipts, deposit instructions, and other documents reasonably necessary to consummate the settlements consistent with this Order.

6. The Court sets an OSC re: Proof of Deposit, for each minor’s net proceeds, for October 14, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. in Department X. No appearance is required if a receipt is filed.

7. The Court retains jurisdiction to enforce this Order and to supervise compliance with the approved disposition of the minors’ proceeds.

Moving Parties are to give notice.
