DEPARTMENT 514 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26STCV18890    Hearing Date: September 2, 2026    Dept: 514

Jones v. Bushnell
26STCV18890
Defendant’s Special Motion to Strike

Tentative Ruling

On July 23, 2026, Defendant Leslie Bushnell filed a Notice of Related Case.  The notice identified two cases pending in the probate division as related to this civil case.

On July 27, 2026, this Court issued an order directing the parties to file the Notice of Related Case with the Supervising Judge of the Civil Division in Department 534 (formerly Department 1), as required by Local Rule 3.3.

The Court has reviewed the file and sees no evidence that the parties have complied with the Court’s order dated July 27, 2026.

The Court CONTINUES the hearing on Defendant’s Special Motion to Strike for approximately 45 days.

The Court ORDERS Defendant Leslie Bushnell, by September 8, 2026, (1) to submit the Notice of Related Case directly to Department 534, (2) to file the Notice of Related Case in all cases identified in the notice, to serve all parties in all cases identified in the notice , to comply with all requirements of California Rules of Court, rule 3.300 and Local Rule 3, and the follow up with Department 534 to confirm that the Notice of Related Case has been received in that department. 

The Court ORDERS Defendant Leslie Bushnell, by September 15, 2026, to file a written report in this Case No. 26STCV18890, showing compliance with this order and the Court’s order of July 27, 2026.

The Court SETS a Non-Appearance Case Review Re: Submission of Notice of Related Case to Department 534 for September 22, 2026, at 10:30 am, in this department.

The Judicial Assistant is directed to give notice.
