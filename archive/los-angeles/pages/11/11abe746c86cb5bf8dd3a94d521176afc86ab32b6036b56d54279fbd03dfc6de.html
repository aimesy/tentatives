DEPARTMENT 517 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24STCV26042    Hearing Date: July 21, 2026    Dept: 517

The Court is granting the motion of Plaintiff American Contractors Indemnity Company ("ACIC") for an order awarding ACIC attorney's fees of $7,871.50 and costs of $1,563.61 that ACIC says it incurred in connection with its action against Defendants Capitol Trucking Inc., Igor Tsapar, and Iryna Tsapar (“Defendants”).  The Court is granting ACIC's motion.

ACIC's action against the Defendants was for breach of contract.  The contract at issue contained a provision authorizing the party that prevails in litigation over the contract to recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.   ACIC obtained summary judgment on its claim for breach of contract.  ACIC thus is the prevailing party.

Based on the Court's review of the documentation that ACIC submitted in support of its motion, the Court has concluded that the hourly billing rates of the attorneys and paralegals who worked on the action on ACIC's behalf are reasonable.  The Court also has concluded that the number of hours that the attorneys and paralegals spent on specific tasks identified in the documentation was reasonable. The Court was unpersuaded by Defendants' argument that the amount of fees and costs that ACIC is seeking in the motion is excessive.

Within 7 days of this order, ACIC's counsel is directed to file and serve a proposed amended judgment that incorporates the Court's ruling on ACIC's motion for attorney's fees and costs into the judgment.  The Court is setting a non-appearance hearing for August 4, 2026 at 8:30 regarding entry of the amended judgment.

Case Number: 25STCV36253    Hearing Date: July 21, 2026    Dept: 517

 The Court is sustaining with leave to amend Defendant's demurrer to Plaintiff's claim in its First Amended Complaint ("FAC") for an open book account.  The Court agrees with Defendant that the allegations in the FAC are insufficient to establish an agreement for the creation of an open book account or the existence of a fiduciary obligation that would impose an open book account

The Court is overruling the Defendant's demurrer to Plaintiff's claim in the FAC a violation of the unfair competition law ("UCL") codified in section 17200 of the Business and Professions Code.  Defendant's demurrer to the UCL claim rests on the proposition that the allegations in the FAC are insufficient to show that Plaintiff has suffered an injury to give it standing to pursue the claim.  The Court disagrees with the Defendants.  The allegations in the FAC that Defendant has refused to pay money owed to Plaintiff through an assignment of payment rights from third parties to the Plaintiff are sufficient to establish Plaintiff's standing to asset a UCL claim against the Defendant.

Plaintiff shall file and serve a Second Amended Complaint within 21 days of this order.  The case management conference on calendar today is continued to November 18, 2026 at 8:30 a.m.
