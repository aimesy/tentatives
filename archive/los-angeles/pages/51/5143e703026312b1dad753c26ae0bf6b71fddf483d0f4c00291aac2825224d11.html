DEPARTMENT Z LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 23NWLC32719    Hearing Date: July 31, 2026    Dept: Z

Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by the Plaintiff.

Court notes the following case-specific facts:

A judgment pursuant to stipulated agreement was entered on September 16, 2025.  The declaration in support of the motion stated the credits to be $1,987.02.  However, the declaration also stated the principal amount owed to be $2,387.55, the costs to be $293.00, and the total proposed judgment to be $1,693.55, which would be the correct amount if the credits were $987.00.

The Court entered judgment for Plaintiff for $693.53.

Plaintiff requests amending the judgment to $2,387.55 for the principal, $293.00 for costs, less $987.00 for the credits, for a total amount of $1,693.55, which was the total amount initially requested on September 16, 2025. 

Plaintiff's Motion to Amend the Judgment pursuant to California Code of Civil Procedure sections 473(d) and 128(a)(8) is GRANTED.

Court vacates the prior judgment entered on September 16, 2025.

Judgment in favor of the Plaintiff in the amount of $2,387.55 for the principal, $293.00 for costs, less $987.00 for the credits, for a total judgment of $1,693.55. Proposed judgment and order will be signed.
