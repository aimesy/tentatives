Case Name: Doe DH v. LAUSD, et al.

Case No.: 25STCV23946 

Matter: Motion to Compel Compliance

Moving Party: Plaintiff Jane Doe DH

Responding Party: Non-Party Commission on Teacher Credentialing

Notice: OK

Ruling: The Motion is granted in part.


Moving party to give notice.


The Court encourages all parties to appear remotely via LA CourtConnect.  If submitting on the Court's tentative ruling, please follow the instructions provided above.


Plaintiff Jane Doe DH seeks to compel compliance with her business record subpoena served on Non-Party Commission on Teacher Credentialing with respect to Defendant Raul Preciado.

The subpoena seeks the following.

1. Any and all DOCUMENTS, including statements of decision, findings, summaries, opinions, rulings, and/or minutes regarding any California Commission on Teacher Credentialing investigation and/or hearing regarding RAUL PRECIADO (DOB: 06/1964), who was licensed by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing. 


2. Any and all DOCUMENTS regarding any disciplinary action imposed on RAUL PRECIADO, who is licensed by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing. 


3. Any and all DOCUMENTS regarding complaints about inappropriate behavior by RAUL PRECIADO. 


4. Any and all DOCUMENTS regarding any response by RAUL PRECIADO to any complaints made against him. 

5. Any and all DOCUMENTS regarding any alleged improper behavior by licensee RAUL PRECIADO. 


6. Any and all DOCUMENTS submitted to the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing by RAUL PRECIADO, including but not limited to applications, statements, fingerprints, and forms. 


7. Any and all DOCUMENTS referring and/or regarding correspondence and/or communications between the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing and RAUL PRECIADO. 


8. Any and all DOCUMENTS referring and/or regarding correspondence and/or communications between the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing and LOS ANGELES UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT regarding RAUL PRECIADO.

 

9. The California Commission on Teacher Credentialing's complete file of RAUL PRECIADO.


The Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CTC) argues that the release of these documents without a court order will result in criminal prosecution. Additionally, it argues that some of the requests are overbroad or seek privileged or private content.

The Motion is granted as to request nos. 1-2, but internal preliminary drafts, unadopted summaries, and meeting minutes of the Committee of Credentials are excluded pursuant to the Deliberative Process Privilege and Evidence Code section 1040 (Official Information).

The Motion is granted as to request nos. 3-5, but pursuant to Civil Code section 1798.24 and constitutional privacy rights, CTC must redact all personal identifying information (names, contact info, etc.) of minor students, non-party complainants, and third-party witnesses. 

The Motion is granted as to request no. 6, but fingerprint records, DOJ/FBI criminal background check reports (rap sheets), and criminal history information are excluded from production pursuant to Penal Code sections 11105 and 11142.

The Motion is granted as to request no. 7, but settlement communications, confidential medical/psychological evaluations, or third-party personal information contained within the correspondence must be redacted.

The Motion is granted as to request no. 8, but this request is subject to the below limitations. 

With respect to request nos. 1-8, the Court will also exclude records generated by external third parties (e.g., Pasadena PD police reports, court records, or independent medical files) held within CTC’s investigative file. CTC is not the legal custodian of external third-party records and cannot authenticate them via Evidence Code section 1561. (Cooley v. Superior Court (2006) 140 Cal.App.4th 1039, 1045). Plaintiff must seek those directly from the originating entities.

Additionally, the home addresses, telephone numbers, social security number, medical records, or any other confidential information of Preciado and third-parties shall be redacted.

Any documents containing attorney-client or attorney work product privileged materials shall be redacted.

Also, given that the assaults took place in 2009-2010, the Court enters a temporal limitation of 1999 through 2015 for all documents produced.

The Motion is denied as to request no. 9, which is overbroad. 

In sum, the Motion is granted in part as set forth herein. CTC should produce records within 30 days.

Pursuant to Education Code section 44230(a)(2), this Court Order authorizes the CTC to disclose the non-privileged responsive records ordered herein, relieving CTC personnel of liability under Education Code section 44248.

No sanctions.

Moving party to give notice.

Tentative Ruling

Judge Kevin C. Brazile

Department 310

Hearing Date: September 16, 2026

Case Name: Leon v. Torres, et al.

Case No.: 26STCV09479

Matter: Demurrer

Moving Party: Defendants Victoria Torres, Maria Santos Isabel, and Reynaldo Salgado

Sotelo

Responding Party: Plaintiff Maria Leon

Notice: OK

Ruling: The Demurrer is sustained in part, without leave to amend.


Moving party to give notice.


The Court encourages all parties to appear remotely via LA CourtConnect.  If submitting on the Court's tentative ruling, please follow the instructions provided above.


On March 24, 2026, Plaintiff Maria Leon filed the operative Complaint for (1) intentional misrepresentation, (2) financial elder abuse, (3) constructive trust, and (4) quiet title. 

Defendants Victoria Torres, Maria Santos Isabel, and Reynaldo Salgado Sotelo now demur to the entirety of the Complaint for uncertainty and failure to state sufficient facts. 

When considering demurrers, courts read the allegations liberally and in context, and “treat the demurrer as admitting all material facts properly pleaded, but not contentions, deductions or conclusions of fact or law.” (Serrano v. Priest (1971) 5 Cal.3d 584, 591.)  “A demurrer tests the pleadings alone and not the evidence or other extrinsic matters. Therefore, it lies only where the defects appear on the face of the pleading or are judicially noticed. The only issue involved in a demurrer hearing is whether the complaint, as it stands, unconnected with extraneous matters, states a cause of action.”  (Hahn v. Mirda¿(2007) 147 Cal.App.4th 740, 747.)  It is error “to sustain a demurrer without leave to amend if the plaintiff shows there is a reasonable possibility any defect identified by the defendant can be cured by amendment.”  (Aubry v. Tri-City Hospital Dist.¿(1992) 2 Cal.4th 962, 967.)


  1. Fraud

Defendants argue that fraud is not pleaded with specificity as to them because the physical speakers of the misrepresentations were non-moving Defendants Raul Gonzalez and Sonia Quezada Flores. However, Plaintiff sufficiently pleads vicarious liability under a civil conspiracy theory.

A civil conspiracy requires (1) the formation and operation of the conspiracy, (2) illegal operation by the parties, and (3) damage resulting from the operation (Applied Equipment Corp. v. Litton Saudi Arabia Ltd. (1994) 7 Cal.4th 503, 511). Because direct evidence of a clandestine agreement is rarely available, a conspiratorial agreement may be tacit and inferred from the nature of the acts, the relation of the parties, and the economic interests involved (Wyatt v. Union Mortgage Co. (1979) 24 Cal.3d 773, 784–785).

Here, Plaintiff alleges that Isabel, Torres, and Sotelo had full knowledge of the falsity of the statements, were present (Isabel) when the misrepresentations were made to Plaintiff, agreed with Raul and Sonia that the statements be made to induce reliance, and actively participated in the financial proceeds of the fraud—specifically taking record title (Torres/Sotelo) and taking ongoing rental income generated by the property (Isabel). Accepting these well-pled facts and reasonable inferences as true, Plaintiff has adequately alleged Defendants’ concurrence in the fraudulent scheme. The specific details regarding dates, instruments, and property descriptions provide adequate notice. The Demurrer is overruled as to the first cause of action.


  1. Elder Abuse

The elements of a cause of action for financial elder abuse are (1) the defendant took, hid, appropriated, obtained or retained plaintiff's property; (2) plaintiff is at least 65 years of age or a dependent adult; (3) the defendant took, hid, appropriated, obtained or retained plaintiff's property for a wrongful use, with the intent to defraud, or by undue influence; (4) the plaintiff was harmed; and (5) the defendant's conduct was a substantial factor in causing plaintiff's harm.  (See CACI No. 3100.)

Plaintiff alleges she is a 71-year-old, Spanish-speaking elder who was ill and medicated. Plaintiff explicitly alleges that Defendants acted in concert to obtain title to her property and encumber it via misrepresentations and undue influence, and that Isabel actively assists in retaining the property's fruits by collecting rental income generated from the unlawful taking. This satisfies the statutory elements at the pleading stage. The Demurrer is overruled as to the second cause of action.


  1. Constructive Trust

A constructive trust is an equitable remedy to prevent unjust enrichment, not an independent, standalone substantive cause of action (Batt v. City and County of San Francisco (2007) 155 Cal.App.4th 65, 82). Plaintiff concedes that constructive trust is equitable relief. The Demurrer to this standalone count is sustained without leave to amend; however, Plaintiff may seek constructive trust as a remedy in her Prayer for Relief.


  1. Quiet Title

As to Defendants Torres and Sotelo, the Verified Complaint satisfies Code of Civil Procedure section 761.020 by stating the street address, APN, legal description, basis of equitable title, adverse record claims of Torres and Sotelo, and the date for determination. The Demurrer is overruled as to these Defendants.

As to Defendant Isabel, quiet title lies only against those who claim an adverse legal or equitable title, lien, or estate in the real property itself (Code Civ. Proc. § 761.020). Because Isabel is not apparently a record owner on title and holds no adverse estate or security interest in the realty, quiet title cannot lie against her despite her alleged receipt of rental proceeds. The Demurrer is sustained without leave to amend as to Isabel only.


  1. Summary 

The Demurrer is overruled as to the first and second causes of action. 

The Demurrer is sustained as to the third cause of action, without leave to amend. The prayer for relief remains. 

The Demurrer of Defendants Torres and Sotelo is overruled as to the fourth cause of action. 

The Demurrer of Isabel is sustained as to the fourth cause of action, without leave to amend.

Defendants are to file an answer within 10 days.

Moving party to give notice.
