REVIEW OF DEFAULT JUDGMENT PACKAGE

           

Plaintiff Arbat, A Law Corporation (“Plaintiff”) requests default judgment against Defendant Edgar Manvelyan in the total amount of $62,803.15, comprising $59,024.53 in damages as demanded in the Complaint, $3,143.79 in interest, and $634.83 in costs.
