DEPARTMENT Y LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25NWLC60999    Hearing Date: September 18, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC60999: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK N.A. vs VICTORIA B LAVIDES

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the declaration of counsel.   The Court finds that Plaintiff mailed Requests for Admission (RFA) to counsel for Defendant on December 17, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Plaintiff did not receive any responses from Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed its Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted to counsel for Defendant on April 22, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  The address for counsel for Defendant on both proofs of service match the address that is listed on the Answer.

Having considered the moving papers, Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted is GRANTED.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC72254    Hearing Date: September 18, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC72254: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK N.A. vs DAISY M VICENCIO

Tentative Ruling:

The Court issues an Order to Show Cause Re Sanctions pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure §177.5 directed at Dennis Smith, Esq. and Philip Green, Esq. of Arnold & Smith Law Firm, PLLC, counsel for Defendant Daisy Vicencio. 

On July 24, 2026, counsel for Defendant, Dennis Smith and Philip Green of Arnold & Smith Law Firm, PLLC, were ordered to serve a copy of the Court’s Order relieving them as counsel of record for Defendant on Defendant at her current address and all known email addresses and filed a proof of service with the Court.  Counsel for Defendant has failed to do so.  Until such proof of service is filed with the Court, Dennis Smith and Philip Green of Arnold & Smith Law Firm, PLLC remain counsel of record for Defendant Daisy Vicencio.

The Order to Show Cause Re Sanctions is scheduled on October 19, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Department Y of the Norwalk Courthouse.  Appearance via video (LA Court Connect) or in person is mandatory or sanctions will be issued against counsel for Defendant. 

The Court continues the hearing on Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.’s (“Plaintiff”) Motion for Order That Matters in Requests for Admission of Truth of Facts Be Deemed Admitted to October 19, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Department Y of the Norwalk Courthouse.  The Court also notes that Plaintiff failed to provide notice of the continued hearing to Defendant at the address listed in the Order Granting Defendant’s Motion to be Relieved as Counsel as ordered by the Court on July 28, 2026.

Clerk to give notice to all parties and counsel, including to Defendant.
