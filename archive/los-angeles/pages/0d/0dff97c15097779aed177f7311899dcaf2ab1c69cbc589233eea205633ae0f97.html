#12 - SHAVIV DELKHAH vs TESLA MOTORS, INC.

Plaintiff’s motion to compel further responses to Requests for Admission, Set One, is GRANTED IN PART and DENIED IN PART.  The motion is denied as to Requests for Admission Nos. 26 through 31, 34 through 40, and 80 through 82.  The motion is otherwise granted.

Plaintiff’s motion to compel further responses to Special Interrogatories, Set One, is GRANTED IN PART and DENIED IN PART.  The motion is denied as to Special Interrogatories Nos. 14 through 22 and 32 through 38.  The motion is otherwise granted.

Within 20 days of service of this order, Tesla shall serve verified further responses to all discovery requests as to which the motions are granted.  Those further responses shall comply with Code of Civil Procedure sections 2033.220 and 2030.220.  Tesla may not reassert the same boilerplate objections.  If Tesla has already served a fully code-compliant supplemental response to any item covered by this ruling, no duplicative further response is required as to that item.

Because both motions are granted in part, and because the meet-and-confer record is disputed, the Court finds that a sanctions award to either side would be unjust.

Moving Party is to give notice.
