Case Number: 25NNCV02634    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: X

#5 - HAI J. WONG vs GARABED OUSTAYAN, et al.

MOTION TO INTERVENE AND TO EXPUNGE LIS PENDENS

A.  PRD LLC is entitled to intervene for the limited purpose of moving to expunge the lis pendens.

PRD LLC has shown a direct interest in the property affected by the lis pendens. Lai declares that PRD LLC acquired the property at foreclosure and that the lis pendens remains recorded against it. Because the lis pendens burdens the property, disposition of the matter without PRD LLC’s participation could impair PRD LLC’s ability to protect its claimed interest.

PRD LLC states it discovered the lis pendens shortly after obtaining possession in March 2026, attempted informal resolution by the end of March 2026, and filed its motion on May 20, 2026. Wong identifies no prejudice from intervention and instead expressly does not oppose it. 

Accordingly, the motion to intervene is GRANTED, limited to the lis pendens issue presented in the motion.

B.  The lis pendens should be expunged.

Whatever the operative complaint may originally have alleged, Wong now expressly states that she does not oppose expungement and is not seeking recovery of title to, ownership of, or possession of the Arcadia property. She further states that her remaining claims concern alleged wrongful conduct and financial damages. (Response, ¶ 5.)

Wong has not carried her burden to establish the probable validity of any presently asserted real property claim supporting continued maintenance of the lis pendens. Her current position disclaims the very title- or possession-based relief that would justify the notice. To the extent her remaining claims are damages-based, a lis pendens is not a proper device to secure those claims.

The motion to expunge is therefore GRANTED.

The notice of pendency of action recorded as Los Angeles Recorder’s No. 20250273748 against the real property commonly known as 2223 S. 2nd Avenue, Arcadia, California 91006, APN 5789-018-040, is EXPUNGED.

This order resolves only the intervention and lis pendens issues. It does not adjudicate the merits of Wong’s remaining claims against any existing defendant.

Moving Party is to give notice.

Case Number: 25NNCV04145    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: X

#8 - KEVA DILORENZO PAGE vs 713 E. ALTADENA DRIVE

Defendants Ahmed Hassan, Gail Cargill, and Raina Hassan’s motion to quash service of Plaintiff’s First Amended Complaint is GRANTED.  Service of the First Amended Complaint on those moving Defendants is quashed.

Much of Plaintiff’s opposition addresses the merits of the underlying dispute and the parties’ competing factual accusations. Those issues are not before the Court on this motion.  This motion is confined to personal jurisdiction and service, not the merits of Plaintiff’s claims. 

The Court finds its May 26, 2026 ruling did not decide service of the First Amended Complaint.  The opposition asserts the motion is “moot” because it was already resolved by the Court’s prior May 26, 2026 order.  (Opp., p. 2.)  The May 26, 2026 order says otherwise, as it expressly states that the earlier motion concerned the original complaint, that the earlier motion was moot because that complaint was no longer operative, and that the Court was not deciding whether service of the First Amended Complaint was effective.  The minute order also specifically identifies the later motion to quash service of the First Amended Complaint as a separate motion not decided at that hearing.  Accordingly, the instant motion is properly before the Court.

As for the service theory advanced in the opposition—that the First Amended Complaint was mailed to defense counsel and received by counsel’s office—the theory fails. (Opp., pp. 2-3.)  Plaintiff relies on that mailing and on counsel’s prior participation in the case.  However, Defendants expressly state that counsel never agreed to accept service, that Defendants have appeared only specially, and that no general appearance has been made. (Reply, p. 3.)

The Court finds Plaintiff’s showing is insufficient to establish effective service of the First Amended Complaint on these moving Defendants. Receipt of mailed papers by counsel, coupled with a special appearance to challenge jurisdiction, does not establish authorized acceptance of service here.  The motion is, therefore, granted.

Moving Parties are to give notice.
