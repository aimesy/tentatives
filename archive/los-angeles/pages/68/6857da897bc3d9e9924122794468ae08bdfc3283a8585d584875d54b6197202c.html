Case Number: 21STCV10693    Hearing Date: July 28, 2026    Dept: 300

CASE NUMBER:                  21STCV10693

CASE NAME:                        Derick Collins vs. Wheels Labs, Inc.

TYPE OF MOTION:              Motion to be Relieved as Counsel

FILING PARTY:                    Counsel for Defendant Wheels Labs, Inc. – Paul K. Schrieffer

 

RESPONDING PARTY:       Plaintiff Derick Collins

 

HEARING DATE:                 July 28, 2026

Instant Pleading

Counsel for Defendant Wheels Labs, Inc., Paul Schrieffer moves to be relieved as counsel.

Discussion

On April 16, 2026, the matter came for hearing and was continued to July 28, 2026.

As the Court stated in its prior ruling on Counsel’s motion to be relieved as counsel “the Court cannot grant this motion because it would leave Defendant without legal representation. Counsels Schrieffer…and/or Defendant itself must arrange for qualified replacement counsel to substitute in as counsel of record.” (See 08/20/2025 Order.) Such reasoning is applicable to the instant motion. Further, trial is less than one month away.

Conclusion

Based on the foregoing, the motion to be relieved as counsel is DENIED.
