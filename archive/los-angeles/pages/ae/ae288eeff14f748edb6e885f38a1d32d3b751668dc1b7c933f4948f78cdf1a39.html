DEPARTMENT 316 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 22STCV26421    Hearing Date: August 4, 2026    Dept: 316

The Court received the update that the deposition went forward on July 14, 2026. If the parties are satisfied, the motion will come off calendar.  If the moving party continues to seek sanctions, the Court will hear argument. 

Case Number: 25STCP03449    Hearing Date: August 4, 2026    Dept: 316

Both motions are granted.  Moving party is to submit a proposed order for each motion.

Sanctions are granted against debtors Kang, Salas and Flagstaff jointly and severally in the following amounts: for the motion seeking responses to interrogatories, sanctions are awarded in the amount of $2,955, representing 4 hours for counsel Brisbois at the requested rate of $495 per hour and 1.5 hours for counsel Coate at the requested rate of $650 per hour. On the motion seeking the production of documents, sanctions are awarded in the amount of $3,945, representing 6 hours for counsel Brisbois at the rate of $495 per hour and 2.5 hours for counsel Coata at the rate of $650 per hour. The Court finds the requested rates reasonable and has subtracted the time sought for review of the opposition and preparation of a reply brief as no opposition briefs were filed. The Court has also reduced the hearing appearance time in light of the lack of opposition papers.
