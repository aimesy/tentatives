DEPARTMENT T LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

If ALL parties submit on the tentative, then no appearance is necessary unless some other matter (i.e., Case Management Conference) is on calendar. It is not necessary to call the court to request oral argument. Oral argument is permitted on all tentative rulings.

Case Number: 25VECV00149    Hearing Date: July 16, 2026    Dept: T

25VECV00149 ARMEN TSATURYAN, BY AND THR... vs TARZANA MEDICAL CENTER

Ex parte application by plaintiff to advance hearings on motions to enforce the 6/9/2026 order for further responses to special and form interrogatories: Deny.  The court failed to set a compliance date in the order of 6/9/2026.  Upon discovering the error, the court issued an order on 7/10/2026 nunc pro tunc setting the compliance date as 7/31/2026. 

However, the court did not take into account that plaintiff apparently wanted the discovery responses before the depositions of the nurses. Given that supplemental responses were served with objections and without a verification, the court now orders (1) verifications to delivered by email to plaintiff’s counsel by noon on 7/19/2026 and (2) objections stricken, without waiting for the hearing on the motions.
