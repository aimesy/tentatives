20NWLC19934: ABSOLUTE RESOLUTIONS INVESTMENTS, LLC vs FRANCISCO ORTIZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Absolute Resolutions Investments, LLC (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Settlement Agreement with Mutual Release and Stipulation for Entry of Judgment Upon Default (“Stipulation”) signed by Defendant on October 7, 2020 and filed with the Court on September 16, 2021.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Court’s Order Retaining Jurisdiction Of the Case Under C.C.P. § 664.6 and Dismissing entered by the Court on November 5, 2021.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Case Number: 25NWLC09224    Hearing Date: September 17, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC09224: FIRST NATIONAL BANK OF OMAHA, A NATIONAL BANKING ASSOCIATION vs MARCUS W MASSEY

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed all moving documents filed by Plaintiff First National Bank of Omaha, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its motion for summary judgment, including the separate statement and supporting evidence. 

Plaintiff’s motion for summary judgment was filed on June 12, 2026 and set for hearing on August 17, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Marcus W. Massey (“Defendant”).

The Court finds that Plaintiff has met the initial burden of showing that there are no defenses to its cause of action for breach of contract by proving each element of the cause of action entitling Plaintiff to summary judgment. 

Plaintiff has shown with admissible evidence that Defendant entered into a personal loan contract with Plaintiff and Plaintiff provided Defendant with personal loan proceeds in the amount of $30,000, paid directly to Defendant; Plaintiff rendered and mailed statements of account to Defendant; Defendant made payments on the personal loan account and made no objections to the statements sent to him; Defendant stopped making payments on the personal loan account on December 13, 2024; and Defendant still owes Plaintiff a balance of $24,629.41 on the account.  (Plaintiff’s Separate Statement, Undisputed Material Facts Nos. [UMF] 1-5.)   The Court notes that Separate Statement Undisputed Material Fact No. 4 appears to have a typo indicating that Defendant stopped making payments on “December 13, 2025,” but the evidence demonstrates that Defendant stopped making payments in 2024.  The last account statement that was sent to Defendant was in January 2025.

The burden having shifted to Defendant, the Court finds Defendant has failed to set forth specific facts showing that a triable issue of material fact exists as to any of the elements of the causes of action for breach of contract or that there is any defense thereto required by Code of Civil Procedure section 437c(p)(1). 

The Court finds that Plaintiff is entitled to judgment as a matter of law and GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion for Summary Judgment as to its cause of action for breach of contract. 

Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff First National Bank of Omaha, N.A. and against Defendant Marcus W. Massey in the principal sum of $24,629.41, plus reasonable attorney’s fees and costs. 

Plaintiff must submit a Memorandum of Costs and a declaration in support of reasonable attorney’s fees as well as a Proposed Judgment for the Court’s review and signature.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 26NWLC04000    Hearing Date: September 17, 2026    Dept: Y

26NWLC04000: CAPITAL ONE, N.A., SUCCESSOR BY MERGER TO DISCOVER BANK vs RYAN VACA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the Motion to Set Aside Default and Default Judgment (“Motion”) filed by Defendant Ryan Vaca (“Defendant”) on August 26, 2026.  Defendant served the Motion on Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”).  No opposition has been filed by Plaintiff.   

The Court notes that Default was entered by the Clerk on July 14, 2026.  Default Judgment was entered by the Clerk on August 3, 2026.  

The Court notes that Proof of Service of Summons by a registered process server by personal service was filed with the Court on February 19, 2026 creating a presumption of service under Evidence Code section 647.  Defendant’s declaration, which was signed under penalty of perjury, states that Proof of Service indicates personal service on a “brown-haired white female”; however, Defendant is male.  Defendant also states that he does not live at the address identified in the Proof of Service and does not know who lives there.  Defendant further provides evidence (Uber trip details) demonstrating that he was not at that address at the time of service.

Here, the Court finds that Defendant has rebutted the presumption of service and that there was no actual notice. “Actual notice,” within the meaning of Code of Civil Procedure § 473.5, means genuine knowledge by the defendant, and has been strictly construed.  Relief under the statute is liberally granted to defendants so that cases may be resolved on their merits.  Ellard v. Conway (2001) 94 Cal.App.4th 540, 547-548.     

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure §§ 473(d) and  473.5, the Court will GRANT Defendant’s Motion and set aside the Default Judgment. 

The Court grants Defendant 30 days leave to file a responsive pleading with the Court and serve it on Plaintiff.

The Court will set a OSC Re Status of Pleadings on September 17, 2027 at 8:30 a.m. in Department Y of the Norwalk Courthouse.  The Court anticipates that this date will become the trial date when the responsive pleading is filed by Defendant.

Clerk to give notice.
