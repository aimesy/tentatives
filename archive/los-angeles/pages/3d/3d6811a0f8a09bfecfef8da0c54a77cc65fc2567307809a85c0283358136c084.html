DEPARTMENT 300 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24STCV27402    Hearing Date: August 24, 2026    Dept: 300

CASE NAME:                        CYMA Design, Inc. vs. Henry Mask, LLC, et al.

TYPE OF MOTION:              Motion to be Relieved as Counsel

FILING PARTY:                    Counsel for Defendant Patrick Henry – Miller Law Partners, PC

 

RESPONDING PARTY:       Unopposed

 

HEARING DATE:                 August 24, 2026

 

Instant Motion

Counsel moves to be relieved as counsel.

Discussion

Counsel completed the required forms. Those forms indicate that Counsel served Defendant Patrick Henry at his last known address which Counsel confirmed was current via email, at least 30 days prior to the hearing on this matter.

Counsel states that there has been a breakdown in the attorney client relationship such that continued representation is not possible. Counsel states that, among other things, Defendant Patrick Henry has failed to fulfill his obligations under the applicable attorney-client agreement.

Trial in this matter is not yet set.

Conclusion

Based on the foregoing, the motion is GRANTED, effective upon the filing of the proof of service of the signed order upon the client.
