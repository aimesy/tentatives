BACKGROUND

 

This action arises from the alleged constructive eviction of Plaintiffs from their apartment. On August 5, 2024, Plaintiffs Sandy Esparza (“Petitioner”), Jose Esparza, Crystal Esparza (“Crystal”), and Sean Esparza (“Sean”) (collectively, “Plaintiffs”), filed the Complaint against Defendants Legacy Partners Waterstone LP (“Legacy Partners”) and Does 1 through 20, alleging the following causes of action: (1) negligence, (2) breach of implied warranty of habitability, and (3) constructive eviction. On October 24, 2024, Plaintiffs filed a fictitious name amendment to the Complaint, naming Waterstone Owner LP (“Waterstone”) as Doe 1. On May 19, 2025, at the request of Plaintiffs, Legacy Partners was dismissed from the action.

 

On January 6, 2025, Waterstone filed an Answer.

 

On June 8, 2026, Plaintiffs filed a Notice of Settlement of Entire Case.

 

On May 29, 2026, Petitioner filed the instant Petition for Approval of Compromise of Claim on behalf of Sean (the “Petition”).

 

No opposition or objection to the Petition has been filed.

After thoroughly reviewing the Petition and supporting documents, the Court is satisfied that the proposed settlement is fair, reasonable, and in the best interests of the minor. However, the proposed order, Judicial Council Form MC-351, is incongruent with the Petition.

 

The Court accordingly GRANTS the Petition, conditioned upon Petitioner submitting an amended order within five days. Upon timely compliance, the Court will enter the order without further hearing.

 

CONCLUSION 

 

The Petition for Approval of Compromise of Claim, filed by Petitioner Sandy Esparza on behalf of Plaintiff and Claimant, Sean Esparza is GRANTED, conditioned upon Petitioner submitting an amended Judicial Council Form MC-351 within five days.
