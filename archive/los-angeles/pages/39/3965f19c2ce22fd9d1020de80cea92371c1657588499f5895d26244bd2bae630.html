DEPARTMENT S25 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

Imed Hsini v. Mohsen Ghariani, et al.
LASC Case No. 24LBCV00194
Hearing Date: September 11, 2024
Request for Default Judgment by Court
 
 
In preparation for the September 11, 2024 default judgment hearing, the court has identified several concerns with Plaintiff’s request: 
 

  1. Request for Entry of Default was entered on May 9, 2024 which is less than 30 days after service of the summons and complaint to Defendant Danielle Canissa and CDW Logistics, LLC. (C.C.P. §587);
 
  1. Plaintiff checked the wrong box and should have checked “Court Judgment” in the “Request For” section on the 1st page. (See 7/29/2024 Request for Entry of Default/Judgment);
 
  1. No DOEs dismissal filed;
 
  1. No attached/adequate and/or authenticated exhibits provided with Plaintiff’s Declaration. Exhibits should demonstrate, e.g. costs for title and insurance for two vehicles, power of attorney, agreement entered into with Defendants to purchase trucks and provide logistic and shipping services to clients;
 
  1. No contractual or statutory basis provided by Plaintiff as to recovery of attorney fees. Also, attorney fees requested is more than what is allowed in a contract for recovery of reasonable attorney fees under Local Rule of the Court, rule 3.214(a);  
 
  1. Prayer for complaint does not specify damages claimed;
 
  1. No notice of punitive damages as required under Code Civ. Proc., § 425.11, subd. (c).  Appears Plaintiff may be waiving punitive damages based upon proposed judgment;
 
  1. Proposed Judgment (UD-100) incorrect calculations as to attorney fees and prejudgment interest calculations.

Case Number: 24LBCV01506    Hearing Date: July 7, 2026    Dept: S25

Motion:  Plaintiff, Elijah Dior Duque, a minor, by and through his Guardian ad Litem, Angelina Duque’s Petition to Approve Minor’s Compromise  

Moving Party:  Plaintiff, Elijah Dior Duque, a minor, by and through his Guardian ad Litem, Angelina Duque (“Plaintiff”) 

Responding Party:  None 

 

Tentative Ruling 

 

The Court has reviewed the proposed settlement and finds that the settlement itself is fair and reasonable.  The minor claimant here has agreed to dismiss his claims; Defendant Norma Ann Dawson will pay $7,499.99 to minor claimant.  (Pet. ¶ 10.a.)  Plaintiff Jose Jesus Chavez Moreno will receive $74,999.99 and Plaintiff Maricela Chavez will receive $149,999.99.  (Pet. ¶ 11.a.5.)  The amount of attorney’s fees requested, representing 25 percent of the gross settlement to minor claimant, is $1,874.99.  (Pet. ¶ 13.a, Attachment 13a [Shapiro Declaration] ¶ 7.)  The minor claimant, through her guardian agreed to a 45 percent contingency fee arrangement after the first 90 days of the attorney’s representation of minor claimant; however, Plaintiff’s counsel is seeking less than the agreed upon 40 percent.  (Pet., 13.a., Attachment 13a ¶, 7, Attachment 17a [Retainer Agreement] ¶ 6(b).)  The petition does not seek any medical expenses.  (Pet. ¶ 12.)  The petition further seeks $1,270.61 for expenses (other than medical expenses) that have been incurred or advanced.  (Pet. ¶ 13.b, Attachment 13a ¶ 8, Ex. A.) Claimant will receive $4,354.99 after payment of all fees and expenses. The Court finds that Plaintiff’s counsel has set sufficient facts breaking down and explaining the legal fees and costs incurred in litigating this matter.  (Cal. Rules of Court, rule 7.955(b).)   

 

Plaintiff, ELIJAH DIOR DUQUE, a minor, by and through his Guardian ad Litem, Angelina Duque’s Petition to Approve Minor’s Compromise is GRANTED.
