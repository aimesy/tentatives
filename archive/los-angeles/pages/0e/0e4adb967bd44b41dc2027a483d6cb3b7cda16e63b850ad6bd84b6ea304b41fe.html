DEPARTMENT 735 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24STCV06372    Hearing Date: June 26, 2026    Dept: 735

Maurice, et al. v. Town and Country Moving and Storage, Inc., et al.

Plaintiffs’ Motion for Leave to File a First Amended Complaint

 

BACKGROUND 

On March 14, 2024, Plaintiffs Peter Maurice and Curtis Dikes filed a form complaint against Defendant Town and Country Moving and Storage, Inc., asserting causes of action for negligence and breach of contract. On November 18, 2025, Plaintiffs filed a DOE Amendment substituting Leigh Edward Mace as a Doe Defendant in this action.

DISCUSSION

Plaintiffs move for leave to file a first amended complaint to add causes of action for fraud, negligent misrepresentation, and intentional infliction of emotional distress. Plaintiffs assert that discovery has revealed evidence concerning Defendants’ lack of inventory and tracking systems, improper storage conditions, mishandling and alteration of Plaintiffs’ valuable property, and representations made regarding Defendants’ qualifications and capabilities in handling luxury residential moves and specialty items. Plaintiffs comply with CRC Rule 3.1324(b).

In opposition, Defendants argue that Plaintiffs have unreasonably delayed in seeking amendment and argue that the proposed amendment significantly alters the legal theories and available remedies in the action. Defendants also assert that the claims do not relate back to the original complaint and are thus time barred.

The Court will allow amendment. Delay alone is not sufficient grounds for denial. The proposed additions arise from the same events and transactions as those in the original complaint. Trial has been continued to February 2027 and thus Defendants will not be prejudiced by amendment. Defendant’s arguments regarding the statute of limitations cannot be adjudicated on this motion and must be addressed via demurrer, summary judgment or trial. The Court grants Plaintiffs’ motion.

CONCLUSION

            The Court grants Plaintiffs’ motion for leave to file a first amended complaint.

Plaintiffs to give notice.
