DEPARTMENT 300 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24STCV24662    Hearing Date: August 10, 2026    Dept: 300

CASE NUMBER:                  24STCV24662          

CASE NAME:                        Alexis Rogelio Rocha Godina vs. Garfield Beach CVS, LLC, et al.

TYPE OF MOTION:              Motion to Bifurcate

FILING PARTY:                    Defendants Garfield Beach CVS, LLC, Evelyn Moreno, and Victoria Ribera

 

RESPONDING PARTY:       Unopposed

 

HEARING DATE:                 August 10, 2026

Instant Motion:

Defendants move the Court, pursuant to Civil Code section 3295(d) and Code of Civil Procedure sections 598 and 1048(b), for an order bifurcating trial into two phases: (1) a liability and compensatory damages phase and (2) a punitive-damages phase, to be held only if warranted. The motion is unopposed.

On March 27, 2026, the Court granted Defendants motion for summary adjudication as to the issue of punitive damages as it relates to Defendant CVS. (See 03/27/2026 Minute Order.) However, the Court finds that punitive damages remain live against individual Defendants Moreno and Ribera.

Tentative Decision:

The Court finds that bifurcation is mandatory under section 3295(d) and, alternatively, warranted under sections 598 and 1048(b).

The motion to bifurcate is GRANTED.


Case Number: 25STCV16050    Hearing Date: August 10, 2026    Dept: 300

CASE NUMBER:                  25STCV16050                      

CASE NAME:                        Carolyn Lakey, et al. vs. Interinsurance Exchange of the Automobile Club of Southern California

TYPE OF MOTION:              (1) Motion to Compel Respondent Kenny Fuller’s Responses to Special Interrogatories, Set No. One, and Request for Monetary Sanctions. 

                                                (2) Motion to Compel Respondent Kenny Fuller’s Responses to Form Interrogatories, Set No. One, and Request for Monetary Sanctions. 

                                                (3) Motion to Compel Respondent Kenny Fuller’s Responses to Request for Production of Documents, Set No. One, and Request for Monetary Sanctions.             

                                                (4) Motion to Compel Respondent Kenny Fuller’s Responses to Requests for Admissions, Set No. One, and Request for Monetary Sanctions. 

FILING PARTY:                    (1)-(4) Petitioner Interinsurance Exchange of the Automobile Club of Southern California

 

RESPONDING PARTY:       (1)-(4) Unopposed

 

HEARING DATE:                 August 10, 2026

Motions (1)-(4)

Petitioner moves the Court for an order compelling Respondent Kenny Fuller (Fuller) to provide verified responses to Petitioners: (1) Special Interrogatories, Set No. One; (2) Form Interrogatories, Set No. One; (3) Requests for Production, Set No. One; and (4) Requests for Admissions, Set No. One. Additionally, Petitioner seeks sanctions in the total amount of $3,840.00.

Tentative Decision

The motions are GRANTED. Fuller is ORDERED to serve verified responses, without objections within 30 days. The truth of the matters specified in the Requests for Admission be deemed admitted as to Fuller.

Fuller is ORDERED to pay $1,040.00 in sanctions, payable within 30 days.
