Case Number: 25NWLC44335    Hearing Date: July 20, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff, Kinecta Federal Credit Union ("Plaintiff"), including counsel's declaration and the exhibits. No opposition has been filed by Defendant, James Steven Litsis ("Defendant"). The Court finds that Plaintiff served Defendant with Requests for Production of Documents (Set One) by mail on February 13, 2026, and has provided proof of service establishing proper service. Defendant has not served any responses to the discovery requests. On April 15, 2026, Plaintiff served its Motion to Compel Responses to Discovery by mail at the same address used for the discovery requests. The proof of service for the discovery request and the motion reflect the same address identified by Defendant in his Answer.
Having considered the moving papers and Defendant's failure to file an opposition or serve any requested documents, Plaintiff's Motion to Compel and Requests for Production of Documents (Set One) is GRANTED. Defendant shall respond to Requests for Production of Documents (Set One). The Court has also considered Plaintiff's request for monetary sanctions in the amount of $850.50 for the motion. 
The Court finds the requested attorney's fees to be unreasonable and disproportionate in the context of this collection action. The Court further finds that, pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 2031.300(c), "other circumstances make the imposition of the sanction unjust."
Those circumstances include, but are not limited to, the fact that the information and documents sought by Plaintiff appear to consist largely of information already known to Plaintiff, documents already in Plaintiff's possession, and/or information or documents that are unlikely to bear on the resolution of the issues presented in this action. The Court also notes that on Plaintiff's Request for Admissions was granted and the admissions were admitted on May 8, 2026. Under these circumstances, the Court concludes that an award of monetary sanctions would be unjust. Accordingly, Plaintiff's request for monetary sanctions is DENIED.
Plaintiff shall submit a revised proposed order consistent with this ruling.
Plaintiff shall give notice.
