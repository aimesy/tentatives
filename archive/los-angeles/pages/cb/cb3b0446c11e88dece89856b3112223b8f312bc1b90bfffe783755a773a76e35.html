GENERAL INFORMATION

Court Address: 6320 Sylmar Avenue, Van Nuys, CA 91401 (Van Nuys East Building)

Courtroom Telephone Number: (818) 901-4732

Courtroom Hours: 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM (Lunch Hour 12:00 PM - 1:30 PM)

Ex parte applications are heard Monday-Thursday at 8:30 a.m.  

Court website: www.lacourt.ca.gov

 

EX PARTE APPLICATIONS: Ex parte applications are heard Monday-Thursday at 8:30 a.m. All ex parte applications require a proposed order, which shall be submitted separate from the ex parte application. Ex parte applications are reviewed in chambers. Oral oppositions will be considered at the discretion of the Court.

• Represented litigants: All ex parte applications and documents in support thereof must be electronically filed no later than 10:00 a.m. the court day before the ex parte hearing.

• Self-Represented Litigants: May submit ex parte applications up until  8:30 a.m. the day of the hearing.  NOTE: Rules pertaining to notice and service of all ex party documents apply to Self Represented Litgants.

 

REMOTE APPEARANCES: 

·         IF YOU APPEAR REMOTELY, YOU ARE REQUIRED TO USE YOUR VIDEO CAMERA and REGISTER WITH YOUR NAME ON THE SCREEN, NOT JUST A PHONE NUMBER

·         Do not appear while driving

·         Present yourself professionally - this is a court appearance

MORNING CALENDAR/ COURT HEARINGS: The court starts check-in for morning calendar at 8:30 am and starts daily proceedings thereafter. Please be on time for check-in.

COURTESY AND CIVILITY BY COUNSEL: At all times counsel’s conduct before the court, in writing, in communications and with regard to each other and self-represented litigants shall be professional, polite, courteous, and respectful. Counsel are required at all times to comply with local rules and State Bar rules on civility and professionalism.

 

FILINGS: The Los Angeles County Superior Court has implemented electronic filing of all documents filed in the Limited and Unlimited Non-Complex Civil matters by litigants represented by attorneys pursuant to the operative General Order re Mandatory Electronic Filing for Civil. Pursuant to California Rules of Court, Rule 2.253(b), represented litigants are required to electronically file documents with the Court through an approved Electronic Filing Service Provider.

Pursuant to California Rules of Court, Rule 2.253(b)(2), self-represented litigants are exempt from mandatory Electronic Filing requirements and papers may be filed at the filing window or electronically.

TENTATIVE RULINGS: The Court may issue tentative rulings before law and motion hearings. To view tentative rulings please visit the court’s website, www.lacourt.gov.ca.

COURTESY COPIES are only required if a document is e-filed within 2 court days of scheduled hearing pursuant to the General Order re: Electronic Filing.

COMPLAINTS AND ANSWERS AS ATTACHMENTS: All complaints and answers (or petitions and responses) must be separately filed (efiled if by counsel). Complaints (or amended complaints) and answers (or amended answers) cannot be “deemed” filed as an attachment to another document.

NOTICE OF RELATED CASES: The Court requires timely compliance with California Rules of Court 3-300 regarding Notice of Related Cases.

CONTINUANCES: Requests for continuances of hearings or trials may be considered upon written stipulation and order. A stipulation and order shall state the date the complaint was filed, proposed continuance date(s), grounds showing good cause for the continuance, and be submitted with payment of the stipulation and order fee and first appearance fee, if applicable,  of all parties to the stipulation. If there is no stipulation, the parties may seek a continuance by noticed motion or an ex parte application.  Failure to timely and diligently conduct discovery or timely file motions will rarely be considered good cause.

EXPIRATION OF 5 YEAR TIME TO BRING CASE TO TRIAL: Parties are required at all times to be aware of the expiration of the 5 year time limitation to bring their case to trial.

SELF REPRESENTED LITIGANTS: Information for self-represented litigants may be found on the Los Angeles Superior Court website www.lacourt.org, at the Judicial Branch of California’s website https://www.courts.ca.gov/selfhelp.htm or the LA County Law Library website https://www.lalawlibrary.org/selfhelp/faq.

ADA REQUESTS:  Please complete the online form requesting ADA Accommodations at www.lacourt.org

COURT REPORTERS: The court does not provide court reporters unless there is a fee waiver and timely request for a reporter. Even so, there is still no guarantee that the court will have a reporter available under those circumstances. Any party otherwise seeking the presence of a court reporter must secure the service of a private court reporter with a proposed order designating the court reporter as the official reporter. The court must be in receipt of the order prior to the hearing.

EMAILS: From time to time counsel/parties may receive emails from the courtroom. However, you may not email the department without prior court approval. The courtroom email is not regularly monitored.

 

 

TRIAL AND FINAL STATUS CONFERENCE SCHEDULING

Trials are typically set MONDAYs at 9:30 a.m. Final Status Conferences are typically set 10 days before the trial at 9:30 a.m.

Cases assigned to the P.I. Pathway program are assigned a trial date upon filing.  Please be aware of the date as trial dates will not be continued absent unforeseen good cause.

The courtroom is usually dark for trials on Fridays, but this may be modified if needed for timely completion of the trial.

 

 

HEARINGS:

CASE MANAGEMENT CONFERENCES: CMC Statements must be filed and served no later than 15 calendar days prior to the CMC per CRC 3.725. Meet and confer per CRC 3.724 and 3.727 is required prior to each CMC.

MOTIONS: All motions to be heard in Department I are to be reserved through the Court Reservation System.  Follow the links Online Services – Court Reservation System (CRS) on the court’s website. Motions are set for hearing at 8:30 AM.

Self Represented Parties may use the Court Reservation System or contact the courtroom for hearing dates.

Plan ahead. It may take up to 4 months to reserve a date for MSJ/MSAI.

The Court may issue a written tentative ruling, please check www.lacourt.gov.ca .

MOTIONS FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT AND/OR ADJUDICATION:  The Court will rarely continue the Trial date to hear any Motions for Summary Judgment and/or Adjudication. Therefore, any Motions for Summary Judgment and/or Adjudication MUST BE TIMELY FILED AND SERVED.

MOTIONS TO WITHDRAW AS COUNSEL: Please be sure to include on the proposed order an address, telephone number and email address of the client(s). Orders are effective only upon filing the proof of service of the Order Granting Motion to be Relieved on all parties and the client(s).

INFORMAL DISCOVERY CONFERENCES: The Court will hold an Informal Discovery Conference (IDC) on a case-by-case basis. There is no requirement to hold an IDC prior to filing a discovery motion. Please call the courtroom to schedule the IDC.  If the Court holds an IDC a joint statement of discovery in dispute (no more than five pages – excluding exhibits) shall be filed five days prior.    

An IDC will not be held for Song-Beverly cases.

POST MEDIATION STATUS CONFERENCE:  Parties are to meet and confer and file a joint status report 5 court days prior to the hearing.   The Status Report shall advise the Court of the status of ADR and fact discovery (vehicle inspection as well for Song-Beverly actions); anticipated pre-trial motions, if any; trial time estimate; and lead trial counsel mutual availability in months 18 – 24 from filing.  

TRIAL SETTING CONFERENCES

At least 5 court days in advance of the Trial Setting conference, parties are ordered to meet and confer and file a Joint Status Report.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

FINAL STATUS CONFERENCE ORDERS:

LEAD TRIAL COUNSEL ARE TO ATTEND THE FSC IN PERSON WITH TRIAL BINDERS

A final time estimate for the trial will be discussed at the FSC, be aware of which witnesses will be called and their time estimate.

Binders are to be provided at the FSC as follows:

BINDER ONE:

1. Joint Witness List

2. Joint Exhibit List

Include any objections and grounds

Include columns for date identified and date admitted

Include columns for stipulation,  authenticity and admissibility

3. Joint Statement of the Case

4. Joint Proposed Special Verdict Form OR each side’s proposed verdict form

5. List of Joint and Contested Jury Instructions

6. Printed Jury instruction -  fully edited

7. Briefs (if any)

If there is a lack of cooperation in preparing joint documents, please file individual documents with a declaration explaining why joint documents are not provided.

BINDER TWO – MOTIONS IN LIMINE

Briefing schedules for Motions in Limine will be based on Code of Civil Procedure section 1005(b) using the Final Status Conference as the hearing date.  The Court reserves the discretion to disregard untimely Motions in Limine.

The notebook is to contain an index of each parties MILs, oppositions & replies

After the Court’s ruling on a MIL all parties and witnesses must be advised of the court rulings and that they must comply with all court orders.

 

DEPARTMENT TRIAL ORDERS

Provide exhibit notebooks (on the first day of trial)

EXHIBIT NOTEBOOKS FOR TRIAL: 

All exhibits MUST be placed in binders under tabs, and each page must be numbered (bate stamped) within each tab.

Bring 5 Sets of Exhibit Books, tabbed and indexed:                                                         One set for the judicial assistant, one set for the judge, one set for the witness box and each side to have a copy

The exhibit list must be e-filed and included in the binders

 

Depositions must be lodged with the Clerk of the Court prior to the start of trial.

Notify the Court who will be paying for the jury fees.  Jury fees are to be paid forthwith when directed by the Judicial Assistant

If applicable, counsel are to meet and confer on the number of "sides" for purposes of calculating peremptory challenges during jury selection to discuss at the FSC

No use of Golden Rule or Reptile theory

No use of specific dollar amounts during voir dire

No mention of insurance

The Court will issue additional trial orders at the Final Status Conference

 Failure to timely comply will court orders may result in monetary sanctions in the sum of $250 against counsel of record for violation of court orders, CCP section 177.5
