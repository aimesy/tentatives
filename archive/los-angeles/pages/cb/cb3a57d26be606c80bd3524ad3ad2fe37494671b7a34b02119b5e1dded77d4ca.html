MOTION

Petitions for Approval of Minors’ Compromises

MOVING PARTY

Petitioner Ron Levi

OPPOSING PARTY

None

 

The Court has reviewed the Petitions filed on behalf of Petitioner Ron Levi (“Petitioner”) regarding Claimants Adam Levi and Adele Levi (“Claimants”) and continues the matter.  The Court notes the Petitions represent the total amount of the settlement appears to be $215,000, and Claimants will receive $5,000 each, while Petitioner and Plaintiff Hila Palatchi will receive $205,000. 

Additionally, in the Petitions on file, there is a mismatch of the minors’ names on the face of the Petitions and the identification of the claimant in Paragraph 11 of the Petitions. Petitioner must correct this apparent error in the Petitions, accordingly. 

Petitioner is to give notice of the Court’s ruling and file proof of service of same.
