DEPARTMENT 735 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV05259    Hearing Date: August 11, 2026    Dept: 735

Christopher Gallivan v. Los Angeles Orthopedic Surgery Specialists, et al.

Demurrer to Complaint

 

Plaintiff alleges he sought medical care from Defendants on December 1, 2023, but does not allege the reason he required medical attention, the medical services that defendants performed, and the nature of Plaintiff’s injury. Doe defendant Regal Medical Group, Inc. Demurs to the complaint for uncertainty.

            Plaintiff contends he alleged sufficient ultimate facts to state a cause of action. But Regal is not demurring pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 430.10 subdivision (e) for failure to state a claim. Instead, Defendant is demurring because Plaintiff’s bare allegations that Plaintiff suffered an unidentified injury as a result of unidentified acts of negligence for an unidentified medical issue do not give notice to Defendant – or this court – what happened and what the alleged negligence was.

CONCLUSION 

The court sustains Defendant Regal’s demurrer with 20 days’ leave to amend.

Defendant Regal shall give notice.
