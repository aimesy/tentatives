DEPARTMENT P LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 23NWCV02182    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: P

This premises liability action was filed on July 13, 2023. On February 5, 2026, during a Non-Appearance Case Review Re: Bankruptcy, the Court ordered the complaint filed by Plaintiff Helen Marie Stephens (Plaintiff) dismissed without prejudice. Plaintiff has a Motion to Set Aside Default scheduled to be heard on January 7, 2027. Plaintiff now applies ex parte to set aside this dismissal and reinstate the complaint or, in the alternative, to shorten time to hear Plaintiff’s Motion to Set Aside. Plaintiff argues she did not cause the dismissal and should not have to suffer a terminating sanction.

 

Plaintiff’s ex parte application to set aside the dismissal is DENIED without prejudice. The Court does not adjudicate the merits of the Motion to Set Aside on an ex parte basis.

 

Plaintiff’s alternative request, to advance the hearing date on the Motion to Set Aside, is GRANTED. Plaintiff’s Motion to Set Aside is ADVANCED to today’s date and CONTINUED to Thursday, September 17, 2026 at 9:30 AM in Dept. P. Opposition and reply to be filed and served per Code in accordance with the new hearing date. Moving party to give notice

Case Number: 24NWCV01862     Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: P

This sexual harassment action was filed on June 17, 2024. Trial is scheduled to begin on November 30, 2026. Defendant Twin Pelican Surgical Group’s (Defendant) Motion to Compel an IME of Plaintiff Lorena Cuevas (Motion to Compel IME) is scheduled to be heard after trial, on February 11, 2027. Defendant now applies ex parte to shorten time to hear its Motion to Compel IME to August 21, 2026. Defendant argues good cause exists because hearing the Motion to Compel IME after trial would be futile.

 

Defendant’s ex parte application to shorten time to hear the Motion to Compel IME is GRANTED IN PART. The Court cannot accommodate a hearing date on August 21, 2026. Alternatively, Defendant’s Motion to Compel IME is ADVANCED to today’s date and CONTINUED to Thursday, September 3, 2026. Opposition and reply to be filed and served per Code in accordance with the new hearing date. Moving party to give notice.

Case Number: 26NWCV02419    Hearing Date: August 5, 2026    Dept: P

This motor vehicle-personal injury action was filed on July 21, 2026. Plaintiffs Salvador Rodríguez Ceja, Graciela Gonzalez Vargas, and Adriana Rodríguez Gonzalez (Plaintiffs) now apply ex parte for a nunc pro tunc order to backdate the filing date of their complaint.

 

Plaintiffs’ ex parte application for a nunc pro tunc order is DENIED without prejudice. The Court notes that Plaintiffs’ first ex parte application for this same relief was heard on Monday, August 3, 2026. The Court continued the hearing to Thursday, August 6, 2026. Plaintiffs do not establish exigency to hear this application one day in advance of the continued hearing date. Moving party to give notice.
