Case Number: 24NWLC44816    Hearing Date: June 23, 2026    Dept: Z

24NWLC44816: CAPITAL ONE, N.A. vs MARTINE A HERNANDEZ

Tentative Ruling:
The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on February 12, 2025 and filed with the Court on March 7, 2025.  
The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $3,737.27, leaving an outstanding amount of $534.00 due and owing.
Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 
The Order dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. and against Defendant Martine A Hernandez for the remaining sum of $534.00 plus court costs of $603.61, for a total judgment of $1,137.61.
The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Case Number: 25NWLC24533    Hearing Date: June 23, 2026    Dept: Z

25NWLC24533: CITIBANK N.A. vs AIDA SEGOVIA
The Court has read and reviewed the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel, the Declaration in Support of the Motion, and the Proof of Service, filed May 19, 2026.  Cal. Rules of Court 3.1362(d)(1) requires that the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel be accompanied with a declaration stating facts showing that the client has been noticed with the motion either at their current address or last known address.  The Declaration contained on MC 052 form is insufficient because the "Service" section, page 1, paragraph 3, is not filled out.   Further the Proof of Service to Defendant does not state the date that the Motion and supporting documents were mailed to Defendant. The Court DENIES the application.

25NWLC41079: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK N.A. vs ANDREW WEI SHI
