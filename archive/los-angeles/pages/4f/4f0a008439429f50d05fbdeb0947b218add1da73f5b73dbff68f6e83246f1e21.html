DEPARTMENT 316 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 26STCV07690    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: 316

BACKGROUND

This is an unlawful detainer matter. Plaintiff Tripalink Property Management, Inc. alleges that Defendant Cho Luxury Rentals owes over $14,000 in past due rent.

On March 10, 2026, Plaintiff filed a complaint against Defendant.

On June 8, 2026, Plaintiff filed a Motion to Consolidate (“Motion”).

On July 8, 2026, an untimely opposition was filed.

TENTATIVE RULING

Plaintiff’s Motion to Consolidate is DENIED without prejudice. Once Plaintiff relates the three cases into a single department, it may move to consolidate the cases in said department.

LEGAL STANDARD

The trial court¿is authorized to¿consolidate¿pending “actions involving a common question of law or fact”¿so as to¿“avoid unnecessary costs or delay.” (Code Civ. Proc.,¿§ 1048, subd. (a).) The decision to consolidate pending actions is a matter¿wholly committed¿to the trial court’s discretion. (National Electric Supply Co. v. Mt. Diablo Unified School District¿(1960) 187 Cal.App.2d 418, 421.) However, Los Angeles Superior Court Local Rule 3.3, subdivision (g)(1) states that consolidation cannot occur until “[a] motion to consolidate two or more cases [is] noticed and heard after the cases, initially filed in¿different departments, have been related into a single department, or if the cases were already assigned to that department.”

DISCUSSION

Here, Plaintiff seeks to consolidate this case (Case No. 26STCV07690) with two other cases (Case No. 26STCV06887 and Case No. 26STCV07688). (See Motion, p. 2, ¶ 1(a)-(c).) However, these three cases have not been related into a single department. (See LASC Local Rule 3.3, subd. (g)(1).) Rather, only Case No. 26STCV06887 and Case No. 26STCV07688 have been related into a single department (Dept. 730). (See Case No. 26STCV06887 May 22 Minute Order.) This case was not related to the other two cases. (See ibid.)  A notice of related cases was filed in this case on May 11, 2026. The decision on that notice is to be made by the department with the earliest filed case, which here would be Department 730 because 26STCV06887 is the earliest filed case of the purportedly related cases. (See California Rules of Court, Rule 3.300(h)(1)(A).)

Given that the present case has not been related into a single department with either of the cases Plaintiff seeks to have it consolidated with, the motion must be denied. (See LASC Local Rule 3.3, subd. (g)(1).)

CONCLUSION

For the foregoing reasons, Plaintiff’s Motion to Consolidate is DENIED without prejudice. If this case is related to one or both of the cases for which consolidation has been sought, Plaintiff may move to consolidate the cases in the appropriate department.
