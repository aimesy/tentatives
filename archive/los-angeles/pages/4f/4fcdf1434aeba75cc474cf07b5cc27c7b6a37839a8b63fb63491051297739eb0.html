DEPARTMENT O LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25VECV04204    Hearing Date: July 20, 2026    Dept: O

Counsel Ami Meyers' Motion to be Relieved as Counsel is denied for failing to serve Plaintiff.

“The notice of motion and motion, the declaration, and the proposed order must be served on the client and on all other parties who have appeared in the case. The notice may be by personal service, electronic service, or mail.” (CRC 3.1362(d).)
