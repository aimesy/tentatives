[TENTATIVE] ORDER RE: PETITION TO CONFIRM MINOR’S COMPROMISE

 

Dept. 3

8:30 a.m.

July 20, 2026

 

Claimant Aaron Morales (“Claimant”), a minor, by and through their Guardian Ad Litem, Bibiana Ramos (“Petitioner”), has agreed to settle their claims against Defendant Extra Mile International, Inc.in exchange for $225,000. If approved, $74,250 will be used for attorney’s fees, leaving a balance of $150,750 for Claimant, with $750 to be paid to Claimant immediately and $150,000 to be paid to Athene Qualified Assignment Corporation to then be distributed according to terms and conditions of a structured settlement.

Court approval is required for all settlements of a minor’s claim. (Probate Code §§ 3500, 3600, et seq.; Code Civ. Proc. § 372.) The Court has reviewed the proposed settlement and finds that it is fair and reasonable. Further, the requested attorney’s fees, which amounts to approximately 30% of the total settlement, is fair and reasonable.

The unopposed Petition to approve minor’s compromise is GRANTED.

The Court sets an OSC for ________________ for proof of purchase. If a declaration from counsel affirming that the annuity has been funded is filed before the date of the OSC, no appearance will be required.

Per California Rules of Court, Rule 7.952, Petitioner and Claimant must appear at the hearing, unless the Court finds good cause to excuse their appearance. The Court finds that Petitioner and Claimant’s appearance is not necessary but will require Petitioner’s counsel to appear.

Moving party to give notice.

 

Dated this 20th day of July 2026

 

 

 

 

William A. Crowfoot

Judge of the Superior Court

ORDER RE: APPLICATION FOR DEFAULT JUDGMENT

 

Dept. 3

8:30 a.m.

July 20, 2026

 

)

 

 

On April 24, 2026, plaintiff Ciras L.L.C. (“Plaintiff”) filed a request for court judgment and proposed judgment against defendants Love You Latte, Inc. and Talin Stephen (“Stephen”). Plaintiff requests a judgment of $119,745.98, consisting of $100,000 in damages, $16,247.04 in prejudgment interest, and 2,890 in attorney’s fees, and $608.94 in costs. Plaintiff does not substantiate its request for prejudgment interest at a rate of 13% as the contract’s interest rate is “the per annum interest rate established by Comerica Bank as its prime rate for its borrowers, as such rate may vary from time to time (which rate is not necessarily the lowest rate on loans made by Bank at any such time), but in no event less than 2.50% plus  2.75% per annum” with an increase of 3% in the even of a default. Furthermore, on May 27, 2026, Plaintiff filed a request for dismissal dismissing Stephen, but did not file an amended proposed default judgment removing Stephen as a defendant.

Accordingly, the hearing is CONTINUED to ______________ at 8:30 a.m. in Department 3 of the Alhambra Courthouse so that Plaintiff can submit a supplemental declaration substantiating its request for prejudgment interest and a revised proposed order omitting Stephen as the dismissed defendant.
