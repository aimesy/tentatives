DIVERSIFIED INVESTMENTS LAS VEGAS, LLC

 

         vs.

 

AARON ROSEN dba RA CONSTRUCTION   

 

 Case No.: 24STCV04432

 

 

 

 Hearing Date: August 12, 2026

 

Plaintiff’s motion to compel further responses to Form Interrogatories (Set One) is GRANTED. Responses due within 10 days.  However, the Court declines to award sanctions at this time.

 

            On 2/1/2024, Plaintiff Diversified Investments Las Vegas, LLC (Plaintiff) filed suit against Defendant Aaron Rosen (Defendant), alleging: (1) breach of contract; (2) negligence; (3) breach of express warranty; and (4) breach of implied warranty. 

 

            On 4/14/2026, Plaintiff moved to compel further responses to Form Interrogatories (Set One). 

 

Discussion

 

            Plaintiff argues further responses are warranted to Form Interrogatories (Set One). 

 

            In opposition, Defendant does not dispute the substance of the motion, but rather only argues that sanctions should not be awarded.

 

            After review, the Court agrees.

 

            As a preliminary matter, this Court typically requires that parties first participate in an informal discovery conference (IDC) before discovery motions are heard on the merits. Second, Plaintiff’s attempt at informal resolution consisted of a single letter, with no telephone call, no follow-up correspondence, and no conference before filing the motion.

 

            Based on the foregoing, Plaintiff’s motion to compel further responses to Form Interrogatories (Set One) is granted. However, the Court declines to award sanctions at this time.

           

 

It is so ordered.

Case Number: 25STCV30093    Hearing Date: August 12, 2026    Dept: 309

Superior Court of California

County of Los Angeles

 

DEPARTMENT 17

 

TENTATIVE RULING

 

JOVANNY TAPIA

 

         vs.

 

GENERAL MOTORS, LLC

 

 Case No.: 25STCV30093  

 

 

 

 Hearing Date: August 12, 2026

 

 

Defendant’s motion for an order for compliance requiring Plaintiff to appear and comply with the initial deposition is GRANTED. Plaintiff is sanctioned, jointly and severally with counsel, $1,500 due within 15 business days of entry of this order. 

 

On 10/14/2025, Plaintiff Jovanny Tapia (Plaintiff) filed suit against General Motors, LLC (Defendant), alleging violations of the Song-Beverly Warranty Act.

 

On 7/30/2026, Defendant moved to compel Plaintiffs’ compliance pursuant to CCP section 871.26.

 

Discussion

 

            Defendant moves for an order for compliance requiring Plaintiffs to appear and comply with the initial deposition under Code of Civil Procedure (CCP) section 871.26, subdivision (c)(1).

 

The enactment of Assembly Bill 1755 was codified in Chapter 12 of Title 10 to Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure (herein, Chapter 12), which went into effect 1/1/2025. (See CCP § 871.20, et seq.) In passing Assembly Bill 1755, the legislature enacted provisions that require the parties to participate in initial depositions. (CCP §871.26, subd. (c).) The newly enacted provisions are designed to reduce court docket congestion and promote settlement by temporarily staying all discovery (other than the initial disclosure and document exchanges and initial depositions) until an early mediation is completed. (CCP § 871.26, subd. (d).)

 

In opposition, Plaintiff’s counsel argues that the motion should be continued because “Plaintiff’s counsel is presently unable to perform the functions necessary to represent Plaintiff in this litigation, including producing Plaintiff for the initial deposition that GM seeks to compel.” (Opp, 2: 10-12.)

 

However, counsel provides no explanation as to why the attorney-client breakdown was not brought to the attention of the Court at any time prior, or why the motion to be relieved as counsel (a copy of which cannot be located in eCourt) was not advanced. As such, the Court finds a lack of reasonable diligence on counsel’s part, and thus declines to continue the motion. 

 

Based on the foregoing, Defendant’s motion for an order for compliance requiring Plaintiffs to appear and comply with initial deposition is granted. Plaintiff is sanctioned, jointly and severally with counsel, $1,500 due within 15 business days of entry of this order. 

 

 

 

It is so ordered.
