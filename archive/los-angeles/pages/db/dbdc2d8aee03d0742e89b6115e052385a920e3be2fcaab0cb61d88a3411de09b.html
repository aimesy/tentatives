Case Number: 23NWLC36890    Hearing Date: September 16, 2026    Dept: Y

23NWLC36890: CREDILOGICAL SYSTEMS, LLC vs MONIQUE CANTU

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Credilogical Systems, LLC (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation to Dismiss Case Without Prejudice and Retain Jurisdiction Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on April 24, 2025 and filed with the Court on April 24, 2025.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order Dismissing Action Without Prejudice With The Court Retaining Jurisdiction To Enforce The Parties Settlement Agreement entered by the Court on May 5, 2025.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $1,470.00.  The Court notes that Defendant agreed to pay the principal sum of $3,150.00, prejudgment interest of $1,062.08 and court costs of $395.80 for a total sum of $4,607.88.  Plaintiff has default on payments under the terms of the Stipulated Settlement.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Credilogical Systems, LLC and against Defendant Monique Cantu for the sum of $4,607.88, less payments made of $1470.00 for a total judgment of $3,137.88.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC01399    Hearing Date: September 16, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC01399: CROWN ASSET MANAGEMENT, LLC vs TARINA RICE

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Crown Asset Management, LLC (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Settlement Agreement With Mutual Release And Stipulation For Entry of Judgment (“Stipulation”) signed by Defendant on or about March 3, 2025 and filed with the Court on January 16, 2026 with the moving papers.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has not made any payments, leaving an outstanding amount of $11,322.00 due and owing. 

The Court further notes that the Stipulation signed by Defendant states upon default Plaintiff will provide notice of default to Defendant by mail at her address of record.  Plaintiff has done so.  Paragraph 5 of the Stipulation also provides that if the amount in default is not paid within the time prescribed in Plaintiff’s letter, Plaintiff may immediately file a noticed motion to enforce the settlement and may serve such motion on Defendant by mail, which Plaintiff has done.  Proof of service of this motion by mail was filed by Plaintiff on January 16, 2026.  California Code of Civil Procedure (CCP) § 1005 does not require personal service of moving papers as long as the required deadlines for mail service were met.  The Court finds that Defendant, who is in default of the amount she agreed to pay pursuant to the Stipulation, has received proper notice of this Motion.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Crown Asset Management, LLC and against Defendant Tarina Rice for damages in the principal amount of $11,322.00, plus costs of $624.14, or a total judgment of $11,946.34.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Tentative Ruling:

The Court notes a Substitution of Attorney was filed by Defendant Joy R Cortez (“Defendant”) on September 14, 2026, which states that Defendant who was previously in pro per is now represented by counsel.  If new counsel for Defendant requests a continuance of the hearing on this motion, the Court will consider that request.

(“Plaintiff”), including the declaration of counsel.   The Court finds that Plaintiff mailed Requests for Admission (RFA) to Defendant on April 29, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Plaintiff did not receive any responses from Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed its Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted to Defendant on August 5, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  The address for Defendant on both proofs of service match the address that is listed on the Answer.

Having considered the moving papers, Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted is GRANTED.
