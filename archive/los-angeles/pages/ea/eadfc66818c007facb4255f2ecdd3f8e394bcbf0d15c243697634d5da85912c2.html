Case Number: 24NWLC48135    Hearing Date: July 10, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel, the Declaration in Support of the Motion, and the Proof of Service, filed June 8, 2026.  The Court finds that Defendant was served by mail and that Counsel submitted a declaration establishing the service requirements of California Rules of Court, rule 3.1362, have been satisfied.  The Court GRANTS the Motion to be Relieved as Counsel pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure 284(2).  Counsel is relieved effective upon filing the proof of service upon the client.

Case Number: 25NWLC78362    Hearing Date: July 10, 2026    Dept: Z

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on April 15, 2026.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed to Defendant a meet and confer letter on May 20, 2026.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED as to RFA's #1-9.
