Case Number: 25NWLC62059    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC62059: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK N.A. vs CHARLITA M THOMAS

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the declaration of counsel.   The Court finds that Plaintiff mailed Requests for Admission (RFA) to Defendant on December 12, 2025, and provided proof of service by mail.  Plaintiff did not receive any responses from Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed Defendant its Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted on April 22, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Defendant’s address on both proofs of service match the address that Defendant listed on the Answer.

Having considered the moving papers, Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted is GRANTED.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC71527    Hearing Date: July 9, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC71527: CAPITAL ONE N.A. vs ROCKET A MINJIZZLE

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Defendant Rocket A. Minjizzle (“Defendant”).   It is unclear whether Defendant’s Motion to Vacate and Set Aside Judgment (“Motion”) has been served on Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”).  Defendant did not file a Proof of Service of the Motion on Plaintiff and no opposition has been filed by Plaintiff.    

Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 requires that “all moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. . . . if the notice is served by mail, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by five calendar days if the place of mailing and place of address are within the State of California.” 

Since it is unclear whether proper notice of the motion has been given, the Court continues the hearing on the motion to August 5, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y so that proper notice of the motion can be given to Plaintiff.

Defendant is ordered to serve a copy of her Motion on counsel for Plaintiff and file Proof of Service.  To the extent the Motion was served on counsel for Plaintiff, Defendant may file a Proof of Service.

Clerk to give notice.
