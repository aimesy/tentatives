DEPARTMENT S25 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

Imed Hsini v. Mohsen Ghariani, et al.
LASC Case No. 24LBCV00194
Hearing Date: September 11, 2024
Request for Default Judgment by Court
 
 
In preparation for the September 11, 2024 default judgment hearing, the court has identified several concerns with Plaintiff’s request: 
 

  1. Request for Entry of Default was entered on May 9, 2024 which is less than 30 days after service of the summons and complaint to Defendant Danielle Canissa and CDW Logistics, LLC. (C.C.P. §587);
 
  1. Plaintiff checked the wrong box and should have checked “Court Judgment” in the “Request For” section on the 1st page. (See 7/29/2024 Request for Entry of Default/Judgment);
 
  1. No DOEs dismissal filed;
 
  1. No attached/adequate and/or authenticated exhibits provided with Plaintiff’s Declaration. Exhibits should demonstrate, e.g. costs for title and insurance for two vehicles, power of attorney, agreement entered into with Defendants to purchase trucks and provide logistic and shipping services to clients;
 
  1. No contractual or statutory basis provided by Plaintiff as to recovery of attorney fees. Also, attorney fees requested is more than what is allowed in a contract for recovery of reasonable attorney fees under Local Rule of the Court, rule 3.214(a);  
 
  1. Prayer for complaint does not specify damages claimed;
 
  1. No notice of punitive damages as required under Code Civ. Proc., § 425.11, subd. (c).  Appears Plaintiff may be waiving punitive damages based upon proposed judgment;
 
  1. Proposed Judgment (UD-100) incorrect calculations as to attorney fees and prejudgment interest calculations.


Case Number: 25LBCV00137    Hearing Date: July 15, 2026    Dept: S25

Motion:  Plaintiff Esmeralda Zaragoza’s Consolidated Motion to Compel Defendant Childnet Youth and Family Services, Inc. to Provide Further Discovery Responses and Requests for Monetary Sanctions 

Moving Party:  Plaintiff Esmeralda Zaragoza (“Plaintiff”) 

Responding Party: Defendant Childnet Youth and Family Services, Inc.  (“Defendant”)  

 

Tentative Ruling 

Plaintiff moves the Court for an order directing Defendant to produce full and complete responses to Plaintiff’s Special Interrogatories (Set One), Nos. 1-2, Special Interrogatories (Set Two), Nos. 1-35, Form Interrogatories (Set One), Nos. 1.1, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6, 3.7, 4.1, 4.2, 12.1, 12.2, 12.3, 12.4, 12.4, 12.6, 12.7, 13.1, 13.2, 14.1, 14.2, 15.1, and 17.1, Requests for Admission (Set One), Nos. 1-27, and Requests for Production (Set One) Nos. 1-89.  In opposition, Defendant asserts that: Plaintiff failed to file a separate statement; Plaintiff did not sufficiently meet and confer; Plaintiff did not carry her burden of showing that further responses are required; Plaintiff did not show good cause for the Requests for Production (Set One); and Plaintiff’s objection labels do not support relief. No reply has been received.   

 

The parties failed to meet and confer.  

 

California Rules of Court, rule 3.1345(a) requires a separate statement for motions to compel further responses to requests for admission, interrogatories and to a demand for inspection of documents or tangible things.  Plaintiff failed to submit a separate statement.  

 

“Regardless of whether each motion or matter is heard at a single hearing or at separate hearings, the filing fee required by subdivisions (a), (c), (d), and (e) apply separately to each motion or other paper filed.”  (Gov. Code § 70617, subd. (f).)  Instead, Plaintiff should have filed a total of five separate motions: a motion to compel further responses as to the Form Interrogatories, Set One, a motion to compel further responses as to the Special Interrogatories, Set One, a motion to compel further responses as to the Special Interrogatories, Set Two, a motion to compel further responses as to Requests for Production of Documents, Set One, and another motion to compel further responses to Requests for Admission, Set One.   

 

Plaintiff Esmeralda Zaragoza’s Consolidated Motion to Compel Defendant Childnet Youth and Family Services, Inc. to Provide Further Discovery Responses and Requests for Monetary Sanctions is DENIED without prejudice.
