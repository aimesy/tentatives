Case Number: 25NWLC52216    Hearing Date: July 8, 2026    Dept: Z

 25NWLC52216: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs OMAR G HERNANDEZ, AN INDIVIDUAL
The Court finds Plaintiff has met the initial burden of showing that there is no defense to the causes of action by proving each element of the causes of action entitling Plaintiff to judgment.  
On August 18, 2025, Plaintiff WELLS FARGO BANK filed this credit card collections action against Defendant OMAR G HERNANDEZ.  Plaintiff extended credit to Defendant through a credit card ("Account"). (Complaint at p. 4.) Defendant used the card to purchase goods, services, and/or cash advances. (Complaint at p. 4.)  Defendant, in exchange for use of the card, agreed to repay Plaintiff for the principal, fees, and interest on the Account. (Complaint at p. 4.) Plaintiff alleges that Defendant failed to make payments owed on the Account. (Complaint at p. 4.)  Plaintiff's Complaint alleges two causes of action: Breach of Written Contract and Breach of Implied Contract. 
The Court has read and reviewed Plaintiff's Motion for Summary Judgment, Separate Statement of Undisputed Facts, the Requests for Admissions Responses, Declaration of Plaintiff's Most Qualified Witness and attached exhibits. The Court finds that Plaintiff has met its initial burden of establishing all elements in support of the breach of contract claims.  There is no triable issue of material fact.  
The burden having shifted to Defendant, the Court finds Defendant has failed to set forth specific facts showing that a triable issue of material fact exists as to the causes of action or a defense thereto as required by CCP 437c(p)(1).
The court finds that Plaintiff is entitled to judgment as a matter of law and GRANTS the Motion for Summary Judgment. Judgment to enter in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $28,818.23, plus court costs of $960.00, for a total judgment of $29,778.23.
