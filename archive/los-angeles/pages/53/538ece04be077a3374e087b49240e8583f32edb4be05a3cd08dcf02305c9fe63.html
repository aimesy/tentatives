Case Number: 23NWLC09318    Hearing Date: September 23, 2026    Dept: Y

23NWLC09318: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A vs MARISELA GALLO, AN INDIVIDUAL

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”).  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation for Entry of Judgment and Settlement and Release & Dismissal of Action With Consent to Court Retaining Jurisdiction Pursuant to C.C.P. § 664.6 (“Stipulation”) signed by Defendant on March 19, 2024 and filed with the Court on April 25, 2024.   The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order entered by the Court on May 3, 2024.

The Court also notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $9,809.16, leaving an outstanding amount of $3,010.00 due and owing.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. and against Defendant Marisela Gallo for damages in the amount of $3,010.00, plus costs of $450.00, for a total judgment of $3,460.00.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 24NWLC30464    Hearing Date: September 23, 2026    Dept: Y

24NWLC30464: SYNCHRONY BANK vs MARIA MOLINA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Synchrony Bank (“Plaintiff”), the supporting declaration, and all relevant court records.  

In its motion, Plaintiff requests to vacate the Default and Default Judgment entered by the Court on October 30, 2024 and dismiss the action with prejudice.   Plaintiff has presented evidence that Defendant Maria Molina (“Defendant”) claimed fraud on the account. 

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 473(d), the Court may set aside any void judgment or order.  Code of Civil Procedure § 128 also grants jurisdiction to the Court to take appropriate action in the interests of justice.

Based on the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Vacate and Set Aside Default and Default Judgment.   Accordingly, the Default Judgment entered against Defendant on October 30, 2024 is vacated.

The Court further orders that the Complaint filed by Plaintiff and against Defendant is DISMISSED with prejudice.

The Court will sign the Proposed Order submitted by Plaintiff.

Plaintiff to give notice.
