The court considered the moving papers filed in connection with this motion.  No opposition papers were filed.

DISCUSSION

Motion to Compel Responses to Form Interrogatories - Employment

Plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran (“Plaintiff”) moves the court for an order (1) compelling defendant YWCA of Glendale and Pasadena (“Defendant”) to serve responses to Plaintiff’s Form Interrogatories -Employment, Set 1, and (2) awarding monetary sanctions in favor of Plaintiff and against Defendant in the amount of $548.76.

          The court grants Plaintiff’s motion because (1) Plaintiff has shown that Defendant did not serve timely responses to their Form Interrogatories, and (2) Defendant did not submit opposition papers or other evidence with the court establishing that responses have since been served on Plaintiff.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.290, subd. (b); Haddad Decl., Ex. 6 [proof of mail service of Plaintiff’s Form Interrogatories on counsel for Defendant] and ¶¶ 4-18.)

          The court grants Plaintiff’s request for monetary sanctions.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.290, subd. (c).)  The court finds that $548.76 ((0.8 hours x $600 hourly rate) + $61.65 reservation fee + 7.26 filing fee) is a reasonable amount of sanctions to impose against Defendant in connection with this motion.  (Haddad Decl., ¶ 19.)

Motion to Compel Responses to Requests for Production

Plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran (“Plaintiff”) moves the court for an order (1) compelling defendant YWCA of Glendale and Pasadena (“Defendant”) to serve responses to Plaintiff’s Requests for Production, Set 1, and (2) awarding monetary sanctions in favor of Plaintiff and against Defendant in the amount of $548.76.

          The court grants Plaintiff’s motion because (1) Plaintiff has shown that Defendant did not serve timely responses to their Requests for Production, and (2) Defendant did not submit opposition papers or other evidence with the court establishing that responses have since been served on Plaintiff.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2031.300, subd. (b); Haddad Decl., Ex. 6 [proof of mail service of Plaintiff’s Requests for Production on counsel for Defendant] and ¶¶ 4-18.)

          The court grants Plaintiff’s request for monetary sanctions.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.290, subd. (c).)  The court finds that $548.76 ((0.8 hours x $600 hourly rate) + $61.65 reservation fee + 7.26 filing fee) is a reasonable amount of sanctions to impose against Defendant in connection with this motion.  (Haddad Decl., ¶ 19.)

ORDER

          The court GRANTS plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran’s motion to compel responses to Form Interrogatories - Employment, set 1.

          Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 2030.290, the court orders defendant YWCA of Glendale and Pasadena to serve on plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran full and complete verified answers, without objections and which comply with Code of Civil Procedure sections 2030.220-2030.250, to plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran’s Form Interrogatories - Employment, Set 1, within 20 days of the date of service of this order.

          The court GRANTS plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran’s motion to compel responses to requests for production, set 1.

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 2031.300, the court orders defendant YWCA of Glendale and Pasadena (1) to serve on plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran written responses, without objections and which comply with Code of Civil Procedure sections 2031.210-2031.250, to plaintiff plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran’s Requests for Production of Documents, numbers 12-13, and (2) to produce to plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran all documents and things in defendant Alberto Alvarez’s possession, custody, or control which are responsive to those requests, within 20 days of the date of service of this order.

          The court orders defendant YWCA of Glendale And Pasadena to pay monetary sanctions to plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran in the amount of 1,097.52 within 30 days of the date of this order.

          The court orders plaintiff Destiny Kristal Duran to give notice of this ruling.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
