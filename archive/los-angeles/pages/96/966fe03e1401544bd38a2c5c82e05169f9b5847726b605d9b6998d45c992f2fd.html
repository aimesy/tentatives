Case Number: 25NWLC56444    Hearing Date: September 24, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC56444: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs JAIME PEREIDA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A.’s (“Plaintiff”) Motion for Summary Judgment, which was filed on July 1, 2026 and set for hearing on September 24, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Jamie Perieda (“Defendant”).

On September 17, 2026, 12 days before trial and 7 days before the hearing on the summary judgment, Plaintiff filed an ex parte application to continue the trial or for leave of the Court to have its summary judgment motion heard days before trial.  The Court denied Plaintiff’s ex parte application because no good cause was shown and the claimed exigency was sel-created.

A motion for summary judgment under Code of Civil Procedure section 437c(a)(3) must be heard no later than 30 days before trial, unless the court orders otherwise for good cause.  The trial in this case is scheduled on September 29, 2026 – 5 days after the date of the hearing for the motion for summary judgment.  Accordingly, Plaintiff’s Motion for Summary Judgment is denied without prejudice. 

The trial date of September 29, 2026 remains on calendar.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC63842    Hearing Date: September 24, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC63842: CAPITAL ONE, N.A. vs SAMORIA MARTIN

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered Defendant Samoria Martin’s (“Defendant”) Motion to Quash Service of Summons (“Motion”), including the supporting declaration.   Defendant filed and served its Motion on August 12, 2026 along with a Proof of Service by mail on Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”).    

The Motion was initially set for hearing on September 4, 2026.  The Court continued the Motion to September 24, 2026 so that proper notice of the Motion could be provided to Plaintiff.  No opposition has been filed by Plaintiff. 

Defendant specially appears and moves to quash service of summons pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 418.10.  Defendant argues that he was not properly served in compliance with the law.

Evidence Code § 647 creates a rebuttable presumption of valid service when, as here, a registered process service files a Proof of Service with the Court.  The individual questioning the validity of service must present sufficient evidence to demonstrate that service did not occur as stated in the Proof of Service.  Evid. Code §§ 647, 660.

The Court notes that Proof of Service of Summons by a registered process server by personal service was filed with the Court on July 21, 2026 creating a presumption of service under Evidence Code section 647.  The Proof of Service indicates personal service on July 17, 2026 at 4:31 p.m. at “19039 Kittridge St., Apt. 47, Reseda, CA 91335.”  The person served was described as Female, Age: 26-30, Skin Tone: White, Hair: Brown, Eye Color: Hazel, Height: 5’0”-5’3”, Weight: 171-190 lbs.  

Defendant’s declaration, which was signed under penalty of perjury, states that she is not the person described in the Proof of Service.  She also states, under penalty of perjury, that she was not personally served with the Complaint and did not authorize anyone to accept service of the Summons and Complaint on her behalf.  She learned of this lawsuit through mailed consultation advertisements.  The Court finds that Defendant has rebutted the presumption of personal service. 

Having considered the moving papers, Defendant’s Motion to Quash Service of Process is GRANTED.

The Court has read and considered Defendant Derrick Grenier’s (“Defendant”) Motion to Quash Service of Summons (“Motion”), including the supporting declaration.   Defendant filed and served its Motion on August 17, 2026 along with a Proof of Service by mail on Plaintiff LVNV Funding, LLC (“Plaintiff”).    

The Motion was initially set for hearing on September 9, 2026.  The Court continued the Motion to September 24, 2026 so that proper notice of the Motion could be provided to Plaintiff.  No opposition has been filed by Plaintiff. 

Defendant specially appears and moves to quash service of summons pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure § 418.10.  Defendant argues that he was not properly served in compliance with the law.

Evidence Code § 647 creates a rebuttable presumption of valid service when, as here, a registered process service files a Proof of Service with the Court.  The individual questioning the validity of service must present sufficient evidence to demonstrate that service did not occur as stated in the Proof of Service.  Evid. Code §§ 647, 660.

The Court notes that Proof of Service of Summons by a registered process server by personal service was filed with the Court on August 12, 2026 creating a presumption of service under Evidence Code section 647.  The Proof of Service indicates personal service on March 9, 2026 at 6:42 p.m. at “5122 ¾ Hayter Ave, Lakewood, CA 90712.”  The process server stated that the identity was confirmed “by neighbor” and the individual served tried to refuse service by not opening the door.  The documents were left.   The person served was described as a Black male, Age: 55-65, Height: 5’6”-5’8”, Weight: 120-140 lbs.

Having considered the moving papers, Defendant’s Motion to Quash Service of Process is GRANTED.

Case Number: 25NWLC71527    Hearing Date: September 24, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC71527: CAPITAL ONE N.A. vs ROCKET A MINJIZZLE

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the Motion to Set Aside and Vacate Judgment (“Motion”) filed by Defendant Rocket Minjizzle (“Defendant”) on August 24, 2026 and Plaintiff’s opposition filed on September 4, 2026.   

The Court notes that Default was entered by the Clerk on May 18, 2026 and Default Judgment was entered by the Clerk on May 28, 2026. 

Evidence Code § 647 creates a rebuttable presumption of valid service when, as here, a registered process service files a Proof of Service with the Court.  The individual questioning the validity of service must present sufficient evidence to demonstrate that service did not occur as stated in the Proof of Service.  Evid. Code §§ 647, 660.

The Court notes that Proof of Service of Summons by a registered process server by personal service was filed with the Court on December 30, 2025 creating a presumption of service under Evidence Code section 647.  The Proof of Service states that the Summons and Complaint were personally served on Rocket A Minjizzle.

Defendant argues that she was not personally served with the Summons and Complaint.   She has submitted a declaration from her minor daughter (age 15) who states under penalty of perjury that the process server served her with the Summons and Complaint.   The Proof of Service states “personal service” on the Defendant, which is incorrect.  There was no personal service.  Further, Code of Civil Procedure section 415.20(b) governs substituted service and requires, among other things, that the summons and complaint be left with a competent member of the household who is at least 18 years of age. 

On this record, the Court finds that Defendant has rebutted the presumption of service. 

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure §§ 473(d) and 473.5, the Court will GRANT Defendant’s Motion to Set Aside and Vacate the Default Judgment. 

The Court grants Defendant 30 days leave to file a responsive pleading with the Court and serve it on Plaintiff.  The Court encourages the parties to discuss the identity theft claim that Defendant has raised in her motion and conduct a fraud investigation if necessary.

The Court will set a OSC Re Status of Pleadings on September 17, 2027 at 8:30 a.m. in Department Y of the Norwalk Courthouse.  The Court anticipates that this date will become the trial date when the responsive pleading is filed by Defendant. 

Clerk to give notice.
