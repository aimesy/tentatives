DEPARTMENT SEP LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 24NWCV04585    Hearing Date: September 21, 2026    Dept: SEP

Rivero v. Ammari, et al., 24NWCV04585 

This quiet title action was filed on November 21, 2024. There is no trial date scheduled. Defendant Sami Ammari’s (Defendant) Motion for Terminating Sanctions is scheduled to be heard on March 11, 2027. On September 15, 2026, the Court denied Defendant’s ex parte application to advance the hearing on the Motion for Terminating Sanctions to a date before September 30, 2026. Defendant now applies ex parte to advance the hearing on the Motion for Terminating Sanctions to the earliest date that permits Plaintiff the full statutory notice period. Defendant argues good cause exists due to “Defendant's serious health complications, the need to place his legal and personal affairs in order, and the substantial burden of leaving a potentially case dispositive sanctions motion unresolved for approximately six additional months when it can be heard earlier without sacrificing either side's briefing rights.” (Ex Parte, 2:10-13.)

 

Defendant’s ex parte application to advance the hearing on the Motion for Terminating Sanctions is GRANTED. Defendant’s Motion for Terminating Sanctions is ADVANCED to today’s date and CONTINUED to Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 9:30 AM in Dept. P. Opposition and reply to be filed and served per Code in accordance with the new hearing date. Moving party to give notice.
