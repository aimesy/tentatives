Case Number: 23NWLC09805    Hearing Date: July 8, 2026    Dept: Y

23NWLC09805: CAPITAL ONE N.A. vs ROCIO VELASCO

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on May 1, 2023 and filed with the Court on May 15, 2023.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order on Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Judgment entered by the Court on May 19, 2023.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $2,222.01, leaving an outstanding amount of $280.00 due and owing.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order Dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. and against Defendant Rocio Velasco for the principal sum of $280.00 plus court costs of $598.50, for a total judgment of $878.50.

The Proposed Judgment will signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC03783    Hearing Date: July 8, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC03783: BANK OF AMERICA N.A. vs RICHARD ARELLANO

Tentative Ruling on Motion:

Plaintiff Bank of America N.A.’s (“Plaintiff”) Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted (“Motion”) was initially heard on October 20, 2025.  At the hearing, Defendant Richard Arellano (“Defendant”) advised the Court that he had responded to Plaintiff’s Requests for Admission.  The Court continued the hearing to allow Plaintiff to determine whether Defendant had responded to the requested discovery and to meet and confer regarding the requested discovery.

At the continued hearing on February 23, 2026, the Court continued the hearing for second time to May 14, 2026 to allow Defendant to file an opposition to Plaintiff’s Motion.  Due to a scheduling conflict, on the Court’s own motion, the Court continued the hearing to July 8, 2026.  

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff, including the declaration of counsel on August 29, 2025.   The Court has also read and considered the Opposition filed by Defendant on March 25, 2026.  Defendant’s Opposition attaches his responses to Plaintiff’s RFAs.

Since Defendant has now responded to Plaintiff’s RFAs, Plaintiff’s Motion is DENIED.

The Court also notes that the Court set the trial and the continued hearing of the Motion on the same day.  At the request of the parties, the Court will continue the current trial date approximately six months.

Plaintiff to give notice.
