Case Number: 24NWLC07845    Hearing Date: June 30, 2026    Dept: Z

 24NWLC07845: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK, N.A. vs EDUARDO ALVAREZ
Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by the Plaintiff and oral argument by the parties.
Court takes judicial notice of the stipulation signed by the Defendant on June 27, 2024.
Defendant has paid $3,936.00. 
No additional payments have been made and Defendant remains in default.
Based on the stipulation and moving papers, Plaintiff's motion is granted.
Notice of Settlement and Dismissal pursuant to CCP 664.6 is set aside. Judgement in favor of Plaintiff in the amount of $2,299.98 for the principal.  Proposed Order will be signed. 

Case Number: 24NWLC57556    Hearing Date: June 30, 2026    Dept: Z

24NWLC57556: ACCELERATED INVENTORY MANAGEMENT LLC vs RENE VARGAS
The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff. The Court finds Plaintiff mailed a request for admissions (RFA) to Defendant on March 6, 2025.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed to Defendant a meet and confer letter on April 17, 2025.  Plaintiff did not receive a response by Defendant.  Defendant's address on the RFA mailing proof of service for the motion matches the address Defendant listed in Defendant's Answer.  Having considered all moving papers, the Motion for Order Deeming the Request for Admissions Admitted is GRANTED as to RFA's #1-11.
