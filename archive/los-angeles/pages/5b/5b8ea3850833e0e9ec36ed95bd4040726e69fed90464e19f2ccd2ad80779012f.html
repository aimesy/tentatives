Case Number: 23NWLC05512    Hearing Date: July 2, 2026    Dept: Y

23NWLC05512: CAPITAL ONE, N.A. vs MICHAEL D SMITH

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on February 14, 2024 and filed with the Court on February 23, 2024.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order on Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Judgment entered by the Court on February 26, 2024.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $3,100.00, leaving an outstanding amount of $2,638.20 due and owing.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order Dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. and against Defendant Michael D. Smith for damages in the amount of $5,738.20, plus costs of $463.00, less payments of $3,100.00 for a total judgment of $3,101.20.

The Proposed Judgment will signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

24NWLC34561: CROWN ASSET MANAGEMENT, LLC vs ESMERALDA PEREZ

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Crown Asset Management, LLC (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on October 22, 2024 and filed with the Court on April 10, 2026.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Notice of Settlement filed by the parties on February 7, 2025.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant.

Case Number: 25NWLC31730    Hearing Date: July 2, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC31730: BANK OF AMERICA N.A. vs MARIO SADY RAMIREZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court notes that Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) and Defendant Mario Sady Ramirez appeared on June 18, 2026 and advised the Court that they had reached a tentative settlement.  The Court scheduled an Order to Show Cause Re: Dismissal (Settlement) on April 6, 2027.

Although Plaintiff had previously filed a Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted (“Motion”), which was set for hearing on July 2, 2026, that motion was inadvertently left on calendar by Plaintiff and the Court.

On its own motion, the Court takes Plaintiff’s Motion off calendar.   The Court will reset the Motion at Plaintiff’s request if necessary.

Case Number: 25NWLC55689    Hearing Date: July 2, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC55689: BANK OF AMERICA N.A. vs MANUEL OVALLE III

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the declaration of counsel.   The Court finds that Plaintiff mailed Requests for Admission (RFA) to Defendant on December 1, 2025, and provided proof of service by mail.  Plaintiff did not receive any responses from Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed its Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted to Defendant on June 3, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Defendant’s address on both proofs of service match the address that is listed on the Answer.
