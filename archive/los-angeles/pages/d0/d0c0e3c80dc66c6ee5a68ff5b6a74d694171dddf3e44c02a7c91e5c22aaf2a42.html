DEPARTMENT 735 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV28537    Hearing Date: August 10, 2026    Dept: 735

Joedan Management, LLC v. John Byeon, et al.

(1) Motion for an Order Deeming Admitted its Requests for Admissions to Defendant John Byeon and for Monetary Sanctions

 

(2) Motion for an Order Compelling Defendant John Byeon to Respond to Requests for Production of Documents and Monetary Sanctions

 

On September 30, 2026, Plaintiff Joedan Management, LLC (“Plaintiff”) filed a Complaint against Defendant John Byeon, Global JYK Investment Inc., Claremont Towne Cleaners, Inc., Frederick Byeon,  and Tommy’s Market. Plaintiff served Defendant John Byeon with Requests for Production of Documents (set one) and Requests for Admission (set one) on January 16, 2026.  (Motions, Ellis Dec., ¶2.)  Responses were due on February 20, 2026.  (Id. at ¶4.)  No responses were served to either the RFPs or the RFAs.  (Id. at ¶7.)  As of August 6, 2026, no oppositions have been served and there is no indication that substantively compliant responses to the RFAs have been served. 

            Plaintiff’s (1) Motion for an Order Compelling Defendant John Byeon to Respond to Requests for Admission and Request for Sanctions and (2) Motion for an Order Deeming Admitted Its Requests for Admissions to Defendant John Byeon are granted.  Defendant John Byeon is ordered to serve responses without objection to Plaintiff’s RFPs (set one).  Plaintiff’s RFAs (set one) are deemed admitted against Defendant John Byeon. 

            Plaintiff requests sanctions in the amount of $952.50 for the motion to deem requests for admission admitted based on (1) .5 hours motion preparation; (2) 1 hour for opposition review and the hearing; and (3) a $60 filing fee.  The sanctions are reduced to $655 based on (1) 1 hour to prepare the motion and appear at the hearing and (2) a $60 filing fee. 

            Plaintiff requests sanctions in the amount of $1,190.50 based on (1) .9 hours for the motion; (2) 1 hour for opposition review and hearing attendance; and (3) a $60 filing fee. The sanctions are reduced to $893 based on (1) 1.4 hours for the motion and hearing and (2) a $60 filing fee. 

CONCLUSION 

The court grants Plaintiff’s (1) Motion for an Order Compelling Defendant John Byeon to Respond to Requests for Admission and Request for Sanctions and (2) Motion for an Order Deeming Admitted Its Requests for Admissions to Defendant John Byeon.  Defendant John Byeon is ordered to serve responses without objection to Plaintiff’s RFPs (set one) within 20 days.  Plaintiff’s RFAs (set one) are deemed admitted against Defendant John Byeon.  Defendant John Byeon is ordered to pay sanctions in the amount of $1,548.

Plaintiff shall give notice.
