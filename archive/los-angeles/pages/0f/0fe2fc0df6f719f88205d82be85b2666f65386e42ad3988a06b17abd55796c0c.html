KRISTINA RIVAS vs NATURES IMAGE, INC., et al.

 

August 28, 2026

8:30 AM

 

 

NATURE OF PROCEEDINGS: PETITIONER/DEFENDANT’S PETITION TO CONFIRM  CONTRACTUAL ARBITRATION AWARD.

 

RULING:

The Court had continued the Petition from July 29, 2026, for Petitioner to file a Proof of Service addressed to Plaintiff’s counsel of record, which has been filed that same date.

There is no Opposition or Opposing Petition as to this matter.

The Court grants the Petition to Confirm Arbitration Award, and will enter a proposed Judgment that is in conformity with the Arbitration Award.

To replace the proposed Judgment received June 23, 2026, Petitioner shall serve and lodge a corrected and updated Judgment, within 10 days. (E.g., Valsan Partners Ltd. Partnership v. Calcor Space Facility (1994) 25 Cal.App.4th 809, 818.)

Petitioner to give notice.
