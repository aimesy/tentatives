DEPARTMENT T LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV13861    Hearing Date: July 22, 2026    Dept: T

Angeles Dominguez Lopez vs. Crystal Hotel Limited Partnership, et al. 

Motion to Compel Defendant Crystal Hotel Limited Partnership’s Further Responses to Form Interrogatories—General, Set One

Moving Party:                         Plaintiff Angeles Dominguez Lopez

Responding Party:                   Defendant Crystal Hotel Limited Partnership

Tentative Ruling:                    Deny

RULING

On March 18, 2026, Plaintiff Angeles Dominguez Lopez (“Plaintiff”) served Defendant Crystal Hotel Limited Partnership (“Defendant’) with a first set of general form interrogatories.  On May 1, 2026, Defendant served verified responses to the form interrogatories via email.  On June 24, 2026, pursuant to a stipulated extension to the statutory deadline, as acknowledged by the parties in their exhibited email correspondence, Plaintiff filed the instant motion to compel further responses to these interrogatories.  However, in filing and serving this motion on June 24, Plaintiff failed to include a separate statement in support of the motion, as required by California Rules of Court, rule 3.1345(a), or to submit a concise outline in lieu of the separate statement, as allowed under this rule by permission of the Court.  Therefore, the motion to compel further responses is denied.  (In re Marriage of Moore (2024) 102 Cal.App.5th 1275, 1296 [“When a motion fails to include a separate statement, a trial court is ‘well within its discretion’ to deny the motion.”].)
