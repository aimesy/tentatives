Case Number: 26STCV16011    Hearing Date: September 18, 2026    Dept: 514

Fleming v. Western States Carpenters Training Fund
26STCV16011
Motion of Plaintiffs’ Counsel to be Relieved

Tentative Ruling

The motion is denied without prejudice.

Counsel served the client electronically but have not confirmed in their declaration, under oath, that they verified the email address or that the client agreed to accept service by email.  In fact, the declaration of counsel states that the client was served by regular mail, return receipt requested which is inconsistent with the proof of service.

The proposed order is largely blank.

If counsel files a new motion to be relieved, counsel should serve the client by mail at an address that counsel has confirmed to be accurate.
