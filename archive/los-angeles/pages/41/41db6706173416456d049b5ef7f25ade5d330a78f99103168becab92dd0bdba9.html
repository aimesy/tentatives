Case Number: 24NWLC18754    Hearing Date: September 22, 2026    Dept: Y

24NWLC18754: LVNV FUNDING LLC vs ERICA C JENKINS, AN INDIVIDUAL

Tentative Ruling:

On August 13, 2026, Plaintiff LVNV Funding LLC’s (“Plaintiff”) filed a second Application for Ex Parte Order for Entry of Judgment (“Ex Parte Application”) against Defendant Erika Jenkins.  Plaintiff filed its initial Ex Parte Application on October 16, 2025 seeking the same relief.  The Court denied Plaintiff’s initial Ex Parte Application on November 7, 2025 stating as follows: “Request to set aside notice of conditional settlement filed on 10/16/2025 is DENIED as declaration states breach occurred approximately 4 years prior to Plaintiff filing the complaint. OSC remains on calendar, Plaintiff may provide further information in support of request.”

The Court has read and considered Plaintiff’s second Ex Parte Application, including the declaration of counsel in support of entry of judgment pursuant to stipulation.  The Court has also read and reviewed the attached Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Entry of Judgment Upon Default (“Stipulation”).   The Court notes that the Stipulation is undated and the signatures appear to be computer-generated and not handwritten signatures, which is of concern to the Court.  The Court also notes that the address used for Defendant in the letter enclosing the Stipulation includes Defendant’s apartment number while the Proof of Service for this Ex Parte Application and the letter sent to Defendant providing notice that the Plaintiff would be requesting judgment does not include Defendant’s apartment number.  Thus, it is unclear whether Defendant has received notice of this Ex Parte Application.  The Court also notes that the same concerns and errors noted by the Court when it denied Plaintiff’s initial Ex Parte Application are present in Plaintiff’s Second Ex Parte Application.

Accordingly, Plaintiff’s second Ex Parte Application is DENIED.  Due to the age of this case, and Plaintiff’s delay in prosecuting this case, the Court DISMISSES this case without prejudice.  

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC67189    Hearing Date: September 22, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC67189: LVNV FUNDING LLC vs EDIN E MONTERROSO

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff LVNV Funding LLC (“Plaintiff”), including the declaration of counsel.   The Court finds that Plaintiff mailed Requests for Admission (RFA) to Defendant on June 22, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Plaintiff did not receive any responses from Defendant Edin Monterrosa (“Defendant”).  Plaintiff mailed Defendant its Motion for Order Deeming Request for Admissions to Defendant Admitted and For Monetary Sanctions on August 7, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Defendant’s address on both proofs of service match the address that Defendant identified on the Answer.

Having considered the moving papers, Plaintiff’s Motion for Order Deeming Request for Admissions to Defendant Admitted is GRANTED.

The Court has reviewed Plaintiff’s request for sanctions of $810.00, including its attorney’s fees for the preparation of this motion as well as filing costs.  The Court finds Plaintiff’s request unreasonable and disproportionate for a collections case.  Nonetheless, the Court AWARDS sanctions in favor of Plaintiff and against Defendant Edin Monterrosa in the amount of $310.00 (1 hour for the preparation of the motion at the hourly rate at $250 and filing costs of $60).  Defendant is ordered to pay the monetary sanctions awarded by the Court within 30 days.  

The Court will revise and sign the Proposed Order reflecting the Court’s ruling.

Plaintiff to give notice.

25NWLC71251: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs FRANCISCO J AYALA, AN INDIVIDUAL

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the declaration of counsel.   

The Court held an initial hearing date on Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted (“Motion”) on June 18, 2026.  At that hearing, Defendant Francisco Ayala (“Defendant”) stated that he had provided some papers to Plaintiff, but it was unclear whether Defendant had responded to Plaintiff’s Requests for Admission (RFA).  The Court continued the hearing on the Motion to September 22, 2026.  No Opposition has been filed by Defendant and no further pleadings have been filed by the parties.

 The Court finds that Plaintiff mailed Requests for Admission (RFA) to Defendant on January 27, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Plaintiff did not receive any responses from Defendant.  Plaintiff mailed Defendant its Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted on April 1, 2026, and provided proof of service by mail.  Defendant’s address on both proofs of service match the address that Defendant listed on the Answer.

Having considered the moving papers, Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted is GRANTED.

Case Number: 25NWLC72127    Hearing Date: September 22, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC72127: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs CHRISTOPHER M GARCIA, AN INDIVIDUAL

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated July 17, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Christopher Garcia (“Defendant”).

Pursuant to California Evidence Code sections 452 and 453, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s request for the Court to take judicial notice of:

  1. Plaintiff’s Complaint filed on November 21, 2025;
  2. The Court’s Order Deeming Plaintiff’s Requests for Admission as Admitted entered on July 15, 2026; and
  3. Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admission Admitted, and the Requests for Admission submitted in support of that motion, filed on August 7, 2026.

The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot reasonably be controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff.

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. and against Defendant Christopher Garcia for the principal sum of $15,520.15 plus costs in the amount of $430.00 for a total Judgment of $15,950.15.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Case Number: 26NWLC33812    Hearing Date: September 22, 2026    Dept: Y

26NWLC33812: BANK OF AMERICA, N.A. vs FAYE E HERNANDEZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated July 13, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Faye E. Hernandez (“Defendant”).

The Court finds that Defendant, in the Answer filed on June 25, 2026, checked the box stating that Defendant admits that all of the statements of the complaint are true and did not indicate any specific statement as being false. 

Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the Complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action against Defendant and the Answer does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense. 

The Court orders Judgment in favor of Plaintiff Bank of America, N.A. and against Defendant Faye E. Hernandez for the principal sum of $6,694.62, plus costs of $356.00 for a total judgment of $7,050.62.
