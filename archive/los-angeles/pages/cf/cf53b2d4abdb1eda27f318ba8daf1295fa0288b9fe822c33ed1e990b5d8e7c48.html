DEPARTMENT T LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25NNCV02937    Hearing Date: July 29, 2026    Dept: T

Hearing Date(s):                     (1)–(3) July 29, 2026

Case Name:                             Ani Gumrikyan, et al. vs. Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association, a Nonprofit Corporation – California Mutual Benefit - Common Interest Development Corporation, et al.

Case No.:                                25NNCV02937

Motion:                                   (1) Motion to Compel Defendant Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association’s Further Responses to Plaintiff’s First Set of Form Interrogatories; (2) Motion to Compel Defendant Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association’s Further Responses to Plaintiff’s First Set of Special Interrogatories; and (3) Motion to Compel Defendant Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association’s Further Responses to Plaintiff’s First Set of Requests for Production

Moving Party:                         Plaintiff Ani Gumrikyan

Responding Party:                   Defendant Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association

Tentative Ruling:                    (1)-(3) Grant in part

RULING

            Based on the joint brief filed by the parties, the Court notes that the only disputed issue that remains is whether Plaintiff is entitled to recover monetary sanctions.  In reviewing the joint brief and the underlying papers in each motion, as Defendant lacked substantial justification in opposing these motions, and given the absence of any circumstances that would render the imposition of sanctions unjust, the Court finds that the imposition of monetary sanctions is warranted.  However, as the effectiveness of the directed meet and confer efforts serves to highlight the deficiencies in the initial efforts that necessitated a brief continuance, the Court finds that a reasonable reduction in the award is also warranted.  (In re Marriage of Moore (2024) 102 Cal.App.5th 1275, 1300 [“[T]he trial court retains broad discretion to use rational methods to reduce a requested amount in order to reach a reasonable award.”].)  Thus, the requests for sanctions are granted in the total reduced amount of $3,000.00.  The motions are otherwise denied as moot.

            (1) Plaintiff Ani Gumrikyan’s Motion to Compel Defendant Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association’s Further Responses to Plaintiff’s First Set of Form Interrogatories is DENIED.

(2) Plaintiff Ani Gumrikyan’s Motion to Compel Defendant Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association’s Further Responses to Plaintiff’s First Set of Special Interrogatories is DENIED.

(3) Plaintiff Ani Gumrikyan’s Motion to Compel Defendant Cabrini Villas Homeowners’ Association’s Further Responses to Plaintiff’s First Set of Requests for Production is DENIED.

Plaintiffs’ requests for monetary sanctions are GRANTED IN PART in the total reduced amount of $3,000.00 against Defendant and its counsel, jointly and severally.  Said sanctions shall be paid to counsel for Plaintiff within 30 days of this order.
