DEPARTMENT 309

 

TENTATIVE RULING

 

DERSHAY WOODS-BOLER

                          

         vs.

 

WEST GARDENA CARE CENTER LLC dba WEST GARDENA POST ACUTE, et al.

 

 Case No.: 24STCV09832

 

 

 

 Hearing Date: September 17, 2026

 

Plaintiff’s motion to tax costs is GRANTED.

 

On 4/19/2024, Plaintiff Dershay Woods-Boler (Plaintiff) filed suit against West Gardena Care Center, LLC dba West Gardena Post Acute, Kenneth Lehmann, Daniel Rivera, and Maria Bernardo, alleging: (1) disability discrimination; (2) failure to accommodate; (3) failure to engage in interactive process; (4) retaliation; (5) violation of the California Family Rights Act (CFRA); (6) retaliation in violation of CFRA; (7) failure to prevent; (8) violation of Labor Code section 1102.5; (9) violation of Labor Code section 232.5; (10) violation of Labor Code section 98.6; (11) violation of Business and Professions Code section 17200; and (12) constructive wrongful termination.

 

            On 7/17/2026, Plaintiff moved to tax costs.

 

Discussion

 

            Plaintiff argues that Defendant is not entitled to recover costs because: (1) this Court lacks jurisdiction to award costs that were neither requested from nor awarded by the arbitrator; and (2) even assuming this Court has jurisdiction to award Defendant costs, Defendant is not entitled to any costs unless there is a finding that Plaintiff’s lawsuit was “frivolous, unreasonable, or groundless.”

 

            After review, the Court finds insufficient evidence to conclude Plaintiff’s lawsuit was “frivolous, unreasonable, or groundless.” First, the arbitrator made no such finding, and the Court will not make a post hoc determination when it has not been able to consider the substantive facts and arguments of the case. Second, the language pointed to by Defendant to show that the claim is frivolous is based on the arbitrator’s own determinations about Plaintiff’s credibility, which are questions of fact.

 

            Based on the foregoing, Plaintiff’s motion to tax costs is granted.

 

It is so ordered.

Case Number: 24STCV32114    Hearing Date: September 17, 2026    Dept: 309

Superior Court of California

County of Los Angeles

 

DEPARTMENT 309

 

TENTATIVE RULING

 

ASHRAF GORJI

                          

         vs.

 

ROBERT SIMANIAN, et al.

                                         

 Case No.: 24STCV32114

 

 

 

 Hearing Date: September 17, 2026

 

Defendants’ motion to compel Plaintiff Ashraf Gorji to appear and testify at his deposition is GRANTED. Plaintiff is sanctioned, jointly and severally with counsel, $700.00.

 

On 12/5/2024, Plaintiff Ashraf Gorji (Plaintiff) filed suit against Robert Simanian, JRDM International, Inc., and Kolah Farangi (collectively, Defendants), alleging: (1) intentional fraudulent transfer; (2) negligent fraudulent transfer; (3) CCP section 708.210; (4) declaratory relief; and (5) unlawful business practices.

 

            On 8/12/2026, Defendants moved to compel Plaintiff Ashraf Gorji to appear and testify at his deposition.

 

Discussion

 

            Defendants argue good cause exists to compel Plaintiff Ashraf Gorji to appear and testify at his deposition, as he and counsel have willfully refused to attend.

 

            In opposition, Plaintiff argues that he cannot be deposed as part of post judgment litigation, and that there was inadequate meet and confer. However, as noted by Defendants, the underlying complaint in this matter was filed as a new lawsuit with a new case number and new defendants. This matter was not filed under the original lawsuit and as such is considered a new lawsuit, as such, the parties are entitled to conduct discovery in order to defend against Plaintiff’s allegation.

 

            After review, the Court finds good cause to compel Plaintiff to appear and testify at deposition.

 

            Based on the foregoing, Defendants’ motion to compel Plaintiff Ashraf Gorji to appear and testify at his deposition is granted. Plaintiff is sanctioned, jointly and severally with counsel, $700.00 ($350/hr x 2 hr).

 

It is so ordered.

Case Number: 25STCV00797    Hearing Date: September 17, 2026    Dept: 309

Superior Court of California

County of Los Angeles

 

DEPARTMENT 17

 

TENTATIVE RULING

 

RANSON ALMEIDA

 

         vs.

 

SOUTH BAY AVIATION, INC.

 

 Case No.: 25STCV00797

 

 

 

 Hearing Date: September 17, 2026

 

Plaintiff’s motion for leave to file an amended complaint is GRANTED.

 

On 1/13/2025, Plaintiff Ranson Almeida (Plaintiff) filed suit against South Bay Aviation, Inc. (Defendant), alleging: (1) Fair Employment and Housing Act (FEHA); and (2) Military and Veterans Code section 394.

 

            On 6/24/2026, Plaintiff moved for leave to amend to file a first amended complaint (FAC) to include a third cause of action for violation of the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA).

 

Discussion

 

            Plaintiff seeks leave to amend to file an updated complaint to include a third cause of action for violation of the USERRA. 

 

            The policy favoring leave to amend is so strong that it is an abuse of discretion to deny an amendment unless the adverse party can show meaningful prejudice. (Atkinson v. Elk Corp. (2003) 109 Cal.App.4th 739, 761.)

 

            Here, this case is in its infancy and no trial date has been set. As such, the Court finds inadequate evidence of prejudice to justify denying leave to amend.

           

Based on the foregoing, Plaintiff’s motion for leave to file an amended complaint is granted.

 

It is so ordered.
