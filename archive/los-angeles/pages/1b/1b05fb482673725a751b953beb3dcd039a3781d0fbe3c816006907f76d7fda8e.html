DEPARTMENT 309

 

TENTATIVE RULING

 

RONALD LEW

 

         vs.

 

AMERICAN HONDA MOTOR CO.

 

 Case No.: 25STCV07439

 

 

 

 Hearing Date: August 13, 2026

 

Defendant’s motion to compel inspection of Plaintiff’s vehicle is MOOT.

 

On 3/14/2025, Plaintiff Ronald Lew. (Plaintiff) filed suit against American Honda Motor Co. Inc. (Defendant), alleging violations of the Song-Beverly Warranty Act.

 

            On 6/15/2026, Defendant moved to compel inspection of Plaintiff’s vehicle.

 

Discussion

 

            Plaintiff’s opposition makes clear that the substance of the motion is moot, as four inspection dates have since been provided.

 

            However, what remains is the question of sanctions. While Plaintiff has since provided dates for inspection, it does appear this motion was necessary to prompt that. Accordingly, sanctions are still warranted.

 

            Based on the foregoing, Defendant’s motion to compel inspection of Plaintiff’s vehicle is moot.

 

It is so ordered.
