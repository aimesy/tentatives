DEPARTMENT 735 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV19184    Hearing Date: June 29, 2026    Dept: 735

Grimberg v. Hillel Hebrew Academy, et al.

Defendants’ Motion to Stay Action

 

BACKGROUND 

On August 5, 2025, Plaintiff Ayala Grimberg filed a complaint against Defendants Hillel Hebrew Academy, Eitan Sender, Daniel Silverman and Malka Katzin, asserting 14 causes of action for FEHA and Labor Code violations, wrongful termination and UCL violations. On September 22, 2025, Defendants filed a motion to compel arbitration.

LEGAL STANDARD

Code of Civil Procedure section 1281.4 provides, in pertinent part, “[i]f an application has been made to a court of competent jurisdiction . . . for an order to arbitrate a controversy which is an issue involved in an action or proceeding pending before a court of this State and such application is undetermined, the court in which such action or proceeding is pending shall, upon motion of a party to such action or proceeding, stay the action or proceeding until the application for an order to arbitrate is determined . . . .”

DISCUSSION

Defendants move for a stay of this action pending the July 21, 2026 hearing on their motion to compel arbitration. Plaintiff has filed a non-opposition to the motion stating that Plaintiff does not oppose a stay pending determination of the motion to compel arbitration. Accordingly, the Court will stay the action and continue the pending discovery motions set for the date of hearing on the instant motion.

CONCLUSION

            The Court grants Defendants' motion for stay pending hearing on the motion to compel arbitration.

The five discovery motions set for June 29, 2026, are continued to July 21, 2026, at 8:30 am.

Plaintiff to give notice.
