DEPARTMENT 1 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS



Case Number: 24STCV27219    Hearing Date: July 22, 2026    Dept: 1

 

 

MOTION TO ENFORCE SETTLEMENT

 

 

Moving Party:             Plaintiff Rurik Hernandez Covarrubias (Lyfe Law, LLP)

Responding Party:      Defendant Martin’s Power Sweeping, Inc. (Emilio Law Group, APC) (no response filed)

Ruling:                        Motion GRANTED.  Plaintiff to submit a proposed judgment in accordance with their proposed order.           

 

This is a wage and hour action.  On October 20, 2025, plaintiff Rurik Hernandez Covarrubias (Plaintiff) and defendant Martin’s Power Sweeping, Inc. (Defendant) stipulated to dismiss this action in consideration of a settlement of Plaintiff’s individual claims for $45,000, to be made in monthly payments of $5,000.  The Court dismissed the action on November 17, 2025, retaining jurisdiction to enforce the settlement pursuant to Code Civ. Proc. § 664.6 and the parties’ agreement.

 

Defendant made four payments, a total of $20,000, and then defaulted.  (Litwin Decl., ¶¶ 7-11.)  Plaintiff now requests judgment for the outstanding balance of $25,000, as well as $1,875 in attorney fees.  The motion is unopposed.

 

Code Civ. Proc. § 664.6(a) provides that a court may, upon motion, enter judgment pursuant to the terms of the settlement.  The settlement provides that Defendant was to pay $45,000 in $5,000 monthly payments, and that “[i]f any legal action is necessary to enforce or interpret this Agreement, the prevailing party shall recover all costs and attorneys’ fees incurred in such enforcement.”  (Litwin Decl., Ex. 1 [settlement], §§ 1, 15.)  Plaintiff’s motion is well taken and will be granted.

 

CONCLUSION

 

For the foregoing reasons, the motion is GRANTED.  The Court will sign Plaintiff’s proposed order.  Plaintiff is to submit a proposed judgment in accordance with the order and give notice.
