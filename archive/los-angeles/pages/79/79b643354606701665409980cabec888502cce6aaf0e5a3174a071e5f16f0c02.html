Case Number: 25NWLC43554    Hearing Date: June 26, 2026    Dept: Z

 25NWLC43554: WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A. vs ERIC Y YI
The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff in support of the Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including a meet and confer letter dated March 31, 2026.
The Court notes that Plaintiff has filed proof of service of this motion on Defendant at the same address Defendant listed on their Answer.
Pursuant to Cal. Evid. Code § 452 and § 453, the Court GRANTS the request for the Court to take judicial notice of:
  1. The Complaint filed by Plaintiff on August 5, 2025;
  2. The Order granting Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted as to RFA # 1-8, entered on March 18, 2026; and
  3. Request for admissions filed December 18, 2025, in support of Plaintiff’s Motion to Deem Requests for Admissions Admitted.
The Court also takes judicial notice of:
  1. Defendant’s Answer filed on September 2, 2025.
The Court finds the Request for Admissions admitted cannot be reasonably controverted at this stage of the proceedings.  The Request for Admissions admitted are tantamount to Defendant admitting the causes of action alleged by Plaintiff. 
The Plaintiff’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED as the complaint states facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action and the Answer in conjunction with the admissions deemed admitted does not state facts sufficient to constitute a defense.
The Court orders Judgement in favor of Plaintiff for the principal sum of $9,950.43, $425.00 for costs, and $800.00 for attorney's fees for a total judgment of $11,175.43.  Proposed judgment will be signed.
