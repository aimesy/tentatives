DEPARTMENT 26 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

If you desire to submit on the Court's tentative ruling, you may do so by e-mailing Department 26 at the Spring Street Courthouse until the morning of the motion hearing.
 
The e-mail address is [email protected]
 
The heading on your e-mail should contain the case name, number, hearing date, and that you submit. The message should indicate your name, contact information, and the party you represent. Please note that if you submit on the court's tentative ruling and do not appear, another party may appear to argue and the court may change its tentative ruling after the oral argument.
 
If there are no appearances by either side and no submission on the Court's tentative ruling, the court will adopt its tentative ruling as its final ruling. The deadline for a party to withdraw a pending motion, or take it off calendar, is TWO COURT DAYS before the hearing on the motion. The court will enter its tentative ruling on motions that remain on calendar after that deadline.
 
Please note, the above e-mail address is to inform the court of your submission on the tentative ruling. All other inquiries to this e-mail will not receive a response.
 
Parties may schedule remote appearances for hearings on motions via LACourtConnect (https://mylacourt.org/laccwelcome). Remote appearances are not permitted for trial or Applications for Order for Appearance and Examination proceedings absent leave of court.

Case Number: 24STLC02506    Hearing Date: July 6, 2026    Dept: 26

TENTATIVE RULING:

 

Plaintiff Mark Tunne’s Petition to Correct and Vacate Arbitration Award is continued to 08/03/2026 at 10:00 am in Department 26 at the Spring Street Courthouse. By 07/10/2026, Plaintiff is to file proof of service of the Petition to Correct and Vacate Arbitration Award and Notice of the New Hearing Date. Failure to do so may result in the Petition to Correct and Vacate Arbitration Award being denied.

 

Defendants Brandon Blake and Blake & Wang, P.A.’s Petition to Confirm Arbitration Award is advanced to 07/06/2026 and continued to 08/03/2026 at 10:00 am in Department 26 at the Spring Street Courthouse. By 07/10/2026, Defendants are to file proof of service of the Notice of New Hearing Date. Failure to do so may result in the Petition to Confirm Arbitration Award being denied.

 

Opposition and reply papers are to be filed and served in accordance with the Code of Civil Procedure.

 

 

Analysis:

 

On April 5, 2024, Plaintiff Mark Tunne (“Plaintiff”) filed the instant action against Defendants Brandon Blake and Blake & Wang, P.A. (“Defendants”). The Court granted Defendants’ Motion to Compel Arbitration on September 30, 2024. (Minute Order, 09/30/24.)

 

On April 3, 2026, Plaintiff filed the instant Petition to Correct and Vacate Arbitration Award. On April 30, 2026, Defendants filed a Petition to Confirm Arbitration Award which is set for hearing on July 8, 2026.

 

Plaintiff’s Petition to Correct and Vacate does not include a proof of service of the Petition itself, nor a notice of hearing and proof of service of the same. Defendant’s Petition includes a proof of service of the Petition to Confirm, but no notice of hearing or proof of service of the same. Failure to give notice of the papers and hearing date is a violation of due process and of the statutory notice requirements. (Code Civ. Proc., § 1005; Jones v. Otero (1984) 156 Cal.App.3d 754, 757.)

 

Accordingly, the Court continues the hearing on both Petitions so that they may be heard concurrently, and with proper notice to the other side of the moving papers and hearing date.

 

Plaintiff Mark Tunne’s Petition to Correct and Vacate Arbitration Award is continued to 08/03/2026 at 10:00 am in Department 26 at the Spring Street Courthouse. By 07/10/2026, Plaintiff is to file proof of service of the Petition to Correct and Vacate Arbitration Award and Notice of the New Hearing Date. Failure to do so may result in the Petition to Correct and Vacate Arbitration Award being denied.

 

Defendants Brandon Blake and Blake & Wang, P.A.’s Petition to Confirm Arbitration Award is advanced to 07/06/2026 and continued to 08/03/2026 at 10:00 am in Department 26 at the Spring Street Courthouse. By 07/10/2026, Defendants are to file proof of service of the Notice of New Hearing Date. Failure to do so may result in the Petition to Confirm Arbitration Award being denied.

 

Opposition and reply papers are to be filed and served in accordance with the Code of Civil Procedure.

 

 

Moving parties to give notice.


Case Number: 26STCP00836    Hearing Date: July 6, 2026    Dept: 26

TENTATIVE RULING:

 

Petitioner Donna Popalio’s Petition to Release Property from Mechanic’s Lien is granted. Petitioner’s request for attorney’s fees is granted in the amount of $1,600.00 and their request for costs is denied without prejudice.

 

Petitioner is to file and serve a proposed judgment in accordance with this ruling within 20 days.

 

 

Analysis:

 

Petitioner Donna Popalio (“Petitioner”) filed the instant Petition for Release of Mechanic’s Lien against Respondents Harbor Westlake Roofing, Inc. and Danilo Guardado (“Respondents”) on February 27, 2026. The Court scheduled the Petition for hearing on July 6, 2026 and ordered Petitioners to serve the Notice of Hearing on all parties and file proof of service of the same. (Notice of Hearing, 02/27/26.)

 

Petitioner filed Proofs of Substitute Service on April 21, 2026 and default was entered against Respondents the next day. No response to the Petition has been filed to date. 

 

Discussion

 

Petitioner moves for an order releasing the mechanic’s lien recorded against their property by Respondents. Under Civil Code section 8484 the petition for release order must be verified by the petitioner and allege the following:

 

(a) The date of recordation of the claim of lien. A certified copy of the claim of lien shall be attached to the petition.

(b) The county in which the claim of lien is recorded.

(c) The book and page or series number of the place in the official records where the claim of lien is recorded.

(d) The legal description of the property subject to the claim of lien.

(e) Whether an extension of credit has been granted under Section 8460, if so to what date, and that the time for commencement of an action to enforce the lien has expired.

(f) That the owner has given the claimant notice under Section 8482 demanding that the claimant execute and record a release of the lien and that the claimant is unable or unwilling to do so or cannot with reasonable diligence be found.

(g) Whether an action to enforce the lien is pending.

(h) Whether the owner of the property or interest in the property has filed for relief in bankruptcy or another restraint prevents the claimant from commencing an action to enforce the lien.

 

(Civil Code, § 8484.) The Petition complies with the above requirements. Petitioner has demonstrated service of the Petition and Notice of Hearing at least 15 days before the hearing and by substitute service, as required by Civil Code section 8486. The Court grants the Petition in favor of Petitioner.

 

Additionally, Petitioner seeks attorney’s fees of $2,750.00 pursuant to Civil Code section 8488, which provides that “The prevailing party is entitled to reasonable attorney’s fees.” (Civ. Code, § 8488, subd. (c).) As the party in whose favor the Court grants the instant Petition, Petitioner is entitled to an award of reasonable attorney’s fees under this statute. Petitioner’s counsel bills at $550.00 per hour, but does not justify the rate. (Pet., Ryan Decl., ¶2.) For a straightforward and unopposed petition to release mechanic’s lien, the Court finds the billing rate excessive and reduces it to $400.00. Petitioner’s attorney contends he billed three hours to draft the demand letters and Petition, and two hours to prepare for and attend the hearing. (Id. at ¶3.) The time spent to prepare for and attend the hearing, which can be done telephonically, is reduced to one hour. Therefore, Petitioner is awarded attorney’s fees of $1,600.00.  The request for costs is denied without prejudice for failing to indicate the amount sought and the lack of any supporting authority.

 

Conclusion

 

Therefore, Petitioner Donna Popalio’s Petition to Release Property from Mechanic’s Lien is granted. Petitioner’s request for attorney’s fees is granted in the amount of $1,600.00 and the request for costs is denied without prejudice.

 

Petitioner is to file and serve a proposed judgment in accordance with this ruling within 20 days.

 

 

Moving party to give notice.
