DEPARTMENT 735 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV25100    Hearing Date: July 6, 2026    Dept: 735

7980 Sunset Associates v. Ciaraulo

Defendant’s Motion for Reconsideration

 

Because the court entered judgment on February 9, 2026, and defendant has filed a notice of appeal from the judgment, the court lacks jurisdiction to rule on the motion. (Apri Ins. Co. v. Superior Court (1999) 76 Cal.App.4th 176, 182.) Accordingly, the motion is taken off-calendar.
