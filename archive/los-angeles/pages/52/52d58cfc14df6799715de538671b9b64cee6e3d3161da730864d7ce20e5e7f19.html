Case Number: 21NWLC00803    Hearing Date: June 26, 2026    Dept: Y

21NWLC00803: CAVALRY SPV I, LLC AS ASSIGNEE OF CITIBANK, N.A. vs GERALDINE BONILLA

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed Defendant Geraldina Bonilla’s (“Defendant”) Motion to Set Aside and Vacate Judgment, the Opposition filed by Plaintiff Calvary SPV I, LLC as assignee of Citibank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”) and the Reply filed by Defendant.  The Court has also reviewed all relevant court records. 

Plaintiff filed the Complaint on January 8, 2021.  Defendant made a general appearance when she filed her Answer on February 2, 2021.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 410.050, subd. (a).)   Once a party has made a general appearance, “[j]urisdiction of the court over the parties and the subject matter of an action continues throughout subsequent proceedings in the action.”  (Code Civ. Proc., § 410.050, subd. (b).)  

Accordingly, Defendant’s argument that the judgment is void based on allegedly improper service of the Complaint lacks merit.  By filing an Answer, Defendant made a general appearance and thereby submitted to the Court’s personal jurisdiction, rendering any objection to defects in service of the Summons and Complaint waived.  The Court notes that Defendant’s Answer admitted all of the allegations in Plaintiff’s Complaint and other than claiming hardship, Defendant failed to assert any affirmative defenses.  

Defendant also contends that she was not properly served with Plaintiff's Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings. The record does not support this contention. On November 12, 2021, Plaintiff served the Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings by mail to the address Defendant provided in her Answer.

Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 requires that moving and supporting papers be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. Where service is made by mail within California, the notice period is extended by five calendar days. (Code Civ. Proc., § 1005, subd. (b).)

Plaintiff’s service of the motion by mail to Defendant’s address of record complied with the statutory notice requirements. Accordingly, Plaintiff’s motion was properly noticed, and the Court acted within its jurisdiction in ruling on the motion.

Defendant’s contention that the address on file was not “stable” is unavailing. Defendant never provided the Court or Plaintiff with an updated mailing address.  California Rules of Court, Rule 2.200, requires that “[a]n attorney or self-represented party whose mailing address ... changes while an action is pending must serve on all parties and file written notice of the change.”  Defendant failed to comply with this requirement. In the absence of a properly filed and served notice of change of address, Plaintiff was entitled to serve Defendant at the address of record, and Defendant cannot now challenge the adequacy of service based on her own failure to update her address.

Defendant also relies on Code of Civil Procedure section 473.5 as a basis for vacating the judgment. However, section 473.5 affords Defendant no relief under the circumstances presented here. The judgment was entered following the Court’s December 21, 2021 order granting Plaintiff's Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, not by default or default judgment. Accordingly, section 473.5 provides no basis to vacate either the Court’s ruling on the motion or the judgment entered pursuant to that ruling.

Defendant has failed to establish any factual or legal basis warranting relief from the Judgment. Accordingly, Defendant's Motion to Set Aside and Vacate the Judgment is DENIED.

Case Number: 25NWLC21700    Hearing Date: June 26, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC21700: SOUTHWOOD FINANCIAL LLC AS TRUST MANAGER FOR SOUTHWOOD FINANCIAL TRUST I vs JUAN ELIAS

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Southwood Financial LLC as Trust Manager for Southwood Financial Trust I (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulated Settlement Agreement (“Stipulation”) signed by Defendant on November 18, 2025 and filed with the Court on March 6, 2026.   The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order Dismissing Without Prejudice And Retaining Jurisdiction Pursuant to California Code of Civil Procedure 664.6 entered by the Court on April 21, 2026.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Juan Elias (“Defendant”).

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments in the amount of $958.23.  However, the balance now due, owing and unpaid is the principal sum of $15,258.35.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff Southwood Financial LLC as Trust Manager for Southwood Financial Trust I and against Defendant Juan Elias for the pre-judgment amount of $16,243.58 plus court costs of $583.00, minus credits of $958.23 for a total judgment of $15,868.35.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.

(“Plaintiff”) in support of its Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings, including the meet and confer letter dated January 13, 2026, as well as the Complaint and Answer.  No opposition has been filed by Defendant Hugo Gomez (“Defendant”).

Case Number: 25NWLC66131    Hearing Date: June 26, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC66131: CAPITAL ONE N.A. vs TYESHA JACKSON WISE

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Defendant Dane Tyesha Jackson Wise (“Defendant”).  The Court has also reviewed the opposition filed by Plaintiff Capital One, N.A. (“Plaintiff”). 

As noted by Plaintiff, Defendant’s Motion to Vacate Default Judgment and Motion to Stay (“Motion”) was filed with the Court and served on Plaintiff on or about June 4, 2026. 

Code of Civil Procedure section 1005 requires that “all moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. . . . if the notice is served by mail, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by five calendar days if the place of mailing and place of address are within the State of California.”  Proper notice of the motion has not been given.

The Court continues the hearing on Defendant’s Motion to July 15, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. in Dept. Y so that proper notice of the motion can be given to Plaintiff.  

Clerk to give notice.
