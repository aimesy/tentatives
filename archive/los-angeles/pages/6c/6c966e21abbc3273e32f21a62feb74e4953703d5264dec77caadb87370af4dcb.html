Case Number: 23NWCV01361    Hearing Date: August 14, 2026    Dept: C

 

SHAZIA AKMAL vs BMW OF NORTH AMERICA, LLC, Case No. 23NWCV01361

This lemon law action was filed on May 4, 2023, and is set for trial on September 2, 2026. Defendant BMW of North America moves for terminating sanctions on the grounds that Plaintiff has failed to comply with the court’s February 5, 2026, order to produce Plaintiff’s expert for deposition on February 27, 2026, and to produce Plaintiff’s vehicle for inspection on February 23, 2026. Defendant gave Plaintiff another chance after failing to comply with the court’s orders, but Plaintiff then failed to produce the expert file prior to the expert deposition on July 14, 2026, rendering Defendant unable to conduct the deposition. Defendant requests terminating sanctions, exclusion of Plaintiff’s expert from trial and/or monetary sanctions in the amount of $1,995.00. On February 5, 2026, during a hearing on Defendant’s ex parte hearing to advance its Motion to Compel the Deposition of Plaintiff’s Experts and Motion to Compel Vehicle Inspection, the parties stipulated that the vehicle inspection will occur on February 23, 2026, and the deposition of Plaintiff’s expert will occur on February 27, 2026. The parties further stipulated that trial will be continued to 10:00 a.m. on September 2, 2026. No sanctions were imposed. (2/5/26 Minute Order.) Defendant’s efforts to reschedule the expert deposition and the vehicle inspection have proved unsuccessful. (Sheehan Decl., ¶¶18- 25.) On July 31, 2026, the Court advanced the motion to today’s date and informed that parties that if Plaintiff's expert deposition and vehicle inspection are not completed by August 13, 2026, the Court will consider evidentiary and/or terminating sanctions.  In an opposition filed on August 7, 2026, Plaintiff advised the Court that the vehicle inspection occurred on April 22, 2026 and the deposition of Plaintiff’s expert, Randy Waitman, occurred on August 6, 2026. (Cohen Decl., ¶2, 16.) Because Defendant has obtained the discovery it seeks, Defendant’s motion for terminating or evidentiary sanctions is DENIED.  Defendant’s motion for monetary sanctions is GRANTED.  Sanctions in the amount of $1,995.00 is imposed upon Plaintiff and Plaintiff’s counsel, jointly and severally, payable within 30 days.  The Final Status Conference and Trial remain on calendar for August 19, 2026 and September 2, 2026, respectively.  Moving party to give notice. 

 


Case Number: 23NWCV04142    Hearing Date: August 14, 2026    Dept: C

JESUS ORTEGA vs SEAN COHEN, Case No. 23NWCV04142

The matter is on calendar for a bond hearing pursuant to CCP § 529.  On June 11, 2026, the Court granted Plaintiff Jesus Ortega’s motion for a preliminary injunction and set a bond hearing for today.  The parties were ordered to meet and confer regarding Defendant’s entitlement to bond. If issues remain in dispute after meeting and conferring the parties were ordered to file a JOINT SEPARATE STATEMENT outlining their respective positions no later than August 7, 2026. Plaintiff was ordered to submit a declaration of indigency by August 7, 2026.  The Court has reviewed the submissions by the parties which primarily address the merits of the underlying action.  However, in its June 11, 2026 Order, the Court already found that Plaintiff has established a probability of success for purposes of the preliminary injunction.  The issue before the Court at the bond hearing is whether the Court will order a bond, and if so, the amount of the bond.  Allstate asks the Court to order a bond in the amount of $95,262.33.  It appears this amount consists of the $50,000 note plus 10% interest per year beginning August 12, 2019.  Plaintiff declares he “cannot post a $50,000 undertaking, much less an undertaking approaching $95,262.33, and [he] cannot afford the premium or collateral that a bonding company would require for an undertaking in either amount.” (Ortega, Decl., ¶34.)

 

“On granting an injunction, the court or judge must require an undertaking on the part of the applicant to the effect that the applicant will pay to the party enjoined any damages, not exceeding an amount to be specified, the party may sustain by reason of the injunction, if the court finally decides that the applicant was not entitled to the injunction.” (Code Civ. Proc., § 529.) “The court may, in its discretion, waive a provision for a bond in an action or proceeding and make such orders as may be appropriate as if the bond were given, if the court determines that the principal is unable to give the bond because the principal is indigent and is unable to obtain sufficient suretie whether personal or admitted surety insurers. In exercising its discretion the court shall take into consideration all factors it deems relevant, including but not limited to the character of the action or proceeding, the nature of the beneficiary, whether public or private, and the potential harm to the beneficiary if the provision for the bond is waived.” (Code Civ. Proc., § 995.240.)

 

At this juncture, the Court determines that the damages Allstate may sustain by reason of the injunction is the daily interest from the date of the bond hearing to the date of trial, May 25, 2027.  The daily interest is $27.35. (Allstate Position re Bond, Ex. A, Beneficiary’s Demand.) There are 284 days between the bond hearing and trial, so the interest accrued during this period will be $7,767.40.  Plaintiff has not demonstrated an inability to post bond in this amount.  Accordingly, Plaintiff is ordered to post bond with the Court in the amount of $7,767.40 within 30 days.  Plaintiff to give notice. 

 


Case Number: 25NWCV01572    Hearing Date: August 14, 2026    Dept: C

J. W. vs COUNTY OF LOS ANGELES, Case No. 25NWCV01572

The Law Offices of Jack H. Anthony move to be relieved as Counsel for Plaintiff J.W. on the grounds that counsel has not maintained regular contact with client who has repeatedly missed scheduled appointments.  Counsel is unable to further pursue an action without being able to maintain regular contact with the client and without the client’s involvement.  Good cause having been shown, the motion to be relieved as counsel for Plaintiff is GRANTED.  This order shall take effect upon filing of the proof of service upon the client by counsel.  Counsel to give notice.
