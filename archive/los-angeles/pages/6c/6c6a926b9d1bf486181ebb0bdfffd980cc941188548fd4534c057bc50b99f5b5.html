Tentative Ruling

Yuyuan Guo v. Amazon Web Services, LLC, et al., Case No. 24SMCV01056

Plaintiff’s Motion for Leave to File First Amended Complaint

Hearing Date: September 16, 2026

Motion for Leave to File First Amended Complaint

On December 4, 2025, The Court sustained Amazon Web Services’ (“AWS”) demurrer to Plaintiff’s Second Amended Complaint (“SAC”) without leave to amend as to AWS only. Plaintiff’s SAC named fifty-four defendants: AWS, Binance Holdings, Coinbase global, Payward Inc., and 50 Doe defendants. Though it is not clear if any other Defendants were served, the SAC remains the operative pleading against the other fifty-three defendants.

On July 24, 2026, Guo filed a Motion for Leave to File First Amended Complaint seeking to file a first amended complaint against a John Doe, identifying himself as a plaintiff in pro per. According to the Court’s records, Guo is represented by Ronda R. Dixon. A represented party can only appear before the court through their counsel of record. (Code Civ. Proc. § 128.7(a).) If Guo is no longer represented by counsel, he must file a substitution of attorney form. 

Guo’s Motion for Leave to Amend is DENIED because it was not filed by a represented party’s attorney.
