Case Number: 25NWLC09722    Hearing Date: July 20, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC09722: SYNCHRONY BANK vs JOANNA DIAZ

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and considered the moving papers filed by Plaintiff Synchrony Bank (“Plaintiff”), the supporting declaration, and all relevant court records.  

In its motion, Plaintiff requests to vacate the Default Judgment entered on June 12, 2025 because it has brought to Plaintiff’s attention that Defendant may not have been properly served with the Summons and Complaint.  Plaintiff argues that in the interests of justice and pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473(b), the judgment should be set aside and vacated.  Plaintiff asks that the case be restored to active status.  

Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 473 (b), the Court may set aside any void judgment or order.  Code of Civil Procedure section 128 also grants jurisdiction to the Court to take appropriate action in the interests of justice.  

Based on those statutes, and the declaration submitted by Plaintiff, the Court GRANTS Plaintiff’s Motion to Vacate Default Judgment.   Accordingly, the Default Judgment entered against Defendant on June 12, 2025 is vacated.  The Court, on its own motion, also sets aside and vacates the Writs of Execution issued by the Court on August 6, 2025  and April 24, 2026.

The Court grants Defendant 20 days leave to file a responsive pleading.

The Court sets an Order to Show Cause Re: Status of Case on January 7, 2027.  The Court expects that this date will be converted to the trial date after the responsive pleading is filed by Defendant.

Plaintiff to give notice.

Case Number: 25NWLC61349    Hearing Date: July 20, 2026    Dept: Y

25NWLC61349: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK N.A. vs LOLITA PETTIGREW

Tentative Ruling:

The Court has read and reviewed the moving papers filed by Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (“Plaintiff”), including the supporting declaration.  The Court takes judicial notice of the Stipulation Agreement; Jurisdiction Retained Under Code of Civil Procedure Section 664.6 signed by Defendant on January 25, 2026 and filed with the Court on February 11, 2026.  The Court also takes judicial notice of the Order on Settlement Agreement and Stipulation for Judgment entered by the Court on March 11, 2026.

The Court notes that Plaintiff has advised that Defendant has made payments totaling $880.00, leaving an outstanding amount of $6,467.43 due and owing.

Based on the Stipulation signed by the parties and the moving papers submitted by Plaintiff, Plaintiff’s motion is GRANTED. 

The Order dismissing this action pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6 is set aside.  Judgment is hereby entered in favor of Plaintiff JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and against Defendant Lolita Pettigrew for the remaining sum of $6,467.43 plus court costs of zero, for a total judgment of $6,467.43.

The Proposed Judgment will be signed by the Court.

Plaintiff to give notice.
