ANALYSIS:

 

            Safeco Insurance Company of America filed this subrogation action against Hung David Ngo, who then answered.  Safeco propounded 20 form interrogatories and nine requests for admission on Ngo.

 

A party must respond to interrogatories within 30 days after service of such interrogatories. (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.260, subd. (a).)  If a party fails to do so, it waives the right to object, and the propounding party may move for an order compelling responses to the interrogatories.  (Id., § 2030.290, subd. (a), (b).) 

 

            A party must respond to requests for admissions within 30 days after service of such requests. (Code Civ. Proc., § 2033.250, subd. (a).)  If a party fails to do so, it waives the right to object, and the propounding party may move for an order that the genuineness of any documents and truth of any matters specified in the requests be deemed admitted.  (Id., § 2033.280, subd. (a), (b).)

 

            Safeco served Ngo with the form interrogatories and requests for admission in May 2025.  To date, no responses have been served to either discovery request.  Ngo has not filed an opposition indicating otherwise. 

 

Accordingly, the Court GRANTS the motion to compel responses to the form interrogatories and Ngo is ordered to serve verified responses without objections to the form interrogatories within 30 days of this order.

 

The Court GRANTS the motion to deem the requests for admission admitted and the contents of Safeco’s first set of requests for admission are deemed admitted.

 

Ngo must also pay sanctions for causing Safeco to move to compel responses to the interrogatories and to deem the requests for admission admitted.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 2030.290, subd. (c); id., § 2033.280, subd. (c).)  Safeco requests $735.00—for three hours of its counsel’s work plus filing fees—for each motion for a total of $1,470.00 from Ngo.  The Court finds this request reasonable.  Ngo must pay Safeco $1,470.00 within 30 days of this order. 

 

Safeco is to give notice.

ANALYSIS:

           

            Mercury Insurance Company filed a subrogation action against Sana Hicks.  The parties settled and entered a stipulation that required Hicks to pay $6,854.44 at $100.00 per month.  The Court dismissed this action without prejudice and retained jurisdiction to enforce the stipulation pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6.  Hicks subsequently breached the agreement.  Mercury now seeks judgment against Hicks pursuant to the stipulation.  Hicks has not opposed Mercury’s motion.

 

            Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6, subdivision (a), allows a court to enter judgment pursuant to the terms of a written, signed settlement.  If the parties agree, a court may dismiss the case without prejudice and retain jurisdiction to enforce the settlement until full performance is made.  (Ibid.) 

 

            The stipulation between Mercury and Hicks is valid and enforceable under Code of Civil Procedure section 664.6.  In the event of breach, the stipulation provides for the entry of judgment in the principal sum—$6,854.44—minus any payments Hicks made before breaching.  The stipulation also allows for interest at 10 percent per year from the date of the complaint’s filing, attorney fees pursuant to the Local Rule, and costs.

 

Mercury requests a judgment of $8,614.84 against Hicks, comprising $6,854.44 in principal, $1,635.40 in interest, $225.00 in costs, and $0.00 in attorney fees—minus $100.00 for payments received before Hicks’s breach.  However, Mercury provides no explanation of how the amounts for interest and costs were calculated.  The Court therefore declines to award them.

 

Accordingly, the Court GRANTS IN PART the motion and sets aside and vacates the dismissal entered on February 28, 2024, and enters judgment pursuant to the stipulation in the reduced amount of $6,754.44, reflecting $6,854.44 in principal less $100.00 for payments received.

 

Mercury is to submit a proposed judgment reflecting these terms within 10 days of this order, and to give notice.
