DEPARTMENT 26 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS

If you desire to submit on the Court's tentative ruling, you may do so by e-mailing Department 26 at the Spring Street Courthouse until the morning of the motion hearing.
 
The e-mail address is SSCDEPT26@lacourt.org
 
The heading on your e-mail should contain the case name, number, hearing date, and that you submit. The message should indicate your name, contact information, and the party you represent. Please note that if you submit on the court's tentative ruling and do not appear, another party may appear to argue and the court may change its tentative ruling after the oral argument.
 
If there are no appearances by either side and no submission on the Court's tentative ruling, the court will adopt its tentative ruling as its final ruling. The deadline for a party to withdraw a pending motion, or take it off calendar, is TWO COURT DAYS before the hearing on the motion. The court will enter its tentative ruling on motions that remain on calendar after that deadline.
 
Please note, the above e-mail address is to inform the court of your submission on the tentative ruling. All other inquiries to this e-mail will not receive a response.
 
Parties may schedule remote appearances for hearings on motions via LACourtConnect (https://mylacourt.org/laccwelcome). Remote appearances are not permitted for trial or Applications for Order for Appearance and Examination proceedings absent leave of court.

Case Number: 23STLC04895    Hearing Date: September 16, 2026    Dept: 26

TENTATIVE RULING:

 

Plaintiff Mercury Insurance Company’s Motion to Vacate Dismissal and Enter Judgment is granted. Judgment is to be entered in Plaintiff’s favor and against Defendant Genaro Gutierrez Adame in the amount of $4,378.12 principal, costs $403.87, and attorney fees $1,187.50.

 

Moving party is to file and serve a proposed judgment in accordance with this ruling within 20 days.

 

 

Analysis:

 

On August 4, 2023, Plaintiff Mercury Insurance Company (“Plaintiff”) filed this subrogation action against Defendant Genaro Gutierrez Adame (“Defendant”). Defendant filed an Answer to the Complaint on February 22, 2024. On September 8, 2025, Plaintiff filed a Stipulation and Order for Dismissal. The Court entered the stipulation and dismissed the action on the same date. (Stip and Order, filed 09/08/25.)

 

On June 12, 2026, Plaintiff filed and served the instant Motion to Vacate Dismissal and Enter Judgment.

 

Discussion

 

The instant Motion is brought under Code of Civil Procedure, section 664.6, which states in relevant part:

 

If parties to pending litigation stipulate, in a writing signed by the parties outside of the presence of the court or orally before the court, for settlement of the case, or part thereof, the court, upon motion, may enter judgment pursuant to the terms of the settlement. If the parties to the settlement agreement or their counsel stipulate in writing or orally before the court, the court may dismiss the case as to the settling parties without prejudice and retain jurisdiction over the parties to enforce the settlement until performance in full of the terms of the settlement.

 

(Code Civ. Proc., § 664.6, subd. (a).) “Parties” include “an attorney who represents the party” and an insurer’s agent. (Code Civ. Proc., § 664.6, subd. (b).) The settlement must include the signatures of the parties seeking to enforce the agreement, and against whom enforcement is sought. (J.B.B. Investment Partners, Ltd. v. Fair (2014) 232 Cal.App.4th 974, 985.) The settlement agreement complies with the statutory requirements set forth above because it was signed by both parties. (Motion, Espinoza Decl., Exh. A, p. 4.)

 

The settlement obligates Defendant to pay Plaintiff $8,079.76, starting with an initial payment of $1700.82 from Defendant’s insurer, followed by monthly payments by Defendant on the remaining $6378.94 starting on August 15, 2025. (Id. at Exh. 1, p. 4, ¶1.) The settlement agreement also provided that in the event of Defendant’s default, Plaintiff may seek judgment in the amount of $6,378.94, plus costs of $244.87 for filing and $99.00 for service, and costs and fees to execute the judgment, less credit for any payments made. (Ibid.) The insurance company paid $1700.82 toward the settlement and Defendant paid $300, after which Defendant defaulted. (Id. at ¶4.) Therefore, Plaintiff is entitled to judgment in the amount of $6,078.94 principal  ($6,378.94 - $300), underlying costs of $403.87 (filing fee of $244.87, service cost of $99.00, motion cost of $60.00), plus attorney fees of $1,187.50, for a total of $7,670.31. (Id. at ¶5.)

 

 

Conclusion

 

Plaintiff Mercury Insurance Company’s Motion to Vacate Dismissal and Enter Judgment is granted. Judgment is to be entered in Plaintiff’s favor and against Defendant Genaro Gutierrez Adame in the amount of $6,078.94 principal, costs of $403.87, and attorney fees of $1,187.50.

 

Moving party is to file and serve a proposed judgment in accordance with this ruling within 20 days.

 

 

Moving party to give notice.


Case Number: 26STCP01826    Hearing Date: September 16, 2026    Dept: 26


TENTATIVE RULING:

 

Petitioner Elljah Tajau Carrol is ordered to appear at the hearing and explain the relief sought.

 

If Petitioner does not appear, the Court will strike the Petition on its own motion pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure, § 436(b).

                 

Analysis:

 

On May 13, 2026, Petitioner Elljah Tajau Carrol (“Plaintiff”), in propria persona, filed the instant Petition, which seeks to start a new case. (Pet., p. 1.) The Petition states in its entirety, “Hi. I would like to start a new case. I come from Russia.  Varrent Nuber is asking to raise GR back to 900.”  (Ibid.)  The Petition attaches flyers related to a prescription and to attorney services.  There is no declaration to authenticate the attachments or to explain them.  It does not appear from this information that Petitioner is seeking relief that may be granted by a court.  The Petition identifies “Pina 35592” as the Respondent.  It is not clear to the Court whether that identifies a person or entity.  Nothing in the record establishes service of the Petition on Respondent.  

 

Accordingly, the Court orders Petitioner to appear and explain the relief sought. 

 

If Petitioner does not appear, the Court will strike the Petition on its own motion pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure, § 436(b).

 

Judicial Assistant to give notice.
