motion TO COMPEL FURTHER RESPONSES TO REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION OF DOCUMENTS, SET ONE

 

 

MOVING PARTY:                 Defendant and Cross-Defendant Ilan Kenig 

 

RESPONDING PARTY:       Cross-Complainant Moises Gilinski

Motion to Compel Further Responses to Requests for Production of Documents, Set One

The court considered the moving and opposition papers filed in connection with this motion.

DISCUSSION

Defendant and cross-defendant Ilan Kenig (“Kenig”) filed and served this motion to compel further responses to Requests for Production of Documents, Set One, on cross-complainant Moises Gilinski (“Gilinski”) by electronic service on August 17, 2026.  (Motion, p. 19, Proof of Service.) 

The court finds that Kenig has not given proper notice of this motion.  “[A]ll moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing.”  (Code Civ. Proc., § 1005, subd. (b).)  The notice period is extended by two court days for electronic service.  (Code Civ. Proc., §§ 1010.6, subd. (a)(3)(B) [“Any period of notice, or any right or duty to do any act or make any response within any period or on a date certain after the service of the document, which time period or date is prescribed by statute or rule of court, shall be extended after service by electronic means by two court days . . .”].)  The proof of service attached to this motion states that Kenig served it on Gilinski by electronic service on August 17, 2026.  (Motion, p. 19, Proof of Service.)  Sixteen court days before the September 9, 2026 hearing was August 17, 2026, which places the deadline for electronic service on August 13, 2026.

The court therefore denies Kenig’s motion because he did not give the required statutory notice of the motion and hearing.

ORDER

            The court denies defendant and cross-defendant Ilan Kenig’s motion to compel further responses from cross-complainant Moises Gilinski to Requests for Production of Documents, Set One.

            The court orders cross-complainant Moises Gilinski to give notice of this ruling.

IT IS SO ORDERED.

Motion to Compel Production in Accordance with Statement of Compliance

 

MOVING PARTY:            Plaintiff Diane Franco

 

RESPONDING PARTY:   Defendant FCA US, LLC

Motion to Compel Production in Accordance with Statement of Compliance

The court considered the moving and opposition papers filed in connection with this motion.

DISCUSSION

Plaintiff Diane Franco (“Plaintiff”) moves the court for an order compelling defendant FCA US, LLC (“Defendant”) to produce documents in accordance with its statement of compliance in its response to Plaintiff’s Requests for Production of Documents, Set One, and for sanctions.

The court denies the motion as moot because Defendant has demonstrated that it produced documents responsive to Plaintiff’s requests for production on January 28, 2026. (Cicierski Decl. ¶ 4; Ex. B; Code Civ. Proc., § 2031.320.)

The court grants Plaintiff’s request for sanctions. (Code Civ. Proc., § 2031.320, subd. (b).) “The court may award sanctions under the Discovery Act in favor of a party who files a motion to compel discovery, even though . . . the requested discovery was provided to the moving party after the motion was filed.”  (Cal. Rules of Ct., rule 3.1348(a).) The court finds that $962.50 (2.5 hours x $385 per hour = $962.50 attorney’s fees) is a reasonable amount of sanctions to impose on Defendant. (Rucker Decl. ¶ 11.)

ORDER

The court denies plaintiff Diane Franco’s motion to compel production in accordance with statement of compliance.

The court orders defendant FCA US, LLC to pay sanctions in the amount of $962.50 to plaintiff Diane Franco within 30 days of the date of service of this order.   

The court orders plaintiff Diane Franco to give notice of this ruling. 

IT IS SO ORDERED.

RESPONDING PARTY:       Plaintiff Shyan Pawl

(1)   Defendant Teni Amadian’s Demurrer and Motion to Strike Portions of Complaint

(2)   Defendant Alexander Mardirossian’s Demurrer and Motion to Strike Portions of Complaint

(3)   Defendant Artur Kamalimasihi’s Demurrer and Motion to Strike Complaint

 

The court considered the moving papers and the first amended complaint filed in connection with this motion.

DISCUSSION

On July 31, 2026, plaintiff Shyan Pawl (“Plaintiff”) filed his first amended complaint.  Because Plaintiff filed the amended complaint before the date oppositions to the demurrers were due, August 26, 2026, the demurrers and motions to strike the original complaint are moot.  (Code Civ. Proc., § 472 [“A party may amend its pleading once without leave of the court . . . after a demurrer or motion to strike is filed but before the demurrer or motion to strike is heard if the amended pleading is filed and served no later than the date for filing an opposition to the demurrer or motion to strike”].) 

ORDER

            The court therefore orders defendants Teni Amadian, Alexander Mardirossian, and Artur Kamalimasihi’s demurrers and motions to strike the complaint off calendar as moot.

            The court orders plaintiff Shyan Pawl to give notice of this ruling.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
