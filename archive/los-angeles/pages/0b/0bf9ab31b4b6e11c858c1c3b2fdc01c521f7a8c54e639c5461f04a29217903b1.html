            This is a commercial unlawful detainer action.

 

Plaintiff Commerce Realty Developers, a California general partnership moves for summary judgment or, in the alternative, summary adjudication.

 

TENTATIVE RULING

 

            Plaintiff Commerce Realty Developers, a California general partnership’s motion for summary judgment is GRANTED.

 

ANALYSIS

 

Motion For Summary Judgment

 

Discussion

 

Plaintiff Commerce Realty Developers, a California general partnership moves for summary judgment or, in the alternative, summary adjudication.

 

(s) Subdivisions (a) and (b) do not apply to actions brought pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 1159[1]) of Title 3 of Part 3.

 

     (Civ. Proc. Code § 437c(s).)

 

            Thus, a separate statement is not required.

 

            A plaintiff moving for summary judgment need not disprove affirmative defenses, but only need produce evidence establishing the elements of its claims. (Oldcastle Precast, Inc. v. Lumbermens Mutual Casualty Co. (2009) 170 Cal.App.4th 554, 564-65.)

 

            Here, Plaintiff’s Complaint for unlawful detainer alleges that the term under the Lease commenced on August 18, 2018, a Three Day Notice To Pay the sum of $131,714.26, the amount of Rent outstanding under the Lease through July 31, 2026 for the past year, or Quit was served on July 6, 2026 and more than three days have elapsed since the service of the Notice, and there was no lawful tender of the amounts set forth therein. (Complaint, ¶¶ 6, 13, 14.)

 

The basic elements of unlawful detainer based on default in payment of rent contained in Civ. Proc. Code, § 1161(2) are:

 

(1) Tenant is in possession,

 

(2) Tenant is in possession without permission,

 

(3) Tenant is in default of rent,

 

(4) Tenant has been served and has three days' notice in writing and

 

(5) Service of such notice is made upon the tenant

 

 

            The Complaint prays for the following relief:

 

1.  For immediate restitution and possession of the Premises;

 

2.  For an order that the Lease is forfeited and terminated;

 

3.  For an order that Defendants' right to possession of the Premises has been terminated;

 

4.  For past due Rent totaling $131,714.26, and damages according to proof but in no case less than $1,022.83 per day for each day Defendants continue in possession of the Premises from August 1, 2026 up to the time of Judgment;

 

     (Complaint, Page 4, Prayer.)

 

            These allegations are supported by the Declaration of Patricia Rocha at ¶¶ 3 – 10.

 

            As noted above, Plaintiff need not disprove any affirmative defenses. As such, Plaintiff has met its initial burden of demonstrating that it is entitled to judgment as prayed for in the Complaint.

 

As such, the burden shifts to Defendant to demonstrate that a triable issue of material fact exists.

 

In failing to file a timely opposition, Defendant has not met its burden of demonstrating that a triable issue of material fact exists. As such, the motion for summary judgment is GRANTED.


[1] Civ. Proc. Code, § 1159 (forcible entry); § 1160 (forcible detainer); § 1161 (unlawful detainer).
