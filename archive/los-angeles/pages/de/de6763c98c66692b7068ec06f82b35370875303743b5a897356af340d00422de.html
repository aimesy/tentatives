DEPARTMENT 735 LAW AND MOTION RULINGS


Case Number: 25STCV28537    Hearing Date: August 4, 2026    Dept: 735

Joedan Management, LLC v. John Byeon, et al.

Plaintiff’s Motion to Compel Defendant John Byeon to Respond to Special Interrogatories and for Monetary Sanctions

 

            Plaintiff moves to compel responses from Defendant Byeon to Special Interrogatories, Set One. Plaintiff propounded special interrogatories on January 16, 2026. (Ellis Decl., ¶ 2, Ex. 1.)  Defendant did not provide responses. (Id. at ¶¶ 5, 7.) Therefore, Plaintiff is entitled to an order compelling responses to special interrogatories. Plaintiff requests $1,012.00 in sanctions, consisting of $952.00 in attorney’s fees and a $60.00 filing fee. (Id. at ¶ 8.)

CONCLUSION 

The Court grants Plaintiff’s Motion to Compel Defendant John Byeon’s responses to Special Interrogatories, Set One. The Court awards $1,012 sanctions payable to Plaintiff.

Plaintiff shall give notice.
